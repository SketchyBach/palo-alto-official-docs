 Onboard a GoTo Meeting App to SSPM 

 Connect a GoTo Meeting instance to SSPM to detect posture risks. 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Strata Cloud Manager 

 SaaS Security Posture Management license 

 Or any of the following licenses that include the Data Security license: 

 CASB-X 

 CASB-PA 

 For SSPM to detect posture risks in your GoTo Meeting instance, you must onboard your
 GoTo Meeting instance to SSPM. Through the onboarding process, SSPM connects to a
 GoTo Meeting API and, through the API, scans your GoTo Meeting instance for
 misconfigured settings. If there are misconfigured settings, SSPM suggests a
 remediation action based on best practices. 

 SSPM gets access to your GoTo Meeting instance through OAuth 2.0 authorization.
 During the onboarding process, you are prompted to log in to GoTo Meeting and to
 grant SSPM the access it requires. 

 To onboard your GoTo Meeting instance, you complete the following actions: 

 Identify the Administrator Account for Granting SSPM Access 

 Connect SSPM to Your GoTo Meeting Instance 

 Identify the Administrator Account for Granting SSPM Access 

 During the onboarding process, SSPM will redirect you to log in to GoTo Meeting.
 After you log in, GoTo Meeting will prompt you to grant SSPM the access it needs
 to your GoTo Meeting instance. 

 Identify the administrator account that you will use to log in to GoTo
 Meeting during onboarding. 

 Sign out of all GoTo Meeting accounts. 

 Signing out of all GoTo Meeting accounts helps ensure that you sign in
 under the correct account during the onboarding process. Some browsers
 can automatically sign you in by using saved credentials. To ensure that
 the browser does not automatically sign you in to the wrong account, you
 can turn off any automatic sign-in option or clear your saved
 credentials. Alternatively, you can prevent the browser from using saved
 credentials by opening the Cloud Management Console in an incognito
 window. 

 Connect SSPM to Your GoTo Meeting Instance 

 By adding a GoTo Meeting app in SSPM, you enable SSPM to connect to your GoTo
 Meeting instance. You must consent to specific permissions when adding the GoTo
 Meeting app. 

 Log in to 
 Strata Cloud Manager . 

 Select Configuration SaaS Security Posture Security Applications Add Application and click the GoTo Meeting tile. 

 Under posture security instances, Add Instance or,
 if there is already an instance configured, Add New 
 instance. 

 You are redirected to the GoTo Meeting login page. 

 Log in to GoTo Meeting using the credentials for the administrator account
 that you identified earlier. 

 GoTo Meeting displays a consent form that details the information that
 details the access permissions that SSPM requires. 

 Review the consent form and allow the requested permissions.

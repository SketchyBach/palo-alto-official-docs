 Tenant Management Through the Strata Multitenant Cloud Manager 

 Learn how to add and delete tenants in Strata Multitenant Cloud Manager . 

 The Strata Multitenant Cloud Manager enables you to create and manage a hierarchy of business organizations and
 units, each of which is a tenant . For each tenant, you specify a name that
 helps you and others to easily identify it, such as the company name, a division, or a
 geographic location) along with a business vertical designation, such as retail or
 wholesale). Finally, you will assign licenses to the individual tenants in your
 hierarchy. 

 Use Common Services: Tenant Management to
activate licenses and manage subscriptions.

 Replace an M-200 or M-600 Drive 

 Learn how to replace a system drive and log drive on
an M-200 or M-600 appliance. 

 M-200 and M-600 appliances store the Panorama™ system files
and system logs on a single solid-state drive (SSD) and the logs
collected from Palo Alto Networks® firewalls are stored on hard-disk
drives (HDDs). The HDD log drives are in RAID 1 arrays so that if
a drive fails, you can replace the failed drive without service
interruption.

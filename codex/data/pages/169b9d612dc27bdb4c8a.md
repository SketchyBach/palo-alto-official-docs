 Network Segmentation for a Reduced Attack Surface 

 Learn how to segment your network to keep your data safe. 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 NGFW (Cloud Managed) 

 NGFW (PAN-OS or Panorama Managed) 

 AIOps for NGFW Premium (for Strata Cloud Manager ) 

 The following diagram shows a very basic example of Network Segmentation Using Zones . The more
 granular you make your zones (and the corresponding security policy rules that
 allows traffic between zones), the more you reduce the attack surface on your
 network. This is because traffic can flow freely within a zone (intra-zone traffic),
 but traffic cannot flow between zones (inter-zone traffic) until you define a
 Security policy rule that allows it. Additionally, an interface cannot process
 traffic until you have assigned it to a zone. Therefore, by segmenting your network
 into granular zones you have more control over access to sensitive applications or
 data and you can prevent malicious traffic from establishing a communication channel
 within your network, thereby reducing the likelihood of a successful attack on your
 network.

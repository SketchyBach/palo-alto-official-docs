 Log forwarding 

 Stay informed and updated about events in your system by forwarding alerts and reports to an external service, such as a syslog receiver, a Slack channel, or an email account. 

 Logs provide information about events that occur in the system. These logs are a valuable tool in troubleshooting issues that might arise in your Cortex XDR tenant. 

 To stay informed about important alerts and events, you can configure your notifications and specify the type of logs you want to forward. You can choose to receive these notifications through a syslog receiver, a Slack channel, or an email account.

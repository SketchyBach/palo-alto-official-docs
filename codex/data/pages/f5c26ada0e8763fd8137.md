 Remote Browser Isolation (RBI) protects your managed users through isolation, by ensuring that
 websites load in a protected air-gapped environment and that no malicious code executes
 on the browser on their managed devices. 

 Natively integrated with Prisma Access , RBI provides your users
 with a near-native browsing experience that is security policy driven, by allowing you
 to easily apply isolation profiles to existing security policies. You can also benefit
 from simple onboarding and convenient monitoring and visibility into RBI . 

 Review the release information to stay up to date with the latest features and issues
 with RBI .

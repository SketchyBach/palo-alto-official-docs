 View Device and App Status and Information 

 View Device Information 

 Learn how to view Cortex XDR agent app for iOS device endpoint information. 

 To view the endpoint information and the Cortex XDR agent app information on the iOS device: 

 On the Cortex XDR home screen, tap the menu icon, and then tap Device Information . 

 The app displays device information, the endpoint name, and network information. 

 Tap X to close the screen. 

 On the Cortex XDR home screen, tap the menu icon, and then tap About App . 

 The app displays the Cortex XDR agent app information. 

 Tap X to close the screen.

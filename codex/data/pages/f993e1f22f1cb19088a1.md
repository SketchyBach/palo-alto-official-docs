 New Service Region for Advanced Threat Prevention 

 April 30, 2025 

 Palo Alto Networks now provides access to a new service region for the Advanced
 Threat Prevention service, located in Tel Aviv, Israel (FQDN: il.hawkeye.services-edge.paloaltonetworks.com ). The FQDN
 is used by the Advanced Threat Prevention service to facilitate inline cloud
 analysis service requests, which provides access to real-time, ML-based detection
 engines, to protect against evasive zero-day threats, command-and-control (C2)
 traffic, and injection attacks. 

 Typically, the default global service domain ( hawkeye.services-edge.paloaltonetworks.com) automatically connects you to the nearest service provider.
 However, if you want to override the automatically selected server due to your
 organization's data residency and performance requirements, you can manually specify the cloud content
 FQDN .

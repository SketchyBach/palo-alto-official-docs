 Remove a WildFire Appliance from Panorama Management 

 You can remove WildFire standalone appliances
from Panorama management. When you remove a standalone WildFire appliance
from Panorama management, you no longer enjoy the benefits of centralized
management and must manage the appliance using its local CLI and scripts. 

 Select Panorama Managed WildFire Appliances . 

 Select the WildFire appliance or appliances you want to
remove from Panorama management by selecting the checkbox next to
each appliance or by clicking in an appliance’s row. 

 Remove the selected WildFire appliances
from Panorama management.

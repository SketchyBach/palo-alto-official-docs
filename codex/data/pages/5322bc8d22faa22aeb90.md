 PA-5400 Series Firewall Electrical Specifications 

 Learn about the electrical specifications of the PA-5400
series firewall and its components. 

 The following table describes the PA-5400 Series firewall
electrical specifications. To learn about the module and component
electrical specifications of the PA-5450, see the PA-5450 Firewall Component Electrical Specifications . To learn
about the power cords compatible with the PA-5400 Series firewalls,
see PA-5400 Series Firewall Power Cord Types . 

 Specification Value 

 Power Supplies 
 PA-5410, PA-5420, PA-5430, PA-5440 , and PA-5445 

 Two load sharing 1,200W AC or DC power supplies. If one power supply fails, the second power
 supply provides full redundancy. 

 PAN-PWR-1200W-AC 

 PAN-PWR-1200W-DC 

 PA-5450 

 Up to four 2,200W AC or DC power supplies; two power supplies
are required at minimum. 

 PAN-PWR-2200W-AC 

 PAN-PWR-2200W-DC 

 Input voltage 
 PA-5410, PA-5420, PA-5430, PA-5440 , and PA-5445 

 AC
power supplies —100 to 240VAC (50-60Hz) 

 DC power supplies — -48 to -60VDC 

 PA-5450 

 AC power supplies —100 to 120VAC and 200 to 240VAC
(50-60Hz) 

 DC power supplies — -48 to -60VDC 

 Power Consumption 
 PA-5410, PA-5420, PA-5430, PA-5440 , and PA-5445 

 Maximum—760W 

 Average—630W 

 PA-5450 

 Varies
based on your hardware configuration. Learn how to view the PA-5450 firewall
power statistics to gauge power consumption. 

 Maximum Current Consumption 
 PA-5410, PA-5420, PA-5430, PA-5440 , and PA-5445 

 AC power supplies —7A@100VAC, 3A@240VAC 

 DC power supplies — 15A@-48 VDC, 12A@-60VDC 

 PA-5450 

 AC power supplies —14A@100-120VAC; 12.5A@200-240VAC 

 DC power supplies — 52A@-48 to -60VDC 

 Maximum Inrush Current 
 PA-5410, PA-5420, PA-5430, PA-5440 , and PA-5445 

 AC power supplies —25A@230VAC; 50A@120VAC 

 DC power supplies —200A@72VDC 

 PA-5450 

 AC power supplies —17.5A@230VAC; 35A@120VAC 

 DC power supplies —50A@72VDC

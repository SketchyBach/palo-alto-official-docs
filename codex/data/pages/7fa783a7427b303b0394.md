 Firewalls & Appliances 

 Palo Alto Networks®  next-generation firewalls detect known and unknown threats, including in encrypted traffic, using intelligence generated across many thousands of customer deployments. That means they reduce risks and prevent a broad range of attacks. For example, they enable users to access data and applications based on business requirements as well as stop credential theft and an attacker’s ability to use stolen credentials.  

 Palo Alto Networks Prisma SD-WAN Instant-On Network (ION) devices enable the cloud-delivered branch, integrating diverse Wide Area Network (WAN) connection types and leveraging a unique application-defined approach. They simplify WAN management and reduce costs by providing deep application visibility, control, and intelligent traffic steering, ensuring an exceptional user experience for all applications. 

 Panorama™ management appliances provide a centralized management for your firewalls. Panorama monitors, configures, and automates security management across your network.

 Delete a Custom Role 

 Learn how to delete a custom role. 

 After an administrator has created a custom role for
an app, the Account Administrator as well as any other App or Instance
Administrator for the app can delete that role. 

 If you delete
a custom role, the users with that role will lose the permissions that
the role granted. 

 From the Activation Console , navigate to 

 Access Management Manage Roles for the app whose custom role you want to delete. 

 Select the dropdown for the role. 

 Select Delete .

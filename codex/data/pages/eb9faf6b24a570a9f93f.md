 New Features - SaaS Security - May 2026 

 Newly Discovered SaaS Apps Notification in SaaS Security Inline 

 Release Date: May 2026 
 | 
 Last Updated: May 2026 

 Administrators often struggle to maintain immediate visibility into shadow IT and unauthorized software, creating critical blind spots when new applications enter the network undetected. Without proactive alerts, security teams might spend days unaware of potentially risky tools exchanging corporate data. The Newly Discovered SaaS Apps Notification feature in SaaS Security Inline addresses this challenge by providing continuous visibility into unknown applications entering your environment. 

 Whenever the system observes a SaaS application on your network for the very first time, it proactively surfaces this intelligence directly on your dashboard. This capability eliminates the need for manual log hunting by automatically highlighting the exact volume of new applications detected over a rolling seven-day period. By instantly isolating these recent additions into a dedicated, curated view, your security operations team can efficiently assess the risk profile of each newly introduced tool. 

 This focused visibility empowers you to evaluate whether an application aligns with corporate compliance standards, requires immediate access restrictions, or warrants further investigation before broad adoption occurs. Because the system strictly tracks these initial discoveries within a consistent seven-day window, you always maintain an accurate, real-time pulse on your evolving application inventory. By surfacing these insights proactively, this capability ensures you stay ahead of shadow IT sprawl, enforce strict governance over your software footprint, and systematically reduce the attack surface associated with unvetted application deployments across your organization.

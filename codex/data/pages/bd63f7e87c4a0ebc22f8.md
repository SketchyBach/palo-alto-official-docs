 PA-5200 Series Physical Specifications 

 Learn about the PA-5200 Series firewall physical specifications. 

 The following table describes PA-5200
Series firewall physical specifications. 

 The physical specifications are identical for all PA-5200
Series models (PA-5220, PA-5250, PA-5260, and PA-5280 firewalls). 

 Specification Value 

 Rack units and dimensions 

 Rack units—3U 

 Dimensions—5.25”H X
21”D X 17.25”W (13.33cm X 52.07cm X 43.81cm) 

 The depth
dimension includes hardware that protrudes from the back of the
firewall. 

 Weight 

 Firewall weight—46lbs (20.87Kg) 

 Shipping weight—62lbs (28.13Kg)

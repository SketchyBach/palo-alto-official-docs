 Playbook development checklist 

 Plan, build, customize, and test playbooks in Cortex AgentiX with this development checklist. 

 The playbook development checklist follows the logical flow for developing a playbook. 

 playbook-development-flow-4.png 

 Review these steps to implement your playbook successfully. 

 Step

 Step 1. Plan your playbook 

 During planning, define the playbook flow. Consider the process, its steps, and its decisions. These become playbook tasks. 

 See topic 

 Step 2. Build your playbook 

 Use an out-of-the-box playbook, customize one, or create one. Create tasks, inputs, and outputs. Maintain version history. 

 See topic 

 Step 3. Customize your playbook 

 Fine-tune the playbook. Extract indicators, extend context, and add issue fields. 

 See topic

 Debug errors and use playbook metadata to troubleshoot performance.

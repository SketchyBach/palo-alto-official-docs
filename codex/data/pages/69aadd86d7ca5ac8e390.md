 Plan your playbook 

 Plan automated security workflows before building a playbook in Cortex AgentiX. 

 When defining your playbook workflow, consider these questions: 

 Which processes need automation? 

 Which decisions need manual intervention? 

 Does automation require dynamic decisions or complex data interpretation? 

 Which parts of the workflow are time-sensitive? 

 When is the case considered remediated?

 Open a Support Case with Strata Copilot 

 Learn how to effectively use Strata Copilot for troubleshooting, feedback, and support case creation. Discover streamlined processes for getting technical assistance and remediation guidance. 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Prisma Access (Managed by Panorama or Strata Cloud Manager) 

 NGFW , including those funded by Software NGFW
 Credits 

 One of the following licenses that includes access to Strata Cloud Manager : 
 Prisma Access 

 AIOps for NGFW Premium license (use the Strata Cloud Manager app) 

 Strata Cloud Manager Essentials 

 Strata Cloud Manager Pro 

 A valid support entitlement associated with
 your Palo Alto Networks assets 

 An active Customer Support Portal (CSP) account
 linked to your tenant 

 Strata Copilot insights can also depend on the products you’re using with
 Strata Cloud Manager , your licenses ,
 and your role
 permissions . 

 Strata Copilot is your versatile AI assistant designed to enhance your
 experience with Strata Cloud Manager. You can leverage Strata Copilot to
 troubleshoot Strata Cloud Manager issues and gain insights. By mastering Strata
 Copilot's capabilities, you'll optimize your workflow, quickly address challenges,
 and maximize the potential of your Strata Cloud Manager environment. 

 If the issue requires further assistance, you can create a fully
 pre-populated support case using the context already gathered from your session. The
 case includes the product, category, severity, a detailed description, steps already
 taken, and affected assets—all derived from your session and tenant data. You review
 the case summary and submit it in one click. The AI continuously improves its
 resolution accuracy based on validated outcomes, delivering better troubleshooting
 over time. 

 Get AI-Guided Troubleshooting 

 Describe your issue in natural language and receive troubleshooting
 guidance. 

 Select Strata Copilot from the left navigation in Strata Cloud
 Manager. 

 In the chat input field, enter a description of your issue. 

 For example: “My IPSec tunnel to the Dallas branch is down” or
 “Users can’t connect through GlobalProtect since this morning.” 

 Review the troubleshooting steps provided. 

 Follow the recommended resolution steps. If the issue resolves, no further
 action is needed. 

 Open a Support Case with Strata Copilot 

 If troubleshooting doesn’t resolve your issue, open a support case
 directly from Strata Copilot. The case is pre-populated with context from your
 session and tenant data. 

 Initiate case creation using one of the following methods: 

 In a Strata Copilot chat, enter Open a case or
 tell Copilot you want to open a support case. 

 Open a Support Ticket at the bottom of the
 Copilot interface. 

 Describe your issue in plain language when prompted. 

 Strata Copilot uses your input along with your active session
 context—including the page you are on, configurations you are editing,
 and relevant system alerts—to build the case. 

 Review the pre-populated case summary. 

 The following fields are filled automatically based on your
 session and tenant data: 

 Product and component —inferred from your
 navigation and query context. 

 Severity —suggested based on the issue
 characteristics. 

 Description —composed from your input and
 correlated system data. 

 Affected assets —identified from your tenant,
 with support for multiple assets if the issue is systemic. You
 can select the asset based on the issue you are facing. 

 Steps already taken —captured from the
 troubleshooting in your session. 

 (Optional) Attach supporting files, such as screenshots or log exports.

 Submit to create the case. 
 Your case arrives at the support engineering team with full context,
 reducing back-and-forth communication and accelerating time to resolution. 

 If you need to step away during case creation, Strata Copilot
 preserves your session state for one hour, allowing you to resume
 where you left off. 

 Share Feedback About a Response 

 For any Strata Copilot response, you can provide feedback to improve
 future interactions: 

 Click the thumbs up icon to indicate that a response was
 helpful. 

 Click the thumbs down icon to indicate that the response
 didn’t meet your expectations. 

 When you provide a thumbs down, include detailed feedback about what
 you expected versus what Strata Copilot returned. Your feedback directly
 improves accuracy and relevance for future interactions.

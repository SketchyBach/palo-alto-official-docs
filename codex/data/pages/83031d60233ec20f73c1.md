 Reports 

 Overview of Cortex Xpanse reports and how to create, edit, and download them. 

 Reports offer a streamlined way to analyze and present data, helping to identify trends, evaluate response effectiveness, and facilitate communication across teams. Cortex Xpanse provides predefined executive reporting and industry benchmark reporting, as well as the ability to create custom reports. 

 Reports display data in the form of widgets, which enable analyzing data in different formats such as graphs, pie charts, or text. 

 After generating a report, it appears in the Reports + Reports tab, so you can use the report again. 

 If reports do not download after you click Run , add your Expander URL to your browser's pop up blocker exceptions.

 Cloud NGFW for AWS Privacy and Data Protection 

 Learn about Cloud NGFW for AWS privacy and data protection. 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Cloud NGFW for AWS 

 Cloud NGFW subscription 

 Palo Alto Networks Customer Support Account (CSP) 

 AWS Marketplace account 

 User role (either tenant or administrator) 

 At Palo Alto Networks, we are dedicated to supporting a defense-in-depth security model
 to help protect the customer’s data at all stages of its lifecycle, in transit, in
 memory, and at rest, as well as through key management. For more information, please
 visit the Privacy section in the Trust Center . The Trust 360 Program details the corporate-wide
 security, compliance, and privacy controls to protect our customers’ most sensitive
 data. 

 Palo Alto Networks captures, processes, stores, and protects personal data in Cloud NGFW
 for AWS in accordance with the terms in Cloud NGFW for AWS Privacy Datasheet .

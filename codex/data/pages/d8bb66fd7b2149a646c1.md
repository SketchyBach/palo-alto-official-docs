 Monitor Tenants Through the Strata Multitenant Cloud Manager 

 Learn how to monitor tenants in the Strata Multitenant Cloud Manager . 

 The Strata Multitenant Cloud Manager enables you to monitor your tenants and licenses directly from the
 platform. When you go to the platform, it displays a Summary screen with a roll-up of
 information across all of your tenants. Depending on your license, you can view details
 about your Prisma SASE, Prisma Access , or Prisma SD-WAN 
 tenants.

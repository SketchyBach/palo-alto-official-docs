 Unsubscribe from Advanced DNS Security 

 Remove Advanced DNS Security rules from your DNS Firewall rule groups and cancel your AWS Marketplace subscription to stop charges. 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Amazon Route 53 Resolver DNS Firewall 

 Advanced DNS Security subscription (active) 

 AWS Marketplace subscription management permissions 

 To stop subscription charges, you must remove all Palo Alto Networks rules from your DNS Firewall rule groups in addition to canceling your AWS Marketplace subscription. If you cancel the Marketplace subscription but leave Palo Alto Networks rules in your rule groups, charges may continue until you remove the rules. 

 Unsubscribing from Advanced DNS Security on Route 53 DNS Firewall is a two-step process. You first remove all Palo Alto Networks partner managed rules from every rule group that contains them, then cancel the subscription through the AWS Marketplace console. 

 Open the Amazon VPC console. 

 Remove Palo Alto Networks rules from all rule groups 

 For general guidance on navigating the Amazon VPC console and managing DNS Firewall resources, refer to the Amazon Route 53 documentation . 

 Select DNS firewall Rule groups . 

 For each rule group that contains Palo Alto Networks rules, select the partner managed rules and choose Delete . 

 Confirm deletion when prompted. 

 Repeat this step for every rule group in your account that contains Palo Alto Networks partner managed DNS threat protection rules. If you distributed subscriptions to multiple accounts through AWS License Manager, remove Palo Alto Networks rules from rule groups in those accounts as well. 

 Open the AWS Marketplace console. 

 Cancel the AWS Marketplace subscription 

 For general guidance on managing AWS Marketplace subscriptions, refer to the AWS Marketplace documentation . 

 Navigate to Manage subscriptions . 

 Open the Delivery method list and choose SaaS . 

 Under Agreement , open the Actions list and choose Cancel subscription next to the Palo Alto Networks Advanced DNS Security product. 

 In the Cancel subscription dialog box, enter confirm , then choose Yes, cancel subscription .

 New Features - Strata Logging Service - November 2024 

 Log Forwarding from Strata Logging Service China to AWS S3 

 Release Date: November 2024 
 | 
 Last Updated: May 2026 

 Compliance and data sovereignty requirements in China often restrict straightforward cross-border log access. Strata Logging Service provides a tailored solution that enables log forwarding from your Strata Logging Service instances in China to external AWS S3 storage. This feature addresses the unique operational requirements of the region. You can manage log data delivery securely, including the ability to send logs to S3 hosted in Singapore. The solution ensures that during configuration, you receive compliance requirements related to personally identifiable information (PII) that leaves the country. This capability allows you to maintain compliance with relevant data privacy regulations and supports your overall log control and long-term storage strategy.

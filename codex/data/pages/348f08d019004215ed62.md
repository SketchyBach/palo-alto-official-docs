 Steps to unsubscribe CNGFW Tenant from AWS Market place 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Cloud NGFW for AWS 

 Cloud NGFW subscription 

 Palo Alto Networks Customer Support Account (CSP) 

 AWS Marketplace account 

 User role (either tenant or administrator) 

 To unsubscribe a Cloud NGFW tenant from AWS Marketplace: 

 Sign in to the AWS Management Console . 

 Go to the My Subscriptions page. 

 Select the subscription for the product that you want to cancel. 

 Choose Cancel subscription . After canceling your
 subscription, you can't launch your application. 

 If you plan to resubscribe to the service, you must wait at least 2
 hours after initiating the unsubscription on the AWS
 Marketplace. The unsubscription process involves an AWS
 unsubscribe-pending phase that takes approximately one hour, followed by
 a synchronization period for Cloud NGFW processes. Attempting to
 resubscribe immediately after unsubscribing is not supported and may
 result in errors. 

 For more information, see Configuring metering for usage with
 SaaS subscriptions and Cancel your subscription

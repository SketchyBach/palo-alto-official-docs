 Clear Logs 

 Device > Log Settings 

 You can clear logs on the firewall when you Manage Logs on the
Log Settings page. Click the log type you want to clear and click Yes to
confirm the request. 

 To automatically delete logs and reports,
you can configure expiration periods. For details, see Logging
and Reporting Settings .

 View excepted issues 

 View Cortex AgentiX excepted issues and track remaining exception periods. 

 You can view excepted issues by sorting or filtering the Issues page. 

 Navigate to Cases & Issues → Issues . 

 Filter the list of issues by Excepted = Yes to see the complete list of excepted issues. 

 View the Remaining Exception Period field to see how much time is left until the exception expires.

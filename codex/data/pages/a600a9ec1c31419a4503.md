 INC_RN_SPN_LONG_DURATION_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED _THRESHOLD 

 Learn about the INC_RN_SPN_LONG_DURATION_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED_THRESHOLD
 incident. 

 Synopsis 

 SPN capacity exceeded 90% usage for at least 4 hours per day for 3 of the last 5 days
 for SPN <spn-name> <direction ingress | egress> traffic. 

 Incident Code—INC_RN_SPN_LONG_DURATION_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED_THRESHOLD 

 Severity—Warning 

 For details about incident severity, see Incidents Distribution Over
 Time in Incidents and Alerts Overview . 

 Required License 

 Prisma Access 

 Details 

 Impact 

 Total number of branch sites terminating in that SPN. 

 Raise condition 

 <node-name> 85th percentile bandwidth (ingress or egress)
 exceeded 90% of SPN capacity for three consecutive business
 days.

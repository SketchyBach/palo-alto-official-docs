 Wall Mount the ION 1000 

 Wall mount the Prisma SD-WAN ION 1000 using the optional
wall mount kit. 

 Wall mount the ION 1000 using the optional
wall mounting kit that includes ear brackets. 

 Gather the wall mounting brackets, also referred
to as the ear brackets. 

 These brackets enable you to anchor the ION 1000 to the
wall. 

 Place the ION 1000 upside down and locate the four (4)
screw holes on each corner of the device. 

 Place the ear brackets on each side of the device and
align the screw holes on the bracket and the device. 

 Fasten the ear brackets with the screws to secure the
brackets. 

 Place the device on the wall where you plan to mount
it and mark the wall to insert the wall anchors for each screw hole. 

 Use a power drill to apply the four (4) white wall anchors
into the screw holes. 

 Insert the screws partially into the anchors, leaving
a tiny gap to hang the ear bracket. 

 Hang the device onto the wall, making sure to match the
four (4) screw holes on the ear bracket with the four (4) screws
on the wall. 

 Fasten the screws securely to mount the device on the
wall. 

 Verify that the ION 1000 is securely mounted on the wall. 

 Do not remove the rubber pads from
the Prisma SD-WAN ION 1000 to prevent overheating and damage.

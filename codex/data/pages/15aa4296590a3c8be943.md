 Activating Threat Prevention and Advanced Threat Prevention ensures that a Palo Alto Networks 
 NGFW evolves from a basic traffic filter into a sophisticated security
 engine capable of deep-content inspection. This further enables the NGFW 
 to look beyond the envelope of a connection and analyze the actual data payload for
 malicious intent, effectively neutralizing known vulnerabilities , viruses ,
 and spyware through a robust, daily-updated signature database. While the
 standard license focuses on cataloging and blocking established threats, the Advanced
 tier introduces inline AI and machine learning to detect and stop zero-day
 exploits and evasive command-and-control (C2) traffic in real-time. Together, these
 services provide a layered defense-in-depth strategy that prevents attackers from
 exploiting software bugs, delivering malicious files, or exfiltrating data, even if the
 threat has never been seen before. 

 NGFW —The base Threat Prevention and Advanced Threat Prevention subscriptions are activated similarly on NGFW to other cloud service subscriptions. Refer to Activate Subscription Licenses 
 for more information. 

 Prisma Access — Prisma Access typically includes cloud service
 subscription-based features as part of a bundle solution. As a result, these
 subscriptions are automatically enabled when you activate your Prisma Access 
 license. 

 For more information on activating Prisma Access , refer to: Activate Your Prisma Access
 License.

 PAN-GLOBAL-REG-MIB.my 

 PAN-GLOBAL-REG-MIB.my contains global, top-level OID
definitions for various sub-trees of Palo Alto Networks enterprise
MIB modules. This MIB doesn’t contain objects for you to monitor;
it is required only for referencing by other MIBs.

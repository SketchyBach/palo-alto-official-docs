 Panorama Non-Commit Operations Slowness 

 Incident Code 

 INC_NGFW_PANO_NON_COMMIT_OP_SLOW 

 Severity 

 High 

 Category 

 Device 

 Subcategory 

 Management 

 Description 

 This alert triggers when certain jobs in Panorama have shown an increase in the
 time it takes them to execute and complete, such as: load, save, and revert config
 operation 

 Raise Condition 

 This incident is raised when the system detects significant delays in non-commit
 configuration operations. It indicates that at least one key management action,
 such as Save, Load, or Revert, has taken longer than 30 seconds to complete within
 the last hour. This suggests potential performance issues with the management
 plane. 

 Clear Condition 

 This incident will automatically clear after a sustained period of normal
 performance is observed. To ensure system stability, the condition requires that
 no Save, Load, or Revert operations take longer than 30 seconds for 72 consecutive
 hours. Once this threshold of consistent, healthy performance is met, the incident
 will be resolved.

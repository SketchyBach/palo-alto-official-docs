 Business applications 

 Build and manage applications as holistic entities. Gain centralized visibility across the SDLC to monitor assets and remediate threats based on business risk. 

 Business applications (applications) provide a unified business view of code, open-source components, infrastructure, and runtime assets. They connect security findings to ownership and business context across the software development lifecycle (SDLC). 

 Core capabilities 

 Creation methods : You can build applications manually , programmatically via AP I, or dynamically using code or cloud Criteria 

 Risk management : Use applications to define boundaries, assess risk, and prioritize remediation for your critical services 

 Access control : Application Scope-Based Access Control (SBAC) limits user access to assigned applications, supporting least-privilege access across data, issues, and policies 

 View and manage applications 

 To review and maintain your existing applications, refer to View and manage applications (UI) .

 To visually explore asset relationships and gain deeper risk context across your environment, refer to Business application assets .

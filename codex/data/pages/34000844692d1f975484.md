 Learn about the new features in the Activation Console . 

 Feature Description 

 New Activation Console UI 
 September
 2022 

 A new Activation Console UI is now available for apps
 with tenant service group (TSG) 
 support. This new UI enables you to more easily manage Common Services , such as
 license activation, subscription management, tenant management, and
 identity and access management. 

 In the new UI, apps and services are organized by tenant instead of
 by support account. After an app gains TSG support, instances of the
 app will begin to disappear from the support account view. You can
 find these app instances by toggling off View by Support
 Account . After an app instance has migrated, use
 Common Services for
 license activation, subscription management, tenant management, and
 identity and access management. 

 Apps that do not have TSG support will not appear on the new UI. To
 access them, toggle on View by Support
 Account . 

 Bulk Role Assignment 
 June 2020 

 To help you more quickly and easily manage access privileges ,
 the Activation Console now features a Select
 All button and enables you to select up to 100 users
 at a time for role assignment. 

 Role Visibility for Cortex XDR Licenses 
 May
2020 

 To help you better manage your roles for Cortex XDR administrative users, the Activation Console now clearly indicates when an administrator has a
 role that is no longer available in your current XDR license tier. 

 For example, if you assign an administrator the Security Admin role (which requires a Cortex XDR
 Prevent or Cortex XDR Pro per Endpoint license) and change your
 license from Prevent to Pro per TB, the Activation Console 
 displays that administrator at the top of your list of users with a
 tooltip informing you to assign them a different role. 

 Custom Role Creation and Management 
 March
2020 
 For situations where predefined app roles don’t grant the permissions you need,
 you can now create custom
 roles from a list of granular permissions and assign them to
 Cortex XDR users from the Activation Console .

Remote Development Environments

Koi extends its protection beyond local developer machines to support remote development environments, providing consistent visibility, risk assessment, and protection wherever AI development takes place. 

Today, Koi supports Coder and Amazon SageMaker, allowing organizations to secure cloud-hosted development workspaces and AI environments using the same governance model as local endpoints.

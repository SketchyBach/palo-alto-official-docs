 Antenna Specifications 

 Learn about the firewall antenna's frequency range, peak gain, and more. 

 The following table describes the PA-400R Series firewall antenna
 specifications. 

 The PA-455R-5G supports a 4x4 MIMO antenna
 that must be purchased separately. Refer to the antenna's datasheet for specifications
 and other information. 

 Specification Value 

 Antennas 

 PA-410R-5G and PA-450R-5G 

 Four 5G multi-band antennas 

 Frequency range 

 PA-410R-5G and PA-450R-5G 

 615-960MHz / 1500-1600MHz / 1710-2690MHz / 3300-3700MHz 

 Peak gain 

 PA-410R-5G and PA-450R-5G 

 2.3dBi in 800MHz band, 4.4dBi in 1575MHz band, 2.6dBi in 2170MHz
 band, 1.7dBi in 3300MHz band, 3.8dBi in 4400MHz band 

 VSWR 

 PA-410R-5G and PA-450R-5G 

 <3:1 

 Feed impedance 

 PA-410R-5G and PA-450R-5G 

 50Ω 

 Power handling 

 PA-410R-5G and PA-450R-5G 

 30 dBm 

 Interface 

 PA-410R-5G and PA-450R-5G —SMA (F) Connectors 

 PA-455R-5G —N-type Connectors

 WF-500B Appliance 

 Palo Alto Networks now offers a new WildFire
appliance with improved performance and compliance with additional
security certifications to meet your organization’s on-prem malware
analysis requirements. 

 The WF-500B appliance operates
on a newly designed hardware platform running the latest software
release— PAN-OS 10.2.2 , which incorporates
analysis improvements for malicious scripts and MS-Office 2010 files, quality
of service enhancements (logging output formatting improvements,
appliance cluster stability, and general operational efficacy),
as well as a host of bug fixes. The WF-500B appliance has also been
designed to conform to certifications that meet stringent regulatory
requirements. 

 For more information about product certifications,
refer to: Palo Alto Networks Trust Center ;
and more product specifications, refer to the WildFire Data Sheet .

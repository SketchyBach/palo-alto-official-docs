 SaaS Security Report Section for Top AI Applications by Usage 

 Release Date: March 2024 
 | 
 Last Updated: June 2026 

 SaaS Security Reports now provide a PDF snapshot that highlights the SaaS apps in use in your organization and the security risks they pose. Now, when you generate a report , the report includes a new section that lists the top 30 AI app by usage.

 Third-Party Plugin Detection in SSPM for Salesforce 

 Release Date: March 2024 
 | 
 Last Updated: May 2026 

 To help you address the threats posed by third-party plugins, SaaS Security Posture Management gives you visibility into the third-party plugins that are being used in your organization. You can then take action by approving the plugin or by revoking user access to it. SaaS Security Posture Management now provides third-party plugin scans for Salesforce . To perform third-party plugin scans, SaaS Security Posture Management requires additional permissions. To enable third-party plugin scans, make sure that the Salesforce account that SaaS Security Posture Management uses for scans has the following additional permissions:

 GlobalProtect for IoT Devices 

 Configure the GlobalProtect app on IoT devices. 

 GlobalProtect™ is an application that runs on your endpoint
(desktop computer, laptop, or server, or IoT device) to protect
you by using the same security policies that protect the sensitive
resources in your corporate network. For IoT devices, GlobalProtect™
secures traffic to and from the device to any source or destination
anywhere on the internet or within your corporate network. 

 You can install GlobalProtect on IoT devices 
 that are embedded within the following operating systems: 

 IoT on Android 

 IoT on Raspbian 

 IoT on Ubuntu 

 IoT on Windows

for the PAN-OS® 11.0 software release. 

 The following list displays the Open-Source Software (OSS) versions and licenses used in
 our PAN-OS® 11.0 software. It is our goal to capture and provide here the version of
 each component used in the latest maintenance release of this feature release. We
 recommend that, if you haven't already, you upgrade to the latest recommended
 maintenance release to ensure that you have all available fixes, enhancements, and
 vulnerability updates for your products. 

 For some OSS components, we may include and distribute multiple
 OSS versions for deployment on and for use with different Palo Alto Networks products to
 deliver the different features and functionality available on those products. However,
 we do not attempt to capture these differences in detail or per product in these OSS
 listings. To confirm which versions are running on your product when there is more than
 one version listed, Sign in to your Palo Alto Networks account on
 the Customer Support Portal and open a ticket to request the
 information for your specific products.

 Detection Modules 

 Identity Analytics 

 ATT&CK Tactic 

 Credential Access (TA0006) 

 ATT&CK Technique 

 Steal or Forge Kerberos Tickets: AS-REP Roasting (T1558.004) 

 Severity 

 Medium 

 Description 

 A user enumerated all accounts that don't require pre-authentication in the organization and specifically requested tickets for those accounts. This is typically a sign of an AS-REP Roasting attack. 

 Attacker's Goals 

 Crack account credentials by obtaining an easy-to-crack Kerberos ticket. 

 Investigative actions 

 Check who used the host at the time of the alert, to rule out a benign service or tool requesting weak Kerberos encryption.

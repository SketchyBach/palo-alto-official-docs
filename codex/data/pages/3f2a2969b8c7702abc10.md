 Create a Gmail Email Transport Rule 

 Create a Gmail email transport rule to forward traffic to the Enterprise Data Loss Prevention (E-DLP) for inline email inspection. 

 Configure the Email DLP data filtering settings . 

 These settings determine the timeout and email message size parameters for
 emails forwarded to Enterprise DLP and specify the actions Enterprise DLP takes when these parameters are exceeded. 

 Log in to
 the Google Admin portal . 

 In the Dashboard, select Apps Google Workspace Gmail Compliance . 

 In the Content compliance section, Add Another
 Rule . 

 Configure the email transport rule. 

 In Content compliance , enter a
 descriptive name for the transport rule. 

 For Email messages to affect , select
 Outbound . 

 This instructs Gmail to forward the email to Enterprise DLP 
 before it leaves your network when the email recipient is outside
 your organization. 

 Configure email forwarding to Enterprise DLP for emails that have
 not been inspected. 

 In Add experiences that describe the
 content you want to search for in each
 message , select If ALL
 of the following match the message . 

 Add a condition to forward emails that
 haven't been inspected by Enterprise DLP . 

 In Add setting ,
 select Advanced content
 match . 

 For Location , select
 Full Headers . 

 For Match type ,
 select Not contains text . 

 For Content , enter
 x-panw-inspected . 

 Click Save to continue. 

 Add a condition to forward emails to
 Enterprise DLP when the sender is blank or
 empty. 

 In Add setting ,
 select Advanced content
 match . 

 For Location , select
 Envelope Sender . 

 For Match type ,
 select Not matches regex . 

 For Regexp , enter
 ^$ . 

 Click Save to continue. 

 Save . 

 Configure the action Gmail takes for emails that have already been
 inspected by Enterprise DLP , and the encryption settings. 

 In If the above expressions match, do the
 following , enable
 Change Route . 

 Select the Email DLP
 Host you created. 

 For Encryption (onward delivery
 only) , select Require secure
 transport (TLS) . 

 Configure the types of Gmail accounts the transport rule affects. 

 Show Options . 

 After you expand the options menu, the button displays
 Hide Options . 

 In Account types to affect ,
 select Users ,
 Groups , and
 Unrecognized / Catch-all . 

 Save . 

 Verify that the email transport rule was successfully added and that the
 Status is
 Enabled .

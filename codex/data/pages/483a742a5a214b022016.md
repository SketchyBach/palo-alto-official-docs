 PAN-OS OpenConfig Dial-Out Support 

 Dial-Out connections for OpenConfig with streaming telemetry. 

 Dial-out connections automatically send telemetry data to endpoints outside of your
 plugin host. 

 OpenConfig plugin versions 2.0.2 and before Dial-Out connections to Palo Alto
 Networks services are hard-coded and region specific. AIOps for NGFW ingests these
 metrics to gain insights into the state of your PAN-OS devices. 

 For more information about the information stored and sent by Palo Alto Networks, review
 the PAN-OS Privacy Data Sheet . 

 Dial Out Connections are sent at intervals of 5 minutes, 1 hour, and 1 day. Palo Alto
 Networks send subscriptions sent by OpenConfig to the AIOps server, the OpenConfig
 plugin will automatically use streaming telemetry connections. You can also generate
 commands on-demand to get an immediate response from the OpenConfig plugin. 

 The logging, config, and reporting models are used to generate telemetry data to send to
 AIOps. 

 You can view the active subscriptions by navigating to Device OpenConfig in the Subscriptions field. The following image
 shows an example based on the default configuration set by Palo Alto Networks. 

 Each connection may have multiple queries attached to one connection.

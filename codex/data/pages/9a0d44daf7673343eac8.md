 Cortex Agentic Assistant permissions 

 Configure the Cortex Agentic Assistant permissions, which includes AI Prompts and Agent permissions in Cortex XSIAM. 

 The Cortex Agentic Assistant is an AI-powered assistant that provides autonomous-agent capabilities, natural-language querying, insights, and automated-investigation support within the XSIAM tenant. 

 This permission is split into the following: 

 AI Prompts : Reusable instruction templates 

 Cortex Agentic Assistant Agents : Interactive AI assistants and their underlying actions.

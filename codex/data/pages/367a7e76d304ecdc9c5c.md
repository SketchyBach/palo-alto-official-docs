 Network > Interfaces > Fail Open 

 Learn how to configure fail open ports on certain firewalls to maintain a connection
 despite power or operating system failure. 

 Certain firewall models have fail open ports that can be configured to provide a
 pass-through connection in the event of a power or operating system failure. This
 feature is disabled by default and must be enabled by selecting Enable Fail
 Open . 

 The following firewalls support fail open: 

 Firewall Model Fail-Open Ports 

 PA-410R and PA-410R-5G 

 Ports 1 and 2 

 PA-450R and PA-450R-5G 

 Ports 3 and 4 

 545-POE, PA-550, PA-555-POE, and PA-560

 Automation permissions 

 Configure permissions for automation features. 

 This section covers permissions for playbooks, scripts, playground, and the Automated Exclusion Center. 

 Caution 

 Cortex XDR enforces a strict permission dependency chain for automation and case management. You must grant at least View access to Scripts before you can grant access to Playbooks. Consequently, you must grant at least View access to Playbooks before you can enable Cases & Issues.

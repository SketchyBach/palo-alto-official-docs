 Inventory 

 Upload or remove assets 

 Adjust your inventory by adding or removing assets yourself through the Cortex Xpanse UI. 

 In Cortex Xpanse you can upload assets or remove assets from your inventory through the UI. This feature simplifies inventory management by enabling you to to make ad hoc inventory updates yourself as needed. To make large-scale scope changes to your inventory (for example, a merger with a new organization), contact your Customer Success Architect for assistance. 

 Note 

 You must have a role with Asset Uploads permission to upload assets. You must have the Instance Administrator role to remove assets.

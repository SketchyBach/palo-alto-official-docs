 HA Timers 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 CN-Series deployment 

 CN-Series 10.2.x or above Container Images 

 Panorama running PAN-OS 10.2.x or above version 

 Helm 3.6 or above version client for CN-Series deployment with Helm 

 High availability (HA) timers are used to detect a firewall
failure and trigger a failover. To reduce the complexity in configuring
HA timers, you can select from three profiles: Recommended , Aggressive ,
and Advanced . These profiles auto-populate the
optimum HA timer values for the specific firewall platform to enable
a speedier HA deployment. 

 Use the Recommended profile for typical
failover timer settings and the Aggressive profile
for faster failover timer settings. The Advanced profile
allows you to customize the timer values to suit your network requirements.

 Severity 

 Low 

 Description 

 An RDS snapshot was exported to an S3 bucket. The destination S3 bucket was not seen in your organization in the last 30 days. 

 Attacker's Goals 

 Exfiltrate data to an unknown bucket. 

 Investigative actions 

 Check the legitimacy of the referenced destination bucket. 

 Review further logs for the source snapshot ID. 

 Review further actions performed by the identity and role.

 Prisma SD-WAN 

 Prisma SD-WAN is a cloud-delivered service that offers app-defined, autonomous SD-WAN to securely connect your branch offices and data centers while minimizing cost and complexity. As a key component of the Secure Access Service Edge (SASE) framework, Prisma SD-WAN ensures modern enterprises can achieve secure, high-performance connectivity. At its core, the application performance engine powers a software-defined WAN solution, transforming traditional WANs into a simplified, secure, application-driven fabric (AppFabric). It virtualizes diverse underlying transports, creating a unified hybrid WAN. Review the  privacy datasheet  for details on what personal information Prisma SD-WAN collects and what Palo Alto Networks does with it.

 AL_SC_SECONDARY_WAN_TUNNEL_FLAP 

 Learn about the AL_SC_SECONDARY_WAN_TUNNEL_FLAP alert. 

 Synopsis 

 Primary WAN tunnel <tunnel-name> for the service connection is flapping. 

 An alert is raised if the secondary WAN tunnel for the service connection flaps (up
 to down or down to up) 5 times within the past 10 minutes. 

 Alert Code—AL_SC_SECONDARY_WAN_TUNNEL_FLAP

 XDR Collector audit logs 

 Learn more about XDR Collector audit logs. 

 Notice 

 Ingestion of logs and data requires a Cortex XDR Pro per GB license. 

 Cortex XDR logs entries for events related to the XDR Collector monitored activities. Cortex XDR stores the logs for 365 days. To view the XDR Collector audit logs, select Settings → XDR Collector Audit Logs .

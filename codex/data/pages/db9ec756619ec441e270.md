 Strata Copilot Multilingual Support 

 Release Date: April 2026 
 | 
 Last Updated: May 2026 

 Managing security across global teams is challenging when your preferred language isn't supported. Strata™ Copilot now supports multiple languages, enabling you to interact in your preferred language while maintaining the full capabilities of Strata Copilot . Translation covers all user interactions, including free-form queries, streaming updates, curated questions, playbook flows, error messages, and charts, providing a comprehensive localized experience. 

 Strata Copilot supports the following languages: English, Chinese, French, German, Japanese, Korean, and Spanish. Language offerings will be expanded based on customer demand. 

 All existing security guardrails and content validations remain fully functional on translated content, maintaining the same level of protection as English interactions. You might find multilingual support particularly valuable if you operate in global environments with diverse teams or if you prefer working in your native language for improved efficiency and comprehension.

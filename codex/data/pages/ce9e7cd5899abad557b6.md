 Rarely seen sender address in the organization 

 Synopsis 

 Field

 Deduplication Period 

 1 Day 

 Required Data 

 Microsoft 365 Emails 

 Detection Modules 

 Email 

 ATT&CK Tactic 

 Reconnaissance (TA0043), Initial Access (TA0001) 

 ATT&CK Technique 

 Phishing for Information (T1598), Phishing (T1566) 

 Severity 

 Informational 

 Description 

 An email was received from a sender that has not been observed in the organization in the last 30 days. 

 Attacker's Goals 

 Aim to steal sensitive information 

 Trick recipients into downloading harmful payloads 

 Aim to gain unauthorized access to the organization's systems. 

 Investigative actions 

 Check the email address for any unusual spellings, missing letters, or unknown domains. 

 If the message contains attachments or links, scrutinize them for any suspicious indications. 

 Monitor further actions taken, such as file downloads or access to potentially malicious links.

 Log in to the Palo Alto Networks WildFire portal using
your Palo Alto Networks support credentials or your WildFire account.
The portal opens to display the dashboard, which lists summary report
information for all of the firewalls associated with the specific
WildFire subscription or support account. For each device listed,
the portal displays statistics for the number of malware samples
that have been detected, benign samples that have been analyzed,
and the number of pending files that are waiting to be analyzed.
Your WildFire portal account displays data for all samples submitted
by firewalls on your network that are connected to the WildFire
public cloud, as well as data for samples manually submitted to
the portal. Additionally, if you have enabled a WildFire appliance
to forward malware to the WildFire public cloud for signature
generation and distribution, reports for those malware samples can
also be accessed on the portal.

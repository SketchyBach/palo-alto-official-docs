 Approaching Max Capacity - Service Groups 

 Incident Code 

 INC_NGFW_CAPACITY_SERVICE_GROUPS 

 Severity 

 Warning 

 Category 

 Device 

 Subcategory 

 System Resources 

 Product 

 Supported on NGFWs and VM-Series firewalls. 

 Description 

 The number of service group objects has been consistently high and is approaching
 the maximum capacity the firewall can support. 

 Raise Condition 

 The incident is triggered when Service Groups usage exceeds 85% of the capacity of that respective firewall model and stays beyond 85% for a minimum of five days in the past seven days. 

 Clear Condition 

 The incident is cleared when the Service Groups usage remains below 85% of the respective firewall model's capacity for a continuous period of two weeks

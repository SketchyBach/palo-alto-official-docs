of the PA-5400 series firewall and its components.

physical specifications. 

 Specification 

 Value

 inches (57.15 cm) 

 PA-5450 firewall —30 inches (76.2 cm).

 PA-5410, PA-5420, PA-5430, PA-5440 , and PA-5445 firewalls —17.34
 inches (44.04 cm) 

 PA-5450 firewall —17.4 inches (44.2 cm).

 Appliance—35 lbs (15.88 kg)

 Fan tray—1.4 lbs (0.64 kg)

 Power Supply (DC)—2 lbs (.9 kg)

 PA-5450 firewall —5U

 PA-5410, PA-5420, PA-5430, PA-5440 , and PA-5445 firewalls —Two
 AC or DC power supplies. The AC and DC power supplies are
 hot-swappable. 

 PA-5450 firewall —Four AC or DC power supplies. The AC and DC power supplies are
 hot-swappable.

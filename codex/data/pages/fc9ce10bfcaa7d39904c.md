 INC_ZTNA_CONNECTOR_TUNNEL_DOWN 

 Learn about the INC_ZTNA_CONNECTOR_TUNNEL_DOWN incident. 

 Synopsis 

 This incident is raised when the tunnel <tunnel-name> is down for connector
 <connector-name> in connector-group <group-name>. The incident is cleared when
 the tunnel between the connector and ZTT is reestablished. 

 Incident Code—INC_ZTNA_CONNECTOR_TUNNEL_DOWN 

 Severity—Critical 

 For details about incident severity, see Incidents Distribution Over
 Time in Incidents and Alerts Overview .

Learn how Malware Protection blocks malicious non-binary software

Block new installations and automatically remediate any non-binary software identified as malicious through Wings, Koi’s agentic AI-powered risk engine scan. Relevant findings: 

Malicious activity detected

Spyware activity

Why It Matters

Marketplaces are increasingly targeted by threat actors for malware distribution.

Malicious non-binary software can compromise your environment and expose sensitive data.

Proactively blocking these packages ensures your workforce remains secure.

How It Works

Proactively blocks packages confirmed as malicious.

Continuously updated through SCMD to ensure coverage of the latest threats.

Operates seamlessly without disrupting your workforce workflows.

Benefits

Real-Time Protection: Stops malicious packages before they can be installed.

Automated Updates: Leverages Koi's Wings agentic AI-powered risk engine intelligence.

Zero Disruption: Integrates seamlessly with your environment.

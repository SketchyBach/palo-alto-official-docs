 ATT&CK Tactic 

 Execution (TA0002), Stealth (TA0005) 

 ATT&CK Technique 

 Command and Scripting Interpreter: AppleScript (T1059.002), Obfuscated Files or Information: Command Obfuscation (T1027.010) 

 Severity 

 Low 

 Description 

 The AppleScript interpreter process was executed with an obfuscation technique in the command line. 

 Attacker's Goals 

 Evade defenses and impede forensics by encrypting, encoding, or obfuscating payloads at rest or in transit. 

 Investigative actions 

 Analyze the command line and determine whether it performs any malicious or suspicious actions. 

 Check the events generated by the process or its children for potential malicious behavior. 

 Verify the legitimacy of the persistence mechanism being created or modified.

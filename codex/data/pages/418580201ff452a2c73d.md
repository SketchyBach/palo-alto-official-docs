 Configure Palo Alto Networks as a Service Provider Through Common Services 

 Learn how to configure Palo Alto Networks as a service
provider through the Common Services . 

 After you add an identity federation ,
you can configure Palo Alto Networks as a service provider by downloading
the service provider (SP) metadata from Common Services .
The SP metadata helps you configure your identity provider integration
with Palo Alto Networks as an SP, so that you don’t have to provide
the details manually. 

 There are various ways to access Common Services Identity & Access ,
but only one is described here. Download the SP metadata from Common Services Identity &
Access Identity Federations . 

 Use one of the various ways to access 
 Common Services Identity & Access . 

 Select Identity & Access/Access
Management Identity Federations . 

 Scroll to your identity federation of choice and Download
SP Metadata . 

 Go to your domain provider’s console and upload the file. 

 Also in your domain provider’s console, if available,
disable the app visibility. Palo Alto Networks does not support displaying
the application icon to users. The console details look similar
to the following, but all providers are slightly different.

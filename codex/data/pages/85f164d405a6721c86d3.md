 Activate/Retrieve a Firewall Management License when the
Panorama Virtual Appliance is not Internet-connected 

 How to activate the firewall management license when
the Panorama virtual appliance is not Internet connected. 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 NGFW (Managed by Panorama) 

 Panorama virtual appliance 

 Customer Support Portal 

 Registered Panorama management server 

 Before activating and retrieving a firewall
management license on the Panorama virtual appliance, you must Register
Panorama . In order to manage devices on Panorama, you will
need to activate a device management license. The device management
license you activate will determine the number of devices Panorama
can manage. Log Collectors and WildFire appliances are not treated
as managed devices and will not count toward the number of devices
allotted by the device management license. If you are running an evaluation
license and want to apply a license that you purchased, you must
still register and activate/retrieve the purchased license. 

 After
upgrading to PAN-OS 8.1, you will be prompted to retrieve a valid
Panorama management license when you first log in to the Panorama
web interface when Panorama has finished rebooting. To activate
or retrieve the valid management license if the Panorama virtual
appliance is offline or unable to reach the Palo Alto Networks update
server, you must get the relevant appliance information for the
Panorama virtual appliance and upload it to the Customer Support
web site. 

 Log
in to the Panorama Web Interface . 

 ( Initial Deployment only ) Enter the Panorama Serial
Number . 

 Select Panorama Setup Management and
edit the General Settings. 

 Enter the Panorama Serial Number (included
in the order fulfillment email) and click OK . 

 Select Commit Commit to Panorama and Commit your
changes. 

 Upload the Panorama virtual appliance information to
the Customer Support website. 

 On the Retrieve Management License dialogue,
click the here link to gather the UUID, CPUID,
Panorama Version and Virtual Platform information. Click Download
Link to download a XML file of the required Panorama
information that can be uploaded to the Customer Support Portal. 

 On initial deployment, may need to log out and back in
to the web interface to see the dialogue. 

 Log in to the Palo Alto Networks Customer Support web site . 

 Click Get Support in the upper
right-hand corner. 

 Select Products Devices , find your Panorama virtual appliance and in the Action
 column, click the edit icon ( 

 ). 

 Select Is the Panorama Offline? and
enter the Panorama information gathered in Step 2, or click Select files... to
upload the downloaded XML file. 

 Agree and Submit the EULA. 

 Install the device management license. 

 In the Actions column, download the device
management license. 

 In the Panorama web interface, click Panorama Licenses and Manually
upload license key . 

 Click Choose file , locate the
downloaded device management license key and click OK . 

 Confirm that the device management license was successfully
uploaded by verify that the Device Management License displays with
the license information.

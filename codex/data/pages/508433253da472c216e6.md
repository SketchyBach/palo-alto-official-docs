 AIOps for NGFW 

 Learn about AIOps for NGFW, a new product offering from
Palo Alto Networks. 

 Available for all PAN-OS 10.0 and later
devices, AIOps for NGFW is a new application on the hub that analyzes
PAN-OS device telemetry and best practice assessment results to
give you a comprehensive understanding of your deployment health
and security posture. 

 Get started with AIOps for NGFW now.

 War Room Indexing 

 Configure War Room indexing in Cortex XSOAR 6.14. 

 By default, Cortex XSOAR does not index notes, chats, and pinned as evidence entries from incident War Rooms and it is not possible to find these entries in the Search Incidents bar. You have the option to index these War Room entries and make them available for search.

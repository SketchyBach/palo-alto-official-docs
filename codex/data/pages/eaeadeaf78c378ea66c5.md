 PA-500 Series Firewall Electrical Specifications 

 Learn about the electrical specifications of the PA-500 Series Firewall. 

 The following table describes PA-500 Series firewall electrical
 specifications. 

 Specification Value 

 Power adapter 

 The PA-500 Series firewalls operate on DC power that is provided by
 the external power adapter (provided). 

 The PA-501 and PA-505 firewalls operate on a single power
 adapter. The 
 other PA-500 Series firewalls can operate on one power
 adapter or you can install a second power adapter for load sharing
 and power redundancy. 

 Input voltage 

 PA-501, PA-505, PA-510, PA-520,
 PA-540, PA-550, and PA-560 

 Power adapter (AC side)—100-240V AC 50-60Hz 

 The power adapter converts the AC power to 12VDC to provide
 power to the firewall. 

 PA-545-POE and PA-555-POE 

 Power adapter (AC side)—100-240V AC 50-60Hz 

 The power adapter converts the AC power to 54VDC to provide
 power to the firewall. 

 Maximum power consumption 

 PA-501
 and PA-505 —23W 

 PA-510 —34.3W 

 PA-520 and PA-540 —30W 

 PA-545-POE —336W 

 PA-550 —57W 

 PA-555-POE —503W 

 PA-560 —106W 

 Maximum current consumption 

 PA-510 —2.5A@12VDC 

 PA-520 —4A@12VDC 

 PA-540 —4A@12VDC 

 PA-545-POE —6A@54VDC 

 PA-550 —5A@12VDC 

 PA-555-POE —9A@54VDC 

 PA-560 —8A@12VDC 

 Power over Ethernet (PoE) 

 PA-545-POE 

 Supported on ports 9, 10, 11, and 12 

 Maximum power reserved per port—90W 

 Total PoE budget allowed (across all ports) —181W 

 PA-555-POE 

 Supported on ports 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, and 12 

 Maximum power reserved per port—90W 

 Total PoE budget allowed (across all ports) —332W

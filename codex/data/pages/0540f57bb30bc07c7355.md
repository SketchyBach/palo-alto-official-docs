 About managed threat hunting 

 Understand how Managed Threat Hunting can help your organization. 

 Cortex XDR provides the Managed Threat Hunting service as an add-on security service. To use Managed Threat Hunting, you must purchase a Managed Threat Hunting license and have a license with a minimum of 500 endpoints. 

 Managed Threat Hunting augments your security by providing 24/7, year-round monitoring by Palo Alto Networks threat researchers and Unit 42 experts. The Managed Threat Hunting teams proactively safeguard your organization and provide threat reports for critical security incidents and impact reports for emerging threats that provide an analysis of exposure in your organization. In addition, the Managed Threat Hunting team can identify incidents and provide indepth review of related threat reports.

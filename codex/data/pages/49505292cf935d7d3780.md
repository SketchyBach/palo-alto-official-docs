 Power over Ethernet (PoE) 

 PA-54R-POE, PA-54R-POE-D-5G, and PA-56R-POE-5G 

 Supported on ports 9, 10, 11, and 12 

 Maximum power reserved per port—90W 

 Total PoE budget allowed (across all ports) —150W 

 150W is the maximum PoE budget
 when the ambient temperature is 60°C or less. 
 If the ambient
 temperature exceeds 60°C, then PoE shuts down in order to avoid
 safety risks.

 INC_SC_SECONDARY_WAN_BGP_FLAP 

 Learn about the INC_SC_SECONDARY_WAN_BGP_FLAP incident. 

 Synopsis 

 The secondary WAN BGP for the service connection is flapping. 

 Incident Code—INC_SC_SECONDARY_WAN_BGP_FLAP 

 Severity—Warning 

 For details about incident severity, see Incidents Distribution Over
 Time in Incidents and Alerts Overview . 

 Required License 

 Prisma Access 

 Details 

 Raise condition 

 The tunnel's BGP peer flapped at least 5 times in 10 minutes. 

 Clear condition 

 The tunnel's BGP is up for at least 8 minutes.

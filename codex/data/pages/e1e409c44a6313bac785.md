 iOS 16 and later 

 You must add the Prisma Agent app to Microsoft
 Intune to deploy the Prisma Agent to any users or endpoints. 

 Before you begin, ensure that the endpoints are enrolled with
 Microsoft Intune . 

 Navigate to the Microsoft Intune Admin Center at intune.microsoft.com and log in to Intune. 

 Select Apps iOS/iPadOS and then click Create . 

 Select the App Type as iOS Store
 App and then click Select . 

 Search and select the Prisma Agent app in the App
 store . 

 In the App Information tab, modify or enter additional information as required,
 and click Next . 

 In the Assignments tab, add the users you want to deploy the Prisma Agent to and click Next . 

 Review the app summary and click Create . 

 To use certificates for authentication, refer to the Certificates section in the Microsoft
 Intune documentation.

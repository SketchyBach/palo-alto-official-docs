 New Features - Autonomous DEM - May 2026 

 Manage Browser-based Real User Monitoring 

 Release Date: May 2026 
 | 
 Last Updated: June 2026 

 To maintain tight control over collected data while providing visibility into newly discovered application domains, Autonomous DEM (ADEM) introduces granular controls for Browser-based Real User Monitoring (RUM) . 

 You can now manage domain discovery independently from data collection. When RUM is active, ADEM automatically discovers new domains but provides controls allowing you to explicitly enable or disable data collection. This ensures you only monitor the specific application domains, users, and user groups that matter most to your organization. 

 Furthermore, the system simplifies large-scale management through wildcard governance and automated clean-up, where inactive discovered domains are automatically purged after 30 days, providing a streamlined and secure monitoring environment.

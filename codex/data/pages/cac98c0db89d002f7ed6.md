 Time Range Filter 

 The Time Range selection
filter appears at the top of all the ADEM dashboards except the Settings dashboard.
You can filter data to display in the dashboard based on the Time Range you
select. 

 The time is localized, so you can filter based
on the local time for your region. Data is fetched every minute,
but the number of datapoints shown in most histograms vary according
to the Time Range selected. 

 For your convenience, ADEM lets you pick the Time
Range from a few predefined ranges or configure your
own date and time range. Datapoints shown in most histograms vary
according to the Time Range selected: 

 If the Time Range selected is... Data point interval in histograms is shown for
every... 

 <48 hours 5 minutes 

 48 hours - <7 days 15 minutes 

 7 days - <10 days 30 minutes 

 10 days - <30 days 1 hour 

 >30 days 3 hours 

 Custom 
 You can set a custom time interval (for example,
start at 5:00 pm on June 1 and end at 4:00 pm on June 2) in addition
to the pre-populated Time Range selections
available in the filter. 

 To create a custom time range: 

 Select Custom in the Time
Range drop down menu. The Select a Time Range dialog
opens. 

 Click the From text box to open the
calendar and select a date and time for the start time of the time
range. 

 Click the To text box and select a
date and time for the end time of the time range. 

 Apply the changes.

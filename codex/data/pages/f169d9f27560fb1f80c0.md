 Notification 

 If the system detects an issue with your
CPU, memory, WiFi or internet connection, you get a notification
on the upper right corner of your device screen. The notification
gives a summary of the issue and provides some preliminary steps
to fix the problem where applicable. 

 The look and feel of the desktop notifications and the Access Experience user interface screens
 depend on the color, text and design schema that your operating system supports. So,
 they could differ a bit from the images shown in this guide. 

 This section provides details on the following supported notification
types.

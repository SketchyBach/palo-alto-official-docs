 View Device and App Status and Information 

 Using the Cortex XDR iOS Widgets 

 Learn how to use the Cortex XDR widgets on your iOS device. 

 Select the Cortex XDR widget to appear on the iOS device home screen and the lock screen, to easily monitor alerts and the connectivity status. When you use the widget, the Cortex XDR agent app does not need to be open for you to see events that have occurred on the device. 

 To open the Cortex XDR app from your device's home screen, tap the Cortex XDR widget.

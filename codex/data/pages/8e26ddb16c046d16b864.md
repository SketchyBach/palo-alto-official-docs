 Collaborative notes and comments 

 Use notes and comments to coordinate case investigations and responses in Cortex XSIAM. 

 Located within the Resolution Center , the Notepad and Comments panels enable team-wide communication and documentation. This workspace ensures all analysts stay aligned by maintaining a continuous record of the investigation in Cortex XSIAM. 

 Capabilities include: 

 Notepad: Record critical evidence, observations, and investigative steps to maintain a shared history for the case. 

 Comments: Share progress updates and discuss the case with team members in real-time.

 Configure Phone Call Blocking 

 Learn how to configure phone call blocking on iPhones, using the Cortex XDR agent app for iOS. 

 Configure Cortex XDR phone call blocking on iPhones. When enabled, this feature allows malicious callers to be blocked, based on the block list that is defined by the administrator. 

 On the iOS device, select Settings , scroll down to Phone , and scroll down to Call Blocking & Identification . 

 Under Call Identification Apps , enable the option for Cortex XDR.

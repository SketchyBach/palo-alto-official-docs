 Remediate Warnings and Alerts 

 How to fix the issues displayed in an upgrade check report. 

 Click a report name to open the report. 

 Click Summary > Checks 
 Flagged. 

 Name: The check that was made. 

 Description: The action needed to remediated the issue
 or an explanation of the check. 

 Status: A critical alert that must be remediated for a
 successful upgrade, or a warning alert to consider making changes before
 upgrading. 

 Details: Specifics of an issue. For example, if a
 Certificate Expiry Check is flagged, the expired certificates are
 displayed. 

 Remediation/Best Practice: What is needed to resolve
 the issue, which may include a specific remediation link. 

 Use remediation links or directions to resolve the issue. 

 Generate a report. 

 Select the pre-remediation and the post-remediation report and
 Compare Upgrade Reports to review the check
 status.

 AL_ZTNA_CONNECTOR_CPU_HIGH 

 Learn about the AL_ZTNA_CONNECTOR_CPU_HIGH alert. 

 Synopsis 

 This alert is raised when the average CPU utilization of the Zero Trust
 Network Access (ZTNA) connector over 30 minutes exceeds 90%. This alert is cleared
 when the ZTNA connector average CPU utilization over 30 minutes is less than
 85%. 

 Alert Code—AL_ZTNA_CONNECTOR_CPU_HIGH

 Prisma Access

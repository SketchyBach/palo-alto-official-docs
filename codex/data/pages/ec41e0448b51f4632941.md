 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 NGFW (Managed by Panorama) 

 Customer Support Portal 

 Panorama serial number 

 Record the Panorama serial number or auth-code
and record your Sales Order Number or Customer ID. 

 For the auth-code, Sales Order Number, or Customer ID,
see the order fulfillment email that Palo Alto Networks Customer
Service sent when you placed your order for Panorama. 

 For
the serial number, the location depends on the model: 

 M-Series
appliance—Log in to the Panorama web interface and record the Serial # value
in the Dashboard tab, General Information
section. 

 Panorama virtual appliance—See the order fulfillment email or
refer to the serial number generated when provisioning Panorama using VM
Flex licensing . 

 The Panorama virtual appliance is
automatically registered when you allocate a serial number using
VM Flex licensing. 

 Register Panorama in the Palo Alto Networks Customer
Support Portal (CSP). 

 The steps depend on whether you already have a login for
the Palo Alto Networks CSP. 

 If this is the first Palo
Alto Networks appliance you are registering and you do not yet have
a CSP login: 

 Go to the Palo Alto Networks CSP . 

 Click Create my account . 

 Enter Your Email Address and respond
to the reCAPTCHA prompt. 

 Click Submit after you successfully
respond to the reCAPTCHA prompt. 

 Select Register device using Serial Number or
Authorization Code and click Submit 

 Complete the fields in the Create Contact Details and Create
UserID and Password sections. 

 Enter the Panorama Device Serial Number or Auth
Code . 

 Enter your Sales Order Number or Customer
ID . 

 Respond to the reCAPTCHA prompt. 

 Click Submit after you successfully
respond to the reCAPTCHA prompt. 

 If
you already have a CSP login: 

 Log
in to the Palo Alto Networks CSP . 

 Click Products Devices Register New Device . 

 You
can also Register a Device in the CSP Support
Home . 

 Select Register device using Serial Number and
click Next . 

 Enter the Panorama Serial Number . 

 Enter the Device Name to apply a name
to search for and identify your Panorama. 

 ( Optional ) Select a Device Tag to
group Panorama with any other devices for which you have selected
a device tag. 

 The device tag must first be created at the account level ( Products Devices Device Tag ) before it can be selected when you register
 Panorama. 

 If the Panorama management server is not internet-connected,
check Device will be used offline and select
the OS Release version. 

 Enter the required Location Information (as indicated by
the asterisks) if you have purchased the 4 hour RMA. 

 Agree and Submit the EULA. 

 After
you see the registration complete message, close the Device Registration dialog.

 ION 1200-C-5G Back Panel 

 The following image shows the back panel of the cellular
 ION 1200-C-5G-WW device and the table describes the back panel components. 

 Item Component Description 

 1 and 11 Antenna Connector Antenna SMA (F) connectors. 

 2 Serial Number Serial number of the ION device. 

 3 Earthing Earth ground connection. The ground connector
is not required during normal operation. 

 4 Part Number Part Number of the ION device. 

 5 IMEI Label IMEI number of the device. 

 6 SIM Cover SIM cover that covers the SIM slots. 

 7 SIM slots 2 SIM slots for cellular network connectivity. 

 8 QR Code QR code links to the SIM installation section
of the Hardware Reference. 

 9 Screws M3 screws. 

 10 QR Code QR code links to the ION .

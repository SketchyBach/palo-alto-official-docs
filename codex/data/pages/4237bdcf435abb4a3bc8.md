 GenAI Prompt Logging in AI Access Security 

 Release Date: August 2026 
 | 
 Last Updated: September 2026 

 You can now capture and audit all user-submitted prompts to GenAI apps to address critical gaps in visibility and compliance. GenAI Prompt Logging logs prompts across your and Prisma Access enforcement channels, providing a centralized forensic record of GenAI interactions in your organization. 

 To enable prompt logging , you configure the GenAI Prompt Logging settings under Enterprise Data Loss Prevention (E-DLP) and select the GenAI apps you want to monitor including ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, and Perplexity. The system captures sanitized prompts, removes non-prompt data such as HTTP headers and API request code, and de-duplicates interim versions to provide clean, accurate records. 

 Logged prompts are stored in Strata Logging Service and accessible through the GenAI Conversation Log, where you can search, filter, and investigate prompt activity by user, app, classification, channel, and risk level. You can export prompt data to CSV or forward logs to your SIEM for integration with existing security workflows. Role-based access controls ensure that only Super Admins can view raw, unsanitized prompts for forensic auditing.

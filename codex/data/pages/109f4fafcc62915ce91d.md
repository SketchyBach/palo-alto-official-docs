 Convert Your Panorama Virtual Appliance 

 Convert your Panorama™ virtual appliance from an evaluation
to a production employment and from a production deployment to ELA
licensing. 

 You can convert your evaluation Panorama™
virtual appliance to a production Panorama virtual appliance to
preserve its existing configuration and begin leveraging the management
platform. 

 If you are utilizing Enterprise License Agreement (ELA) licensing,
you can convert an existing production Panorama virtual appliance
to leverage the benefits of ELA licensing.

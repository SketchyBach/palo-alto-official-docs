 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 NGFW (Managed by Panorama) 

 Strata Logging Service license 

 Cloud Services plugin installed 

 Strata Logging Service is Palo Alto Networks’ cloud-based logging infrastructure. Before
 you can configure your managed firewalls to send logs to Strata Logging Service (previously called the Logging Service), you need to purchase a license for the
 volume of logs in your deployment, and install the cloud services plugin. If you
 already have on premise Log Collectors, you can use Strata Logging Service to
 complement and augment your existing setup.

Panorama Plugins .

 Strata Logging Service . 

 For firewalls running PAN-OS 8.1 or later releases, you can opt to send logs
 to both the Strata Logging Service and to your Panorama and on
 premise log collection setup when you select Enable Duplicate
 Logging (Cloud and On-Premise) . When enabled, the firewalls
 that belong to the selected Template will save a copy of the logs to both
 locations. You may select either Enable Duplicate Logging (Cloud
 and On-Premise) or Enable Strata Logging Service , but not both.

 ION 5200 Overview 

 Lets learn more about the ION 5200 Overview. 

 Prisma SD-WAN ION 5200 is a next generation
software-defined device that can be deployed in a large branch or
a data center. With the introduction of ION 5200, accelerate your
SASE deployment to a large branch or a DC by leveraging WAN connectivity without
installing additional hardware to enable rapid deployments. 

 The new high-performance ION 5200 device comes
with higher power SFP+ ports to support smart SFPs with speeds 1G/10G, RJ-45
1G ports and RJ-45 1/2.5G ports with PoE to power to power external
cellular gateways and other PoE powered devices such as IP phones
or cameras or Wireless Access Points.

 Platform Overview 

 Reports 

 From this page, you can generate executive reports that provide a high-level overview of your organization's data management strategy and performance in the cloud. Each executive report includes a summary of your environment’s inventory, asset distribution, risk posture, risk location, and real-time protection status. 

 To generate an executive report in a PDF format, click the Download icon. 

 Previously generated reports are displayed under Generated Reports .

 Advanced WildFire Public Sector Cloud 

 Palo Alto Networks offers access to a high-security version of the Advanced WildFire
 Public Sector Cloud in US regions to Public Sector (Federal, State, Local, High Education,
 etc) customers who need to operate within a high-security environment. 

 Palo Alto Networks now offers general availability to a high-security Advanced WildFire Public Sector Cloud in US
 regions to Public Sector (Federal, State, Local, High Education, etc) customers who need
 to operate within a high-security environment. 

 The Advanced WildFire Public Sector Cloud complies with Federal Encryption
 Standards (FIPS 140-2) and meets FedRAMP Moderate Baseline / Impact standards, while
 operating using a newer codebase, similar to that of the current Advanced WildFire
 Commercial Public Cloud, for up-to-date analysis capabilities. 

 To access the new Advanced WildFire Public Sector Cloud, you must have
 a valid SKU (contact your 
 Palo Alto Networks sales contact for info about
 addon SKUs) associated with your Advanced WildFire subscription and configure your
 NGFW and Prisma Access devices to forward unknown files and email links for analysis
 to the following: https://pubsec-cloud.wildfire.paloaltonetworks.com. 

 Existing WildFire U.S. Government Cloud Customers: 

 Existing customers will be able to access the Advanced Wildfire Public Sector
 Cloud by reconfiguring their firewalls to use the new URI. 

 In addition to configuration updates, you will need to make any Advanced
 WildFire API calls that use the Advanced WildFire regional clouds to use the new base
 URI. 

 The existing WildFire U.S. Government Cloud (which complies to FedRAMP moderate
 certification standards) is planned for decommission on November 30, 2024 . At
 that time, the existing URI will be redirected to the Advanced WildFire Public Sector
 Cloud. 

 For details about the new cloud offerings, contact your Palo Alto Networks
 sales representative point of contact to discuss any additional operational details. 

 For more information about Palo Alto Networks cloud-based products that provide
 access to FedRAMP-accredited environments, refer to Palo Alto Networks FedRAMP Authorized
 Services .

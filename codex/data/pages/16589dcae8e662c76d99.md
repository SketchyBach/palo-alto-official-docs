 AL_ZTNA_CONNECTOR_APP_STATUS_DOWN 

 Learn about the AL_ZTNA_CONNECTOR_APP_STATUS_DOWN alert. 

 Synopsis 

 An application in the connector-group is completely unreachable and occurs when the
 application doesn't respond to periodic connector probes or all the tunnels in a
 connector-group are down. The alert is cleared when the application becomes
 reachable from this connector or all connectors. 

 Alert Code—AL_ZTNA_CONNECTOR_APP_STATUS_DOWN 

 Required License 

 Prisma Access 

 Severity 

 High/Critical

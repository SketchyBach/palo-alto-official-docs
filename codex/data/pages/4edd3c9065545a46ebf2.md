 NetSec Health Insights: NGFW Devices 

 View the health metrics of your NGFW devices across all locations. 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 NGFW , including those funded by Software NGFW
 Credits 

 AIOps for NGFW Premium license (use the Strata Cloud Manager app) or Strata Cloud Manager Pro 

 → The features and capabilities available to you in Strata Cloud Manager depend on which license(s) you are
 using. 

 The NetSec Health Dashboard provides centralized monitoring of your NGFW devices'
 operational status across your network as viewed on a global geographical map. The
 NGFW Devices tab shows the number of devices that are healthy, unhealthy, or
 unmonitored, along with the total number of incidents. The map view forms the central
 part of the dashboard, showing your devices based on their geolocation data. Devices are
 color-coded to indicate their health status, with clustering functionality for areas
 with multiple devices sharing similar coordinates. 

 NGFW Devices: Map View Interactions 

 In the map view, the number in the circles correspond to a cluster of user devices
 that are geographically colocated. A dot represents a single NGFW device. A green
 circle or dot indicates a healthy device. Likewise, a red one indicates an unhealthy
 device. A gray circle or dot indicates non monitored devices. 

 Hover over a circle on the map to view a summary of the number of healthy,
 unhealthy, and not monitored devices in that location. 

 Drill down or zoom in on any location for a more detailed, city-level view. 

 When selecting a device or cluster on the map, the side panel displays
 specific information about those devices, including their status, software
 version, and device roles. 

 The Devices Not Monitored view provides insights into devices that
 have delayed telemetry or have never been monitored, helping you ensure
 complete visibility across your network. You can click the number of devices
 to view a list of unmonitored devices. 

 The Degraded Device Performance view provides insights into incidents
 affecting your devices, categorized by type, and offers recommendations to
 resolve issues. 

 The NetSec Health Dashboard includes filtering capabilities to help you focus on
 specific aspects of your network. You can filter by device type, device model,
 and device role, allowing for precise monitoring of the elements most relevant
 to your current needs. If devices are missing geolocation data, the dashboard
 provides indicators and direct links to the inventory page where you can add
 this information. You can also click the NGFW Status Monitoring icon to
 navigate to NGFW List .

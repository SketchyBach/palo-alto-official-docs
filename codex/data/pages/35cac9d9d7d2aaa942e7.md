 Description 

 A user added a bot to a team in Microsoft Teams. 

 Attacker's Goals 

 Attackers may leverage Teams bots to maintain persistent access to compromised Teams accounts. 

 Investigative actions 

 Confirm that the bot was created by a certified and trusted entity. 

 Evaluate the permissions requested by the bot to determine if they are excessive or unusual. 

 Determine if it is within the user's role to add bots to teams. 

 Follow further actions done by the account.

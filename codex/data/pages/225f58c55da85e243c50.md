 Enable Self Serve
 Notification 

 When a device, internet, or WiFi issue that may impact the performance of
 your applications is detected, a notification appears describing the issue. Click
 Review in Access Experience to open the Access
 Experience page. This page contains further information about the
 issue. 

 On MacOS, the first time an application attempts to display
a notification the operating system will ask you for permission to do so, if you select
no this disables notifications for this app in system settings. You can
enable the notification at any time later on. On Windows, the notifications
are enabled by default. You can enable/disable notifications
in your system
Settings. 

 The look and feel of the desktop notifications and the Access Experience user interface screens
 depend the brand and theme settings . So, they could
 differ a bit from the images shown in this guide.

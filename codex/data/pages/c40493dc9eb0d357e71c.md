 Monitor Site Experience 

 The Sites tab in NetSec Health dashboard provides a unified view of
 Prisma SD-WAN and third-party sites connected across the network. 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Prisma Access (Managed by Strata Cloud Manager) 

 One of these: 
 Prisma Access license and ADEM Observability 

 Strata Cloud Manager Pro 
 license 

 The Sites tab in NetSec Health dashboard provides a unified
 view of Prisma SD-WAN and third-party sites connected across the network. It
 displays a map view of sites and data centers connected to remote networks and
 provides detailed metrics of on-site connectivity and experience scores across
 networks. This enables you to monitor the status of your remote sites and data
 centers. 

 This dashboard shows the number of configured sites in your deployment. The
 number enclosed in parenthesis is the total number of connected sites and the number
 to the right is the number of sites that are up with Good experience scores. Sites
 are considered based on score for SD-WAN or tunnel status and incidents. 

 The blue line chart indicates the trend of average experience score for all
 sites over time. Below the configured sites, you see the number of sites with
 experience scores as Good/Fair/Poor along with the number of incidents for all
 sites. You can filter the data by Sites only, Sites and Data Centers ,
 or Sites and Prisma Access Locations . 

 If you have Juniper Mist integrated third-party sites,
 you will see Juniper Mist sites in the dashboard. To know more about the
 integration, refer to Juniper Mist Integration . 

 Sites - Map View Interaction 

 You can drill down to a specific site to know the site details such as the PA
 location, DC connected to, standard VPN, secure fabric, and any open incidents.
 It also displays flight paths on the map that shows the connections from
 that site to other locations like a PA gateway or another data center. The color
 of the path indicates the health of the destination. 

 Degraded Site Experience 

 This panel displays the data by Incidents and Segments. Incidents for Prisma
 SD-WAN, Juniper Mist are categorized into infrastructure, network services, data
 centers, and third- party sites. You can further drill down to a specific
 incident by selecting the incident you want to investigate. 

 Site Experience Trends 

 If you have AI-Powered ADEM enabled, you can view site experience trend.
 Click the Experience Trends to view a time series, followed by the
 end-to-end topology chart for Prisma SASE sites.

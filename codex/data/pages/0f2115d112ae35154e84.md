 Get started with Autonomous DEM in Prisma Access to continually monitor the digital
 experience across all users, applications, and remote sites in your SASE
 environment. As long as you have the respective licenses, you can enable Autonomous DEM for your GlobalProtect Mobile Users connecting to Prisma Access or Prisma SD-WAN remote sites that are connected to
 Prisma Access , or both. 

 Before you configure application tests, review ADEM Deployment
 Best Practices to understand how to distribute monitoring across Mobile
 Users, Remote Sites, and Prisma Access Compute locations for optimal coverage
 without impacting endpoint or network performance.

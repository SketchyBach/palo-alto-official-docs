 ION 5200 Back Panel 

 The following table describes the back
panel components of the ION 5200. 

 Item Component Description 

 1 Fan Exhaust Provides ventilation and cooling for the device. 

 The
fans are not field replaceable. 

 2 Ground stud Use the single post ground stud to connect
the firewall to earth ground (ground cable not included). 

 3 PS1 and PS2 Use the power supply inputs to connect AC power
to the ION 5200. The second power supply is for redundancy. When
facing the back of the device, PS1 is on the left and PS2 is on the
right.

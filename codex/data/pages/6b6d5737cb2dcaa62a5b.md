 Where Can I Find My Apps? 

 Learn where to find your apps on the Activation Console . There are choices
 depending if the apps have migrated to a tenant service group (TSG) or not. 

 On the Activation Console , where you find your apps depends on whether
 they have migrated to a tenant service group (TSG): 

 The tenant view displays app instances that support TSGs. 

 The support account view shows you app instances that are not yet
 TSG-compatible. 

 Once an app gains TSG support, instances of the app begin transitioning to the
 tenant view. This happens on a rolling basis, so certain instances of the app may remain
 in the support account view until they are transitioned. If you don't see the app
 instance you are looking for in one view, check the other. 

 For apps in the tenant view, use Common Services for license activation, subscription
 management, tenant management, and identity and access management. Subscription and
 tenant management are only available in the tenant view for TSG-compatible apps. 

 If you do not see your apps in the tenant view, continue to manage license activation and roles from the support account view.

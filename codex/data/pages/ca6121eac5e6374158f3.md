 Cortex XSOAR 8.14 On-prem 

 Open a support session 

 Open a Cortex XSOAR 8.14 On-prem support session from the VM textual UI or SSH. 

 Some deployment issues require investigation beyond the log bundles. Open a support session to give Support or Engineering temporary elevated access to your system for troubleshooting and debugging. 

 When support/engineering finishes their troubleshooting or debugging activities, they log out of the system. The shell closes, and you return to the Textual UI menu. 

 (Optional) Share your screen with the support/engineering person. 

 From the Textual UI menu in your VM, select Support Session . 

 If the Tenant ID field is not populated, upload the tenant license to Cortex XSOAR and then select Refresh . For more information, see Add the Cortex XSOAR license . 

 The Tenant ID displays the tenant license. 

 If your Cortex XSOAR UI is not available, upload your license to the textual UI . 

 Perform secure authentication. 

 Provide the value from the Challenge field to the support/engineering person who is assigned to help you. They will then provide you with a token. 

 Clicking Refresh generates a new challenge. 

 Enter the token in the Token field and select Submit . 

 The support/engineering person now has access to your system in a secure support shell as a user with elevated permissions.

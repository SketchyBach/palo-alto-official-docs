 Severity 

 Critical 

 Title 

 Ability to make configuration changes to Prisma Access may be
 impacted 

 Description 

 We are aware of a service degradation with Prisma Access that may
 be impacting your ability to configure your service. We're
 working on restoring the experience and will provide the next
 update in the Activity widget below. No action is required on
 your side.

 Rack Mount the ION 2000 

 The ION 2000 can be mounted on a standard 19 inch rack.
Learn how to rack mount the appliance using the given brackets. 

 Rack-mount the ION 2000 on a standard 19 inch
rack. In the optional rack-mount kit, there are two identical L-shaped
brackets that is attached to either side of the ION 2000 with two
(2) screws. 

 After you attach the brackets, mount the ION 2000
on to any standard 19 inch rack using three appropriate screws. 

 Gather the L-shaped rack-mounting brackets. 

 Locate the two (2) screw holes on each side of the device. 

 Attach the L-shaped brackets to the screw holes on either
the front or back of the device. 

 Ensure that the screws are securely tightened into the brackets. 

 Attach the L-shaped rack-mounting brackets to a standard
19 inch rack with the long-threaded nails. 

 Finally, verify that the device is securely mounted on
the rack.

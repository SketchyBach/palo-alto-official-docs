 PA-52R-5G 

 Learn how to mount the PA-50R Series firewall on a wall. 

 The following table lists the items included in the PAN-PA-52R-WALL-MOUNT kit. 

 Parts List – PAN-PA-52R-WALL-MOUNT 

 Quantity Item 

 1 Wall mount bracket 

 4 Phillips pan head screw — M4 × 8mm, stainless steel 

 4 Torx head screw (T10) — M3 × 5mm, zinc-plated steel, nylon
 patch 

 2 Desiccant 

 Mark the locations on the wall that line up with the four wall mount holes on
 the bottom of the wall mount bracket. 

 Ensure there are no building services (water, gas, or wiring) behind the
 wall where you intend to install the firewall. 

 Drill holes into the four marked locations on the wall. Make sure to remove any
 loose debris around the holes. 

 Attach the firewall to the wall mount. 

 Identify the four screw holes on the bottom panel of the
 PA-52R-5G. 

 Align the wall mount bracket with the four screw holes. 

 Secure the wall mount bracket to the firewall using the four provided
 T10 Torx M3 x 0.5 screws. 

 Using four M4 Pan head screws screws (two for each side), install the wall
 mount bracket to the previously drilled holes in the wall. 

 Ensure that the firewall is oriented in such a way
 that its front and side panel components are accessible.

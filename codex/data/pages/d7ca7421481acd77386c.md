 Discover Certificates in Kubernetes Clusters 

 Connect Kubernetes clusters to Next-Gen Trust Security to gain visibility into certificate activity in your Kubernetes environments. Use Next-Gen Trust Security to view discovered certificates and monitor their health, automate issuance and renewal, and identify risks. 

 Note : Kubernetes Clusters is available whichever workspace you have selected, and connecting a cluster needs the Superuser role. The cluster belongs to the workspace that was selected when you connected it, and the Workspace column on the Kubernetes Clusters page shows which one. To learn more, see Access Management and Workspace-Scoped Resources . 

 Clusters appear on the Kubernetes Clusters page, accessible from Insights > Kubernetes Clusters , where you can: 

 Connect Kubernetes clusters to NGTS through a connection wizard . 

 Disconnect Kubernetes clusters from the cluster row's right-hand menu. 

 View discovered certificates for all clusters connected to Next-Gen Trust Security. 

 View a status summary in the Summary panel , including unhealthy connections and unique certificates. 

 View cluster information including key details such as properties, issuers, and installed components.

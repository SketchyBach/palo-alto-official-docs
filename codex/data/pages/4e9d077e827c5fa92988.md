 Use Autonomous DEM in Prisma Access to continually monitor the digital experience across all users, applications, and remote networks in your SASE environment. As long as you have the respective licenses, you can enable Autonomous DEM for your GlobalProtect Mobile Users connecting to Prisma Access, or for Prisma SD-WAN remote sites that are connected to Prisma Access, or both.

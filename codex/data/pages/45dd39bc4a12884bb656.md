 What's New in Network Discovery 2.3.0 

 Review the new feature introduced in Network Discovery plugin 2.3.0.

 New Feature Description 

 Multi-threading Support for SNMP Jobs 

 PAN-OS 10.2.17 and later 10.2 releases 

 PAN-OS 11.1 and later 11 releases 

 You can shorten the runtime for your neighbor discovery and
 data refreshment jobs by enabling multi-threading. When you
 configure the jobs, you can choose the maximum number of threads
 to use.

 Before configuring multi-threading, consider your firewall model
 and specifications, the usage of your firewall, and how many
 switches you have in your network. Running the SNMP jobs with
 a high number of threads may consume a lot of resources
 and affect the firewall's performance.

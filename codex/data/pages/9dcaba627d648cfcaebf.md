 View Threat Summary Report Details 

 View the threat summary report on the AutoFocus
portal or generate a printable PDF of the report. The version of
the report on the portal is interactive and lets you see the exact
figures that make up the chart data. 

 Click Reports on the navigation
pane. 

 Configure the report settings
to choose a time period for filtering the report details, and Generate the
report. 

 Your Malware
Session Percentage By Day is compared with the figures for
your industry. 

 Hover over chart elements to view exact counts or percentages. 

 Click on a bar in the Top
Firewalls or Top Upload Sources chart
to add the value to a search. 

 For the charts Malware Session Percentage
By Day and Top Filetypes Per Application ,
select which data to display or hide. 

 Hide filetypes that are seen in larger quantities
to view the counts for filetypes that are seen in smaller quantities. 

 Click on a tag to view Tag Details . 

 Click Download PDF to generate
a PDF of the report.

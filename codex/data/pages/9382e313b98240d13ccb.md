 PA-1400 Series Firewall Power Cord Types 

 Learn about the various AC and DC power cord types that are compatible with the PA-1400
 Series firewall. 

 The following table lists the PA-1400 Series firewall power cords. 

 SKU Number 

 Description 

 PAN-PWR-CORD-US 

 Power cord for North America with IEC-60320 C13 and NEMA 5-15P cord
 ends, 10A, 125V max, 6ft 

 PAN-PWR-C13-C14 

 Power Cord for PDU with IEC-60320 C13 and IEC-60320 C14 cord ends,
 15A, 250V max, 10ft 

 PAN-PWR-CORD-AUS 

 Power Cord, Australia, 10A, 250V, IEC-60320 C13 and AS/NZS 4417 cord
 ends, 6ft 

 PAN-PWR-CORD-EU 

 Power Cord, Europe, 10A, 250V, IEC-60320 C13 and CEE 7/7 SCHUKO cord
 ends, 6ft 

 PAN-PWR-CORD-JP-15 

 Power Cord, Japan, 15A, 125V, IEC-60320 C13 and JISC8303 cord ends,
 6ft 

 PAN-PWR-CORD-JP-250V-15A 

 Power Cord, Japan, 15A, 250V, IEC-60320 C13 and NEMA L6-20P cord
 ends, 8ft, PSE Certified 

 PAN-PWR-CORD-SUI 

 Power Cord, Switzerland, 10A, 250V, IEC-60320 C13 and SEV1011 cord
 ends, 6ft 

 PAN-PWR-CORD-UK 

 Power Cord, United Kingdom, 10A, 250V, IEC-60320 C13 and BS 1363 UK13
 cord ends, 6ft 

 PAN-PWR-CORD-US-13A 

 Power cord, North America, 13A, 125V max, IEC-60320 C13 and NEMA
 5-15P cord ends, 6ft

 Cloud Workspace Rules (CWP) 

 FEATURE 

 DESCRIPTION 

 New compliance standard filter 

 When constructing a Misconfiguration policy, Cloud Workload Rules can now be filtered by Compliance Standard. This enhancement streamlines the discovery of rules mapped to specific standards, ensuring policies are precisely aligned with your organisational compliance requirements.

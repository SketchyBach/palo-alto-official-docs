 ION 5200 Hardware Specifications 

 Prisma SD-WAN ION 5200 device hardware specifications. 

 Learn the hardware specifications of
the ION 5200 device. 

 Feature ION 5200 

 Description Enterprise large branch and data center. 

 Ports 

 Console Port 1 x RJ-45, UART/Micro USB Type B Console 

 WAN/LAN ports 

 Ports 1 - 19 are ethernet ports. By default, all ports are
 DHCP-enabled, ports 1 and 2 are used to connect to internet. 

 11 x 1G (RJ45) 

 4 x 10G/1G (SFP+) 

 4 x MGig 1G/2.5G (PoE++) 

 Bypass Ports 1 - 4 are Bypass pairs (2-pairs, 4 x RJ45) 

 PoE Ports 9 - 12 are PoE, MGig ports indicated with a yellow
 bar between the port numbers, 150W per system, 90W max/port 

 SFP+ Ports Ports 13 - 16 are SFP+ 10G/1G ports. 

 USB 1 x Type-A 

 Flash Storage 240GB NVMe SSD drive 

 Memory 32GB 

 Power Supply 2 plug-in 450 Wat, AC 100–240 V, 50–60Hz 

 Redundant Power Supply Yes (FRU RPS), Hot swappable 

 Mechanical 

 System Cooling Forced Air, 4 Fans. 
 The airflow direction is from the
 front (network ports) to the rear (power supplies) of the
 device. 

 Certifications 

 Certifications IEC 62368-1, cTUVus, FCC & CE Class A, TEC,
 KCC 

 Environmental 

 Operating temperature (3000 m altitude) 32ºF - 104ºF (0ºC - 40ºC) 

 Storage temperature -4ºF - 158ºF (-20ºC - 70ºC) 

 Operating humidity (non-condensing) 5-90% 

 Storage humidity (non-condensing) 5-95% 

 Physical 

 Dimensions 14.15” x 17.15” x1.70” 

 Weight (lbs) 15.5 

 Mount options Four-post Rack

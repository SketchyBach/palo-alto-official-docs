 Install the ION 1200 on a Flat Surface 

 Install ION 1200 on a flat surface such as a desk or
shelf. 

 The ION 1200 series ships with rubber feet attached
to each corner of the device. As pictured below, the ION 1200 device can be horizontally situated on a flat surface. 

 You can also install the ION 1200 on a rack or a wall .

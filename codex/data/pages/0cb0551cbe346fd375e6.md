 Go to Autonomous DEM in Prisma Access 

 Learn how to access the Autonomous DEM functionality
available within Prisma Access. 

 Where Can I Use
 This? What Do I Need? 

 Prisma Access (Managed by Strata Cloud Manager) 

 Prisma Access (Managed by Panorama) 

 Strata Cloud Manager 

 Prisma Access license 

 Autonomous DEM license 

 The hub is a single place where you can access the Palo Alto Networks cloud services
 and apps for your organization. From the Prisma Access app on the hub , you can select Autonomous DEM to monitor the
 digital experience across your SASE environment and use the metrics to identify
 users, remote sites, or applications that are experiencing a degradation and
 pinpoint the cause. To access Autonomous DEM you must have an active Prisma Access
 for Mobile Users license or a Prisma Access for Remote Networks license as well as
 an Autonomous DEM license. 

 Prisma Access is being transitioned to the Prisma SASE Platform to provide these benefits. As a part of this
 transition, you may experience a difference in the steps to access Prisma
 Access. Refer to Transition to the Prisma SASE Platform for further details on the
 changes to expect. 

 To log in to the hub, and get to Prisma Access: 

 To go to Autonomous DEM from the Prisma Access
app on the hub: 

 Use the credentials associated with your Palo Alto Networks
support account to log in to the hub . 

 Launch the Prisma Access app. 

 To go to Autonomous DEM from Panorama: 

 Select Panorama Cloud
Services Insights And Autonomous DEM to
launch the Prisma Access app on the hub. 

 From the Prisma Access app, select Autonomous DEM . 

 If you have activated your Autonomous DEM license, you
will be able start monitoring the digital experience of your SASE
users. If you have not yet activated your license, the ADEM functionality
will be locked.

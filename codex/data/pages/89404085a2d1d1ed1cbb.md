 Deleting Existing Built-in Accounts 

 When you no longer need a built-in account, you can simply delete it. If you don't want anyone to use the built-in account but you also don't want to delete it, just disable it . 

 Deleting an existing built-in account is permanent. Make sure you want to delete it before proceeding. 

 To Delete an Existing Built-in Account 

 Sign in to Next-Gen Trust Security. 

 Click System Settings > Certificate Settings > Built-in Accounts . 

 Find one or more built-in accounts you want to delete and select them. 

 Click Delete , and then click Delete again to confirm.

# Idira CORA AI assistant

Skip To Main Content
Our Products
Idira CORA AI assistant

"Hi, I'm Idira's AI assistant"

Idira's CORA AI assistant transforms the way you interact with the Idira Identity Security Platform Shared Services. With CORA AI capabilities, you can query data from your Idira environment or perform actions that bolster your identity security program outcomes. As a conversational interface, the CORA assistant enables you to engage with Idira's services through natural language. It can understand your queries, provide context-relevant information, and run commands to assist in performing your tasks. The CORA assistant helps you navigate the complexities of identity security and simplifies your daily tasks.

Idira's CORA AI assistant uses Azure OpenAI as the Gen AI tool. You can read more about Idira's commitment to Responsible AI.

Features and benefits

The CORA AI assistant offers a suite of features that enhance your interaction with the Identity Security Platform.

Natural language processing capabilities are integrated into Idira's AI assistant, enabling it to comprehend your queries in regular language phrases. There's no need for you to memorize specific commands or syntax; just write to the assistant as you would to a colleague.

You can perform the following interactions:

Ask questions: Ask the CORA AI assistant about details specific to your environment, such as details about Safes within a certain platform or who has access to a specific Safe - the CORA AI assistant will provide the answers.

CORA AI assistant answers your questions based on the contents found in the official Idira knowledge sources.

Perform tasks: The CORA AI assistant can perform various tasks on your behalf, such as managing accounts and Safes, approving access requests, and retrieving platform information. It is designed to save you time and effort, making those repetitive tasks easier and quicker to perform.

The CORA AI assistant focuses on service approachability and ease of use. This is key to extending identity security to a wider audience. Whether you're a seasoned professional or new to cyber security, the CORA AI assistant ensures that you find what you need quickly and efficiently.

The CORA AI assistant streamlines your interactions with Idira, enhances your user experience, and helps you navigate the complex world of identity security with ease.

Scope

The CORA AI assistant is available throughout the platform on all spaces and services. It is available to users who are assigned to specific user roles.

Supported roles

CORA AI assistant is available to any user who holds at least one of the specific administrative or auditor roles listed below. When users have the required access, CORA is available across all services they are authorized to use.

Manage your chats

Interacting with the CORA AI assistant is managed through the user interface:

Chat interaction options

Service

	

Description




Start a new chat

	

From the menu at the top, select Start a new chat to start a new conversation or clear the previous conversation.




Get a chat suggestion

	

Not sure how to start the conversation? Select one of the provided prompts to perform a popular action.




Confirm an action

	

Before it performs a request, the CORA AI assistant requires you to review and confirm it. Respond with a Yes or a No to complete the request.




View chat history

	

During a single authenticated session, you can access the chat history. Keep in mind that initiating a new chat clears the chat history.




Send feedback

	

Your feedback is valuable. Click a thumbs up for any helpful or accurate responses or a thumbs down if the response missed the mark. You can provide more details in the open text box.

Disable AI functionality

You can disable AI features or tools at any time.

In the Setup space, select AI preferences and disable the tools you do not require. You can enable these tools and reapply them to your system at any time. For more details, see Set AI preferences.

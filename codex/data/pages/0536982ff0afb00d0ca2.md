 ION 2000 Front Panel with LEDs 

 Learn about the various LEDs lights on the ION 2000 device
and its status. 

 The ION 2000 LEDs indicate the status
of the disk, power, and the controller connectivity: 

 Icons Color 

 Displays disk status 

 Disk activity—Orange light (Blinking) 

 Displays controller connectivity status 

 Connected—Blue light 

 Not Connected—Red light 

 Displays power status 

 Powered On—Green light 

 Powered Off—No light

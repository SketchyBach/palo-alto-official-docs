 The following are different Cluster Statuses: 

 Status Description 

 Not Deployed Appears when the configuration is committed and the deploy button is
 not yet triggered. 

 Deploying Appears when the deploy button is triggered and the deployment is in
 progress. 

 Autoscaling (if autoscale enabled) Appears when autoscale-in is triggered by the plugin and is
 continuing. 

 Autoscaling (if autoscale enabled) Appears when autoscale-out is triggered by the plugin and is
 continuing. 

 Warning Appears when the Nodes are deployed, but not all nodes are in
 ONLINE state. 

 Success Appears when the Nodes are deployed, connected to panorama and are in
 ONLINE state. 

 Failure Indicates a failure during the cluster deployment process. 

 Deleting Appears when the Undeploy button is triggered and the deletion of VM
 HSF cluster in progress.

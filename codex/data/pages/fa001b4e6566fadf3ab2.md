 Issuer Certificates Page 

 The Issuer Certificates page lists all certificates that Distributed Issuer servers have issued or received. You don't create certificates on this page. Your installed Distributed Issuer servers handle certificate issuance automatically based on their configurations. 

 Read the Inventory 

 Each entry shows the Distributed Issuer server, the configuration that generated its CA certificate, and a count of certificates that server has issued, grouped by validity period as follows: 

 Long Lived Certs : Validity period of 90 days or more. 

 Short Lived Certs : Validity period of seven days or more, but less than 90 days. 

 Ultra Short Lived Certs : Validity period of less than seven days. 

 You can filter, sort, and export content using the Next-Gen Trust Security user interface.

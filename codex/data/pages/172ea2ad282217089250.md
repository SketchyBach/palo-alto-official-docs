 show high-availability all 

 Description 

 Show all WildFire
appliance cluster high-availability (HA) information, including
HA control link, HA state, and HA transition information, peer software,
content update, and antivirus compatibility information, and peer
connection and role information. 

 Hierarchy Location

 Syntax 

 all; 

 Options 

 No additional options.

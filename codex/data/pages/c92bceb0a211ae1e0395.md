 Android 10 and later versions

 You can view Prisma Agent connection details and access troubleshooting
 options on the app's settings page.

 Go to the Settings page by tapping the Settings icon. 

 You can find connection status, gateway information, and other relevant details
 on the page. 

 Click the left arrow to dismiss the Settings .

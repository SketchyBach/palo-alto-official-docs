 Review the Widgets in the ACC 

 In the ACC Threat
Activity tab, check the Compromised
Hosts widget and Threat Activity widget
for any critical or high severity threats. In the Compromised
Hosts widget, look into the Matching Objects and click
a Match Count value to view the match evidence for the associated
incident.

 Business 

 The Business view provides an executive-level view of service adoption, usage, and license management across your entire tenant hierarchy. 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Strata Cloud Manager 

 Multitenant hierarchy with one or more child tenants 

 Prisma Access, Prisma SD-WAN, or Prisma Browser licenses 

 The Business view provides an executive-level view of product adoption, usage, and license management across your entire tenant hierarchy. This view does not include product scope selection — it displays all products together for a holistic view. Only the Region filter is available. 

 Service Overview 

 The top section provides at-a-glance metrics for each product: 

 Product Metrics Displayed 

 Prisma Access Total users and branch sites across all tenants (these are the licensing dimensions for Prisma Access). 

 Prisma SD-WAN Total branches, devices, and data centers across all tenants. 

 Prisma Browser Total users across all tenants (licensing is user-based for Prisma Browser). 

 Bandwidth Usage 

 Service Description 

 Prisma Access The total licensed bandwidth allocated across all tenants for each product (Prisma Access, SD-WAN, and Prisma Browser) based on activation-time allocations. 

 Prisma SD-WAN Total aggregate bandwidth consumed across all Prisma SD-WAN tenants. 

 Usage Trends (Subscription and Add-ons Adoption) 

 Trend charts show how usage has changed over time (over a 6 month period): 

 Services — Tenant adoption trends for Prisma Access, Prisma SD-WAN, and Prisma Browser over time. 

 Add-Ons — Tenant adoption trends for add-ons over time. 

 These charts help you identify growth patterns and anticipate when you may need to expand capacity or adjust license allocations. 

 Add-on Adoption 

 This section shows how many tenants have each of the following add-on activated: 

 ADEM 

 Strata Cloud Manager Pro 

 CASB 

 Prisma SD-WAN Branch Security 

 Other available add-ons 

 ZTNA-C 

 Prisma Browser (When activated as an add-on and not as a base license) 

 Prisma SD-WAN (This is the add-on activated on a tenant) 

 This gives you visibility into add-on penetration across your tenant base and helps identify opportunities to expand service coverage. 

 Subscription Utilization 

 The subscription utilization section tracks whether tenants are consuming licenses within their purchased allocation: 

 Status Description 

 Standard Tenants whose active usage is within the purchased license quantity. 

 Over-utilized Tenants whose active usage exceeds the purchased license quantity and may need a license expansion. 

 This view is available per product (Prisma Access, Prisma Browser, Prisma SD-WAN) and helps you identify tenants where license adjustments are needed to align usage with entitlements. 

 Subscription Status 

 The subscription status section tracks the lifecycle of licenses across your tenants: 

 Field Description 

 Subscription Type Yearly 

 Status Active or Expired. 

 Activation Date When the subscription was activated. 

 Expiry Date Displays the subscription expiration date. For subscriptions expiring within 30 days, a countdown is shown. For expired subscriptions, the expiration date is displayed. 

 Click on the expired count to filter the tenant table to only those tenants with expired licenses so you can take renewal action. 

 Tenant Table — Business 

 The tenant table on the Business view includes the following columns: 

 Tenant name 

 Vertical (industry segment) 

 Subscription type (Term - Yearly) 

 Subscription status (Active or Expired) 

 Subscription utilization (Standard, Over, or N/A) 

 Activation date 

 Expiry date 

 You can filter the tenant table by product (Prisma Access, Prisma Browser, Prisma SD-WAN), subscription status, and subscription utilization.

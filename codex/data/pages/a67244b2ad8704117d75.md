 The following tools are either required or recommended when installing the PA-400R Series
 firewall hardware. 

 ESD wrist strap 

 Equipment rack screws 

 PA-410R and PA-410R-5G 

 1/8” flat head screwdriver for terminal block screws 

 Crimp tool for #10 ground lug and 8 AWG wire 

 Wire strippers for 8 AWG and 16 AWG wire 

 PA-450R and PA-450R-5G 

 #2 Phillips-head screwdriver for #6-32 screws 

 1/8” flat head screwdriver for terminal block screws 

 Crimp tool for #10 ground lug and 8 AWG wire 

 Wire strippers for 8 AWG and 16 AWG wire 

 PA-455R-5G 

 Mechanical lift or hoist 

 Drill or hammer drill 

 T-45 Torx bit 

 1.43cm (9/16”) SAE wrench or torque driver capable of reaching 27nm (20 ft-lbs)
 torque 

 Torque driver or wrench capable of reaching 14nm (11 ft-lbs) torque

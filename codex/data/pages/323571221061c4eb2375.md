 Revert a Content Pack 

 Revert a Cortex XSOAR 6.14 content pack to an earlier version. 

 You can revert an installed content pack to an earlier version. 

 Note 

 If you roll back to an earlier version of a content pack, other content packs might be affected. For example, if content pack A depends on layouts from content pack B Version 2, reverting to content pack B Version 1 could cause content pack A to stop working. 

 In the INSTALLED CONTENT PACKS tab of the Marketplace, search for the content pack. 

 In the Version History tab of the content pack, view the previous versions. 

 Choose the version you want to revert to and click Revert to this version . The version will be added to your cart. 

 In the Marketplace cart, click Downgrade .

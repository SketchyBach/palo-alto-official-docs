 Configure Cortex XDR Agent App for iOS 

 Configure Message and Call Reporting 

 Learn how to enable your iPhone to report spam messages. 

 You can configure your iPhone to send Cortex developers information about the blocked messages and calls that you choose to report. 

 On the iOS device, select Settings → Phone → SMS/Call Reporting . 

 Do one of the following: 

 To enable reporting, select Cortex XDR , and then tap Enable . 

 To disable reporting, select None .

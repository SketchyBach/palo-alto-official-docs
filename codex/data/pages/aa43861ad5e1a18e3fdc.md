 Features Introduced in PAN-OS 11.1 

 Discover new features introduced in PAN-OS 11.1. 

 With this release, Palo Alto Networks introduces quantum security protections for
 VPN and enhanced logging visibility, provides additional phishing and patient zero
 protections, and simplifies device management. In concert with our ML-Powered next-generation
 firewalls, these services extend and improve your security posture. PAN-OS® 11.1 leverages our
 cloud-based ML detection engines for artificial intelligence (AI) and deep learning techniques
 to secure the modern enterprise with unmatched performance. 

 Review information about the new features, including Quantum
 Security , Advanced WildFire patient zero prevention , IoT and
 Device-ID , new hardware , and more.

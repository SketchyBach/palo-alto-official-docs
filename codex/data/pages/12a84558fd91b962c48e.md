 Activate Your Advanced SD-WAN License 

 Activate your new SD-WAN license on the firewall. 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 NGFW 

 Advanced SD-WAN for NGFW 

 Follow these steps to activate your new Advanced SD-WAN license on the firewall. 

 Locate the activation codes for the Advanced SD-WAN licenses you
 purchased. 

 When you purchased your subscriptions you should have received an email from
 Palo Alto Networks customer service listing the activation code associated
 with each subscription. If you cannot locate this email, contact Customer Support to obtain your activation codes
 before you proceed. 

 Activate your Support SD-WAN license. 

 You will not be able to update your PAN-OS software if you do not have a
 valid Support license. 

 Log in to the web interface and then select Device Support . 

 Click Activate support using authorization
 code . 

 Enter your Authorization Code and then click
 OK . 

 Activate each- Advanced SD-WAN license you purchased. 

 Select Device Licenses and then activate your licenses and subscriptions in one of
 the following ways: 

 Retrieve license keys from license server —Use
 this option if you activated your license on the Customer Support portal. 

 Activate feature using authorization code —Use
 this option to enable purchased subscriptions using an authorization
 code for licenses that have not been previously activated on the support
 portal. When prompted, enter the Authorization
 Code and then click OK . 

 Manually upload license key —Use this option if
 your firewall does not have connectivity to the Palo Alto Networks Customer Support
 Portal . In this case, you must download a license key file
 from the support site on an internet-connected computer and then upload
 to the firewall. 

 To automate activation using the Customer Support Portal API, see the
 process to Activate Licenses . This
 process works for both the hardware and VM-Series firewalls. 

 Verify that the Advanced SD-WAN license is successfully
 activated. 

 On the Device Licenses page, verify that the Advanced SD-WAN license
 is successfully activated.

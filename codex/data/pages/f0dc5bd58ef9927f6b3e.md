This topic explains which URLs to add to your allowlist, and how to configure presigned URLs for Idira IGA.

Configure presigned URLs

You must add the presigned URL zilla-sync-artifact-<your-environment>.s3.<region>.amazonaws.com to your allowlist if you use Idira IGA on ISPSS. You must add this URL before you sync your applications to maintain proper functionality. Presigned URLs allow you to upload files larger than 10 MB, provide content-type restrictions, and prevent cross-tenant access.

Replace the <your-environment> placeholder with your deployment environment code. Your environment code could be app, app2, eu, anz, uk, ca, br, in, or uat. Use the table below for reference.

Replace the <region> placeholder with the Amazon Web Services (AWS) region where you host your Idira IGA tenant. Contact Support to determine your region.

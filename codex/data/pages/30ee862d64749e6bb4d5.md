 Generate a Quantum-Safe Security Report 

 Download, share, or schedule reports that summarize provide cryptographic posture,
 quantum readiness 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Strata Cloud Manager 

 Quantum-Safe Security
 license 

 Includes access to Strata Logging

 Device Telemetry

 Quantum-Safe Security reports summarize key data and insights related to quantum
 readiness, risk exposure, and overall cryptographic posture for assets discovered
 during the selected time period (past 24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days). Each report
 features an executive summary and dedicated sections for applications, user devices,
 infrastructure, and IoT devices. Key metrics and insights covered include: 

 Insights related to business-critical assets or sensitive data require you to
 configure asset criticality first. 

 Data risk breakdown—volume and percentage of assets, data, tunnels vulnerable to
 Harvest Now, Decrypt Later attacks or data exposure 

 Assets using deprecated protocols or vulnerable ciphers or
 expired certificates contributing to cryptographic debt 

 Trends in cryptographic risk, protocol and algorithm use, quantum
 readiness 

 Trend calculations compare data from the chosen time period against the
 equivalent prior period. For example, a report for the Past 7
 Days compares the most recent 7 days against the
 preceding 7 days. 

 Total count and percentage of quantum-ready assets and percentage of
 business-critical assets that are quantum-ready 

 Top recommendations to become quantum ready or quantum safe 

 Top risk contributors—assets handling the data most at risk of Harvest Now,
 Decrypt Later attack or data exposure 

 Hardware contributing the most to blocking PQC adoption 

 Use executive reports for strategic planning, monitoring migration progress and
 remediation efforts, aligning stakeholders, and supporting audit or compliance
 reporting. 

 You can download the PDF report on demand, share it with recipients, or schedule
 report delivery on a recurring basis from the Overview and
 Inventory tabs of the Quantum-Safe Security app.
 Alternatively, you can generate, share, and schedule reports from
 the Pre-defined Reports ( Strata Canvas Reports ) page in Strata Cloud Manager . 

 Launch the Quantum-Safe Security app. 

 Log in to Strata Cloud Manager . 

 Select Insights Quantum-Safe Security . 

 Set the time period the report covers. 

 From the Overview or Inventory ,
 select a Time Interval : 

 Past 24 Hours 

 Past 7 Days 

 Past 30 Days 

 The report will display insights for the selected time period. You can adjust
 the period when you check report filters 
 before report generation. 

 Click Reports 

 , and then select Download ,
 Share , or Schedule . 

 Configure report information and parameters. 

 Enter a Report Name or
 Description . 

 ( Optional ) Review and adjust the report scope. 

 Click Check Filters for Report
 Content . 

 For Time Interval , select
 Past 7 Days , Past 24
 Hours , or Past 30
 Days . 

 To restore the default value (Past 7 Days), click
 Reset . 

 Click Download , or complete steps to Share a Report or Schedule a Report . 

 Share a Report 

 Share an executive report with specific recipients by email. 

 Launch the Quantum-Safe Security
 app . 

 Set the time period the report
 covers. 

 Click Reports 

 , and then select
 Share . 

 Configure report information and
 parameters. 

 For Recipients , enter one or more email
 addresses. 

 ( Optional ) Enter a Message for the
 recipients. 

 Include context to help recipients
 interpret the report, such as the time period covered, notable changes
 in quantum readiness, or specific risk areas you want them to focus
 on. 

 Click Share . 

 Schedule Reports 

 Automate delivery of Quantum-Safe Security reports to email recipients on a
 defined schedule. 

 Launch the Quantum-Safe Security
 app. 

 Set the time period the report
 covers. 

 Click Reports 

 , and then select
 Schedule . 

 Configure report information and
 parameters. 

 Configure the schedule settings. 

 For Frequency , select
 Daily , Weekly , or
 Monthly . 

 For Start Date , select the date to begin
 report delivery. 

 For Send by , select a report delivery
 time. 

 For Recipients , enter one or more email
 addresses. 

 ( Optional ) Enter a Message for the
 recipients.

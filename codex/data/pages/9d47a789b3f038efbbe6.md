 Adjust the Notification Threshold 

 You can change the default notification
thresholds to suit your needs. 

 Open the Monitored Mobile Users page. 

 Click the Self Serve tab. 

 Adjust the thresholds by
sliding the blue dot on the threshold lines against each notification
type and Save your changes.

 Connect Cables to the PA-3500 Series Firewall 

 Learn how to connect cables to the PA-3500 Series Firewall. 

 After you connect AC or DC power to the firewall, connect your management computer to
 the management port on the firewall so you can begin the initial configuration. You can
 optionally connect your management computer to the console port, which provides a serial
 connection to the firewall and enables you to view the bootup messages and manage the
 firewall using the command line interface (CLI).

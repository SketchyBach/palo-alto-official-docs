 Microsoft Teams Integration with ADEM 

 Release Date: July 2024 
 | 
 Last Updated: June 2026 

 UCaaS performance issues such as poor Microsoft Teams call quality are difficult to troubleshoot because networking teams, UCaaS teams, and helpdesk admins typically lack a unified view correlating call quality metrics with network and application performance data. ADEM now integrates Microsoft Teams meeting metrics with Prisma® Access ADEM synthetic data , giving all three teams the information they need to identify and resolve Teams performance issues from a single dashboard. 

 The Microsoft Teams dashboard in ADEM correlates per-meeting quality metrics directly with ADEM synthetic test results for the same users and time periods. This correlation makes root cause analysis faster—you can quickly determine whether a poor Teams call was caused by endpoint conditions, WiFi quality, internet connectivity, or the Teams infrastructure itself, without querying separate systems. 

 You can use this visibility to resolve issues before they affect end-user experience, provide the helpdesk with actionable context when users report call quality problems, or escalate specific performance data to the UCaaS team. The integration surfaces the data where your teams already work, reducing the time spent gathering information from disparate sources.

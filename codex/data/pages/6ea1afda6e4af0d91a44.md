 Uninstall the GlobalProtect App for Android from Chromebooks 

 Uninstall the GlobalProtect app for Android from a Chromebook. 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Android (Chromebooks) endpoints only 

 GlobalProtect app version 6.1 or later 

 Use the following steps to uninstall the GlobalProtect
app for Android from your Chromebook . Keep in mind that by uninstalling
the app, you no longer have VPN access to your corporate network
and your endpoint will not be protected by your company’s security
policies. 

 Open the Google Play Store app. 

 Click the menu button ( 

 ) next to the Google
Play search bar. 

 Select Apps & games My apps & games . 

 Select INSTALLED . 

 From the On this device area, select GlobalProtect . 

 Click UNINSTALL .

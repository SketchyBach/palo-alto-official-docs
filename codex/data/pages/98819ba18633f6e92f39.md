 Rule Details 

 Cortex AppSec Rule ID 

 APPSEC_LIC_3 

 Category 

 Licenses 

 Severity 

 MEDIUM 

 Framework 

 SCA 

 Impact 

 This policy identifies software packages distributed under weak copyleft licenses. Weak copyleft licenses permit combining code with other licenses, including proprietary licenses, without mandating the entire derivative work to be released under the same copyleft license. Please contact the legal team for further investigation.

 Backup and High Availability 

 Find Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS FAQs for backup, recovery, and high availability. 

 How does High Availability work in Cortex XSOAR 8? 

 High availability is part of the design of the system and is available for all tenants. 

 How will backup work in Cortex XSOAR 8? 

 The Cortex XSOAR 8 tenant's data has full regional redundancy and relies on our cloud provider's controls to keep the data available. In any event, Cortex teams are frequently backing up all configurations and data, and the restore process is tested regularly.

 XSOAR 8x On-prem 

 Bandwidth and NTP Requirements 

 Review bandwidth and NTP synchronization requirements for Cortex XSOAR 8x On-prem. 

 You need the following download bandwidth and node synchronization for Cortex XSOAR to operate properly. 

 Bandwidth requirement 

 The minimum required download bandwidth is 10Mbit/s for successful Cortex XSOAR upgrades and Marketplace operations. 

 NTP requirement 

 Ensure all nodes are synchronized with no NTP offset to prevent degraded storage performance. 

 For optimal time synchronization, it is best practice to specify either a single NTP server or at least three servers. Configuring three or more servers enables establishing a quorum, which improves synchronization reliability and allows better detection and resolution of discrepancies between time sources.

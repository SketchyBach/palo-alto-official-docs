 Managed AIRS for AWS Pricing 

 Cloud NGFW pay-as-you-go (PAYG) pricing. 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Managed AIRS for AWS 

 Palo Alto Networks Customer Support Account (CSP) 

 User role (either tenant or administrator) 

 Managed AIRS for AWS is available as a pay-as-you-go (PAYG) subscription in
 the AWS Marketplace . With this model, you pay only
 for what you use each month, with all charges consolidated on the invoice you receive
 from AWS. You can also enjoy AWS Marketplace benefits such as consolidated billing Amazon Web Services Enterprise Discount Program (EDP) . 

 You pay an hourly rate for each Managed AIRS resource. You also pay for the amount of
 traffic, billed by the gigabyte, processed by the NGFW resource. Additionally, you pay
 an hourly rate and for the amount of traffic processed by your Managed AIRS resource
 when you configure security services add-ons (such as Threat Prevention, Advanced URL
 Filtering, DNS Security, or WildFire) or the centralized management add-on. The rate
 charged for the traffic also depends on the aggregate traffic processed by all NGFWs in
 the tenant during the month (referred to as tiered traffic pricing). 

 Credit Pricing Model 

 You can procure and associate Software NGFW Credits to your tenant by paying an upfront cost
 for a long-term contract of one, two, or three years. You purchase these credits while
 taking advantage of AWS Marketplace benefits such as consolidated billing, AWS EDP, and
 automated or configurable renewals. Software NGFW credits allow you to consume Managed
 AIRS resources in your tenant at a lower cost up to a specific capacity until your
 contract expires. You can add contract credits to your tenant through the Software NGFW
 Credits process. 

 The Managed AIRS for AWS platform manages all credit enforcement as follows: 

 The platform ensures a tenant has an active Cloud Marketplace
 subscription and meters consumption hourly as PAYG units. 

 When a customer allocates credits to a tenant, the platform begins
 to meter that tenant's usage both as hourly PAYG units and equivalent Credit
 units. 

 It continuously validates if the tenant's consumption exceeds the
 allocated credits. 

 If a customer goes over their limit, the platform calculates the
 overage and sends PAYG metering records to the Cloud Provider’s marketplace
 metering service. 

 Thus, the platform seamlessly switches between Software NGFW
 credits and an active Marketplace subscription to ensure uninterrupted
 service. For example, when credits expire, the tenant automatically reverts
 to PAYG. 

 If your monthly average consumption exceeds the purchased
 credits, overages are charged at PAYG rates. 

 If you add Software NGFW credits during a free-trial period,
 your contract starts immediately and overrides the free trial. 

 Use the Managed AIRS for AWS pricing estimator to help
 you determine AWS pricing for your Managed AIRS tenant.

 Centralized Management add-on Consumption 

 You can use a Strata Cloud Manager to manage policy rules in your Managed AIRS resources.
 In that case, your centralized management add-on consumption is metered on each NGFW
 resource for each hour you have associated with a Strata Cloud Manager and for the
 amount of traffic processed by that NGFW resource, when you configured it. The rate
 you’re charged for the traffic also depends on the aggregate traffic processed by all
 NGFWs in the tenant during the month (referred to as tiered traffic pricing ).

 Managed AIRS sends logs to the same Strata Logging
 Services associated with your Strata Cloud Manager. You don't pay for additional
 storage. When used with Managed AIRS for AWS, Strata Logging Services automatically
 scales along with the Managed AIRS for AWS resources. As traffic throughput
 increases on these Managed AIRS resources, so does your available storage so that
 you don't need to worry about making manual adjustments to storage to save your log
 data.

 AWS Marketplace Metering Mechanism 

 Managed AIRS uses the AWS SaaS subscription pricing model by translating the tenant’s
 consumption into Units across multiple Custom dimensions and reporting it
 to AWS Marketplace, as shown in the table below. This mechanism provides the flexibility
 to aggregate your entire tenant’s consumption based on a few dimensions. These
 dimensions include the deployment hours of all NGFWs, the amount of traffic they secure,
 and the number of security features they use per hour. The Managed AIRS translates
 Software NGFW credits and reports them as add-on units to the AWS Metering service.

 Insert a SIM Card into a PA-400 Series Firewall 

 Learn how to insert a SIM card into a PA-400 firewall to establish a mobile network
 connection. 

 The PA-415-5G and PA-455-5G firewalls
 support two nano SIM slots to enable mobile network connectivity. 

 Remove the SIM cover. 

 PA-415-5G 

 The SIM cover is on the back of the firewall. Unfasten the two M3 screws on
 the SIM cover using a type one Phillips screwdriver. Remove the SIM
 cover. 

 PA-455-5G 

 The SIM cover is on the front of the firewall. Unfasten the single screw to
 remove the cover. 

 With the chamfered corner (sloping edge) of the SIM facing towards the slot,
 gently push the SIM into the SIM 1 or SIM 2 slot until the SIM clicks into
 position. 

 The SIM may get damaged if you
 attempt to insert it into the slot by the wrong end. 

 SIM 1 is the primary SIM slot by default. If you intend to use only one
 SIM, it is recommended to use SIM 1 as the primary slot. If you want to
 use SIM 2 as the primary slot, you must manually configure SIM 2 as the
 primary SIM slot using the Web Interface. 

 PA-415-5G 

 PA-455-5G 

 After inserting the SIM, place the SIM cover back onto the device and fasten
 the screw(s). 

 To eject the SIM from the device, gently push the
 SIM inward using a finger tip. Release the finger tip and pull out the
 SIM.

 Generate the SaaS Security Report 

 Learn how to generate the SaaS Security Report to share with your SaaS security
 team and executive management team. 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 NGFW (Managed by Panorama or Strata Cloud Manager) 

 Prisma Access (Managed by Panorama or Strata Cloud Manager) 

 SaaS Security Inline license 

 NGFW or Prisma Access license 

 Or any of the following licenses that include the SaaS Security Inline license: 

 CASB-X 

 CASB-PA 

 The SaaS Security Report provides a snapshot of SaaS app usage with
 advanced aggregated data and views. Use the SaaS Security Report to assess the risks
 associated with your SaaS apps and take action to reduce your organization’s
 business and security risks. This report serves as a communication tool between your
 SaaS security team and executive management. You can share this PDF report with your
 SaaS security team for a periodic check-in. You can also share this PDF report with
 your executives to highlight the SaaS apps in use in your organization and the
 security risks they pose. 

 The report includes the following summary information for the SaaS apps in your
 network: 

 Overall SaaS app, user, and usage metrics for SaaS apps that are in use in your
 organization, including risk distribution and SaaS app traffic. 

 Categories with the most usage. 

 Most risky SaaS apps. 

 Unsanctioned and tolerated SaaS apps with highest volume usage. 

 SaaS apps with the least number of users. 

 The riskiest content management apps. 

 The riskiest collaboration and productivity apps. 

 The top AI apps by usage. 

 Log in to 
 Strata Cloud Manager . 

 You can generate the SaaS Security Report from the Reports page in SaaS Security Inline . You can also schedule, share, and download reports
 from the Reports page in Strata Cloud Manager . 
 To generate the report from the Reports page in SaaS Security Inline ,
 complete the following steps: 
 Select Configuration SaaS Security Discovered Apps Reports . 

 Choose a Generate Report time frame for your
 report. 

 Wait a few minutes for SaaS Security Inline to process your
 report. 

 To schedule, share, and download reports from the Reports page in Strata Cloud Manager , complete the following steps: 

 Select Strata Canvas Reports Report Templates . 

 On the Reports page, locate the report template for SaaS
 Security Report in the table. From this table row, you
 can use the icons to Schedule ,
 Share , or Download a
 report based on the template. 
 To schedule a report, complete the following steps: 
 Click the Schedule icon. 

 In the Schedule dialog, specify the Time
 Interval for the report. This setting
 determines the scope of historical data included, which
 can be either the past 7, 30, or 90 days. 

 In the Schedule dialog, specify the Start
 Date , Frequency ,
 and the time At which you want
 Strata Cloud Manager to generate the report. 

 Specify the email addresses that should receive the
 report. Use commas to separate multiple entries. 

 To share a report, complete the following steps: 
 Click the Share icon. 

 Specify the email addresses that should receive the
 report. Use commas to separate multiple entries. 
 Strata Cloud Manager shares the most recently
 generated report. 

 To download a report, click the Download 
 icon.

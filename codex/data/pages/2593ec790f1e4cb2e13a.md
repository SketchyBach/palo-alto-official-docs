 IPSec Tunnel Restart or Refresh 

 Network > IPSec Tunnels 

 Select Network IPSec Tunnels to
display status of tunnels. In the first Status column is a link
to the tunnel info. Click the tunnel you want to restart or refresh
to open the Tunnel Info page for that tunnel. Click
on one of entries in the list and then click: 

 Restart —Restart the selected tunnel.
A restart disrupts traffic going across the tunnel. 

 Refresh —Show the current IPSec SA
status.

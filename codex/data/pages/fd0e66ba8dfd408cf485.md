 View the overall performance of Zoom meeting across your organization during a
 selected Time Range . 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Prisma Access (Managed by Strata Cloud Manager) 

 Prisma Access (Managed by Panorama) 

 Strata Cloud Manager 

 Prisma Access license 

 Autonomous DEM license 

 Zoom QSS license 

 This widget shows you the overall performance for all Zoom meetings for all users in your
 organization. It gives you a bird's eye view of how many Zoom meetings the users
 attended and the sum of all the meetings' duration in minutes along with the percentage
 of meetings/minutes without issues. You see the total number of minutes that the users
 spent on Zoom with the breakdown of how many minutes were issues-free and how many
 minutes had issues with Zoom.

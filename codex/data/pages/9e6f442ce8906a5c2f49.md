 Monitor agent operational status 

 You can view the operational status of any Cortex XDR agent that you manage. 

 From the Cortex XDR management console, you have full visibility into the Cortex XDR agent operational status on the endpoint, which indicates whether the agent is providing protection according to its predefined security policies and profiles.

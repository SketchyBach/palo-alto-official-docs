 Onboard a DocuSign App to SSPM 

 Connect a DocuSign instance to SSPM to detect posture
risks. 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Strata Cloud Manager 

 SaaS Security Posture Management license 

 Or any of the following licenses that include the Data Security license: 

 CASB-X 

 CASB-PA 

 For SSPM to detect posture risks in your DocuSign instance, you must onboard your DocuSign
 instance to SSPM. Through the onboarding process, SSPM connects to a DocuSign API
 and, through the API, scans your DocuSign instance for misconfigured settings. If
 there are misconfigured settings in your DocuSign instance, SSPM suggests a
 remediation action based on best practices. 

 SSPM gets access to your DocuSign instance through OAuth 2.0 authorization. During the onboarding
 process, you are prompted to log in to DocuSign and to grant SSPM the access it
 requires. 

 To onboard your DocuSign instance, you complete the following actions: 

 Identify the Administrator Account for Granting SSPM Access 

 Connect SSPM to Your DocuSign Instance 

 Identify the Administrator Account for Granting SSPM Access 

 During the onboarding process, SSPM redirects you to the DocuSign
 login page. After you log in, DocuSign will prompt you to grant SSPM the access it
 needs to your DocuSign instance. 

 Identify the administrator account that you will use to log in to DocuSign
 during onboarding. 

 Sign out of all DocuSign accounts. 

 Signing out of all DocuSign accounts helps ensure that
you sign in under the correct account during the onboarding process.
Some browsers can automatically sign you in by using saved credentials.
To ensure that the browser does not automatically sign you in to
the wrong account, you can turn off any automatic sign-in option
or clear your saved credentials. Alternatively, you can prevent
the browser from using saved credentials by opening the Cloud Management
Console in an incognito window. 

 Connect SSPM to Your DocuSign Instance 

 By adding a DocuSign app in SSPM, you enable SSPM to connect to
 your DocuSign instance. You must consent to specific permissions when adding the
 DocuSign app. 

 Log in to 
 Strata Cloud Manager . 

 Select Configuration SaaS Security Posture Security Applications Add Application and click the DocuSign tile. 

 Under posture security instances, Add Instance or,
 if there is already an instance configured, Add New 
 instance. 

 Select whether you are signing in to a DocuSign production environment or a
 developer sandbox and Connect . 

 SSPM redirects you to the DocuSign login page. 

 Log in to DocuSign using the credentials for the administrator account
that you identified earlier. 

 DocuSign displays a consent form that details the access permissions that SSPM requires. 

 Review the consent form and allow the requested permissions.

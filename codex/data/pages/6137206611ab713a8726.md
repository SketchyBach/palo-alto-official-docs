 Artifact Integrity Validation 

 Signed image without a prior build scan found 

 Rule Details 

 Cortex AppSec Rule ID 

 APPSEC-ATTEST-2 

 Category - Subcategory 

 Artifact Integrity Validation - Attestation Lifecycle Failure 

 Severity 

 HIGH 

 Impact 

 An image was signed via cortex-cli attest sign without a corresponding build-image asset in the Unified Asset Inventory (UAI). A build-image asset is created when a Cortex-CLI scan is performed on the image. Signing without a prior scan means the signature cannot be correlated to a build-image asset, breaking the scan-to-signature provenance chain and allowing an unscanned (and therefore unverified) artifact to carry a trusted signature.

 AI-Powered ADEM Incidents 

 Learn about incidents that require an AI-Powered ADEM license. 

 Where can I use this? What do I need? 

 Prisma Access (Managed by Strata Cloud Manager) 

 Prisma Access (Managed by Panorama) 

 AI-Powered ADEM license 

 Strata Cloud Manager uses the Unified Incident Framework to
 centralize incident management and provide a more contextual experience. The
 product-specific alerts and incidents are now located under Incidents . The previous Alerts & Incidents
 dashboard remains available in read-only mode for reference. 

 This section describes incidents that may be raised when you have an AI-Powered ADEM license. 

 The AI-Powered ADEM license unlocks the mobile users, remote sites, and application
 experience metrics gathered by ADEM based on the ADEM license(s) that you purchase. The
 AI-Powered ADEM License also gives you access
 to ADEM-specific features such as Self-Serve and Zoom Integration. For more information
 about AI-Powered ADEM , you can review documentation for Activation & Onboarding , Administration , User Guide , and Release Notes .

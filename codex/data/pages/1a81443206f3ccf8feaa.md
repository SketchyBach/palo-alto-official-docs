 Unified End User Coaching and ADEM Self-Serve 

 Release Date: June 2026 
 | 
 Last Updated: June 2026 

 To deliver a consistent end user coaching experience at scale, Strata Cloud Manager introduces unified End User Coaching and ADEM Self-Serve capabilities. Previously, managing end-user security notifications required separate configuration across multiple products. This centralized framework empowers IT teams to deliver access experience-related notifications to end-users with your organization’s branding, customized messages, and language localization. This establishes a seamless, unified notification ecosystem across Autonomous DEM (ADEM) and Enterprise DLP to deliver a consistent and robust experience for every user. 

 You can choose between two notification types to match the severity of each event. Modal pop-ups require users to acknowledge a policy event before continuing their work, while Toast banners appear briefly and auto-dismiss after 30 seconds for lower-priority coaching. A single Brand Profile applies your organization's name and accent color to every notification, ensuring a consistent experience across all products and events. The framework delivers notifications in each end user's device language automatically, and default profiles activate immediately so users receive coaching without additional configuration. Additionally, the Access Experience app is upgraded to show active notifications and a history of notifications across the delivery chain.

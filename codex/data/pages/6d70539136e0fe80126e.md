 Description 

 An email contains a hyperlink whose display text shows a URL different from the actual destination URL. 

 Attacker's Goals 

 Deceive recipients into trusting a visible URL while redirecting them to a different destination, often for phishing, credential harvesting, or malware delivery. 

 Investigative actions 

 Review the display text URL and the actual destination URL. 

 Verify the reputation and legitimacy of the destination domain. 

 Check whether the sender or domain is known or trusted within the organization.

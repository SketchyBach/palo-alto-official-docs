 SASE Resilience and Business Continuity 

 Monitor your multi-region high-availability deployment to maintain security
 continuity during regional cloud service provider outages. 

 Where Can I Use
 This? What Do I Need? 

 Prisma Access (Managed by Strata Cloud Manager) 

 Prisma Access (Managed by Panorama) 

 Prisma Access license 

 Prisma Access (Managed by Panorama) deployments only : Minimum Cloud
 Services plugin version of 6.2.0-h2 

 Prisma® Access allows you to deploy multi-region and multi-cloud for mobile users,
 remote networks, and service connections. Prisma Access expands on its
 multi-region high availability (HA) capabilities by adding resilience to services
 that integrate with Prisma Access, including the Cloud Identity Engine and Enterprise Data
 Loss Prevention ( Enterprise DLP ), with an initial rollout
 starting with deployments in the United States. 

 With SASE Resilience, these services deploy across multiple regions for each tenant.
 This architecture automatically mitigates the impact of regional cloud service
 provider (CSP) failures with no configuration required on your part. In the event of
 a regional outage, traffic fails over to the secondary region transparently,
 maintaining security continuity for your users. 

 To protect against CSP-wide service interruptions, you can also deploy SASE
 Private Location to use in-country multicloud and on-premises hosting
 options. These options let you maintain security continuity by routing to an
 alternate cloud provider or your own on-premises infrastructure when a primary CSP
 becomes unavailable. 

 The High-Availability and Resilience view in Strata Cloud Manager gives you
 visibility into your multi-region and multicloud deployment status so you can
 monitor your resilience posture and confirm that HA is active across your network
 security services. The Cloud Identity Engine and Enterprise DLP now have enhanced
 failover capabilities between cloud regions in the United States. 

 The following services provide multi-cloud and multi-region support: 

 Prisma Access 
 Explicit Proxy 

 Remote Networks 

 Service Connections 

 The following services provide multi-cloud and multi-region support and are
 viewable in the High-Availability and Resilience view: 

 Prisma Access 
 Mobile Users 

 Cloud Identity Engine 

 Enterprise DLP 

 To verify your SASE Resilience settings, complete one of the following tasks. 

 Prisma Access (Managed by Strata Cloud Manager) Deployments : 
 From Strata Cloud Manager , select Configuration NGFW and Prisma Access . 

 Set the configuration scope to Prisma Access . 

 Select Prisma Access Infrastructure . 

 In the High-Availability and Resilience area,
 click the gear to display the settings. 

 Review your deployment status. 
 This view shows your multi-region and
 multicloud HA status for Mobile User deployments. No configuration
 is required—multi-region HA is managed automatically by Palo Alto
 Networks. 

 Additional controls and coverage outside the United
 States are planned for future versions of Prisma Access . 

 Prisma Access (Managed by Panorama) Deployments : 
 From the Panorama that manages Prisma Access , select Panorama Cloud Services Configuration Service Setup . 

 In the High-Availability and Resilience area,
 click the gear to display the settings. 

 Review your deployment status. 
 This view shows your multi-region and
 multicloud HA status for Mobile User deployments. No configuration
 is required—multi-region HA is managed automatically by Palo Alto
 Networks. 

 Additional controls and coverage outside the United
 States are planned for future versions of Prisma Access .

 Uninstall the GlobalProtect App for iOS 

 Use the following steps to uninstall the GlobalProtect
app from your iOS endpoint. Keep in mind that by uninstalling the
app, you no longer have VPN access to your corporate network and
your endpoint will not be protected by your company’s security policies. 

 Tap and hold the GlobalProtect app icon until
the icon jiggles. 

 Tap the X on the top-left corner
of the icon. 

 When prompted, Delete GlobalProtect. 

 Tap Done or press the home button
to return to the home screen.

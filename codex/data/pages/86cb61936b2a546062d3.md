 Managing Shifts 

 Create and manage shifts in Cortex XSOAR 6.13. 

 You can define shifts for various roles and then add them to automations, playbooks and incidents. 

 Define a Role. 

 In the Shifts field, click Add Shift and add the required period. 

 Weekly shifts start on Sunday and are specified in the UTC time zone format. 

 For example, we create a role called First Shift and add a shift starting on Sunday and ending Monday. 

 shift.png 

 Click Save. 

 (Optional) To add the role to a user, in the Users tab, select the user you want to add. 

 roles.png 

 Click Roles . 

 From the drop-down list, select the role you created. 

 add-roles.png 

 Click Save . 

 You can also assign the role to a playbook or automation as required. 

 (Optional) You can view on-call users’ details in a dashboard by adding the required widgets. 

 shift-dashboard.png 

 When assigning an incident, you can manually assign it to analysts who are on-call or the incidents can be assigned automatically, for example, the AssignAnlystToIncident automation.

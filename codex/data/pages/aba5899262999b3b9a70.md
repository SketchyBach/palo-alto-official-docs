 Internet Disconnected 

 Receive Autonomous DEM (ADEM) internet disconnected notifications and remediation steps
 when your device loses its internet connection. 

 Where Can I Use
 This? What Do I Need? 

 Prisma Access (Managed by Strata Cloud Manager) 

 Prisma Access (Managed by Panorama) 

 Prisma Access license 

 ADEM or Strata Cloud Manager Pro 
 license 

 GlobalProtect or Prisma Access Agent license 

 Enable Self Serve
 Notification 

 Your device receives the following notification
when it gets disconnected from the internet. 

 Notification Text What it means Suggested remediation 

 Your device is not connected to the internet. Please check all your
 connections. 

 There is an internet outage in your area. 

 There is a problem with your device because of which it is
not connecting to the internet. 

 You may have turned off internet connectivity. 

 Verify that your device is configured
to connect to the internet. 

 Check your local router status and restart it if necessary. 

 Clicking on the notification opens the Access Experience UI opens. It
 gives you more details about the notification along with some suggestions to fix the
 stated problem. 

 The Review in Access Experience link opens a time series historical chart
 displaying the status of your Internet connection during the last 3 hours. A slider bar
 placed at the right end of the chart by default shows you the most recent status. Move
 your mouse sideways on the chart across various points in time, to understand the
 historical status of the active area. Depending on where you mouse over and click in the
 chart, you can view the status and recommendation that was indicated at that specific
 time within the 3-hour window. The red dots indicate that a notification was sent to
 your device at that time where the dot appears.

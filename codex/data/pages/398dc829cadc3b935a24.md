 INC_ZTNA_CONNECTOR_APP_STATUS_DOWN 

 Learn about the INC_ZTNA_CONNECTOR_APP_STATUS_DOWN incident. 

 Synopsis 

 The incident is raised when an application in the connector-group is completely
 unreachable and the application doesn't respond to periodic connector probes or all
 the tunnels in a connector-group are down. The incident is cleared when the
 application becomes reachable from this connector or all connectors. 

 Incident Code—INC_ZTNA_CONNECTOR_APP_STATUS_DOWN 

 Severity—Critical 

 For details about incident severity, see Incidents Distribution Over
 Time in Incidents and Alerts Overview .

 Subnet Support for Behavior Threats Exclusions 

 Release Date: March 2026 
 | 
 Last Updated: May 2026 

 Manually adding individual custom IP addresses to exclude specific hosts from behavioral monitoring creates unnecessary administrative overhead. Subnet support for Behavior Threats exclusions addresses this challenge by allowing you to define entire network ranges directly within your static exclusion policies . Previously, you were limited to single IP entries, but now you can exclude large blocks of trusted infrastructure, such as management subnets or backup segments, using a single CIDR entry. This capability enables you to significantly reduce false positives and alert fatigue by broad-masking trusted internal networks that generate unusual traffic patterns. Furthermore, your policies will automatically cover new hosts provisioned within an excluded subnet, ensuring consistent protection without requiring manual updates for every server addition. 

 Note: This feature is applicable only for the following four static policies: 

 Impossible Traveler 

 Risky IPs 

 Unsafe Location 

 Unsafe VPN

 Support for Airtable Connector in Data Security 

 Release Date: March 2026 
 | 
 Last Updated: May 2026 

 As organizations increasingly rely on agile platforms like Airtable to manage complex projects, the ease of sharing and automating data movement introduces significant risks of accidental data exposure. The Airtable connector for SaaS Security (Beta) addresses this challenge by integrating seamlessly with Palo Alto Networks Data Security . 

 This integration provides comprehensive visibility and control over your data at rest. By onboarding your Airtable enterprise account, you can automatically scan bases, tables, and attachments for sensitive information, such as PII and intellectual property. You gain granular insights into how data is accessed and shared, ensuring collaborators only have the necessary permissions. Additionally, you can automate remediation (only deleting cells) to swiftly respond to security risks by identifying over-shared assets before they escalate into breaches. This capability helps you maintain a continuous audit trail and enforce consistent security policies across your SaaS portfolio. To empower your workforce to leverage Airtable securely, review the documentation for onboarding your Airtable app.

 Apply Security Policies to the VM-Series Firewall on NSX-T (East-West) 

 Direct Traffic to the VM-Series Firewall 

 This section describes ways to direct traffic to the VM-Series firewall. 

 Where Can I Use
 This? What Do I Need? 

 VMware NSX 

 VM-Series Firewall License (BYOL) 

 Panorama 

 VM-Series plugin 

 Panorama plugin for NSX 

 Configure policy rules to direct traffic virtual
machines or groups of virtual machines to the VM-Series firewall. 

 Select Security Network Introspection (E-W) Rules Add Policy . 

 Click New Policy to give your
policy a descriptive name. 

 Select your service chain from the Redirect
To drop-down. 

 Select the policy and click Add Rule . 

 Click New Rule to give your rule
a descriptive name. 

 Select a source. 

 Click the pencil icon in the source column
to choose a source group of virtual machines. 

 Check the source group or groups. 

 Click Apply . 

 Select a Destination . 

 Click the pencil icon in the destination
column to choose a source group of virtual machines. 

 Check the destination group or groups. 

 Click Apply . 

 ( Optional ) Select Services to
which the rule will be applied. 

 Choose one of the following in the Applied
To field: 

 Select DFW to apply the
rule to all virtual NICs attached to the logical switch. 

 Select Groups to apply the rule to
virtual NICs of members virtual machines in the specified group
or groups. 

 Select the Action — Redirect or Do
Not Redirect . 

 Click Publish . 

 Repeat this process to create additional policy or rules.

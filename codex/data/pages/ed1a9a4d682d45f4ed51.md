 Reports: ( Prisma SD-WAN ) 

 Learn how to use reports in Prisma SD-WAN. 

 Prisma SD-WAN 
 WAN Clarity reports provide an aggregate
 view of traffic distribution and bandwidth utilization in your network. You can
 download the entire reports package or view the reports from the Prisma SD-WAN controller, allowing for week-over-week trend
 comparisons, as well as comparisons across sites and circuits. 

 Reports are available for immediate use as a licensed subscription service.
 Contact the Prisma SD-WAN sales team to enable the subscription. 

 Select Strata Canvas Reports Prisma SD-WAN . 

 Click View Reports on WAN Clarity
 Reports . 

 The Prisma SD-WAN 
 WAN Clarity reports include: 
 WAN Clarity Branch
 Reports 

 WAN Clarity Data Center
 Reports 

 Aggregate Bandwidth Usage
 Reports 

 Select a Time Range and select any of the following in
 the Report for field. 

 Branch 

 Data Center 

 Aggregate Bandwidth Usage

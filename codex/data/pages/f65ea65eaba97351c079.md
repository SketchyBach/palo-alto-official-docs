 ION 9200 Overview 

 Learn about the new ION 9200 hardware specifications. 

 Prisma SD-WAN ION 9200 is a next generation
software-defined device that can be deployed in a data center. With
the introduction of ION 9200, accelerate your SASE deployment to
a DC by leveraging WAN connectivity without installing additional
hardware to enable rapid deployments. 

 The new high-performance ION 9200 device comes
with fiber ports offering RJ-45/SFP Combo WAN ports, higher power
SFP+ ports to support smart SFPs, wireless LAN Access points, PoE
to power external cellular gateways, and other PoE powered devices
such as IP phones or cameras or Wireless Access Points.

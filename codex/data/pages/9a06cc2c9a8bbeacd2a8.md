 PA-1400 Series firewall compliance overview. 

 Palo Alto Networks obtains regulatory compliance certifications
to comply with the laws and regulations in each country where there
are requirements applicable to our products. Our products meet standards
for product safety and electromagnetic compatibility when used for
their intended purpose. 

 To view compliance statements for the PA-1400 Series firewalls,
see PA-1400 Series Firewall Compliance Statements .

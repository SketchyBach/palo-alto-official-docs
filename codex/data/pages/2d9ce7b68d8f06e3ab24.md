 Switch Layer 2/Layer 3 Change Mode 

 Lets see how to switch or edit the switch configurations for ION 1200-S and ION 3200
 devices in Prisma SD-WAN 

 Where Can I Use
 This? What Do I
 Need? 

 Prisma SD-WAN (Managed by Strata Cloud Manager ) 

 Prisma SD-WAN 

 The integrated Layer 2 switch ports enable you to connect multiple devices directly
 on the L2 LAN or add downstream switches or Wireless Access Points (WAP). L2 LAN
 switch ports is supported on ION 3200 on ports 5 -10. When the ION 3200 device is
 running in L2 mode, it will function as ION 1200-S, and when the ION 3200 is running
 in L3 mode, it will serve as the current ION 3200 device. 

 The mode change operation is allowed when the device is in a claimed state; it is not allowed in
 unclaimed or assigned or any other states. Also, when the device is
 declaimed or unclaimed, it returns to L3 mode. 
 If the mode change operation
 fails or takes longer than expected, you can attempt to trigger the
 operation again after 15 minutes. The retry process allows for a total of 3
 attempts (the initial trigger and two subsequent retries). After the third
 attempt, if the operation fails, the system will enter a TIMEOUT state.
 Please contact Palo Alto Networks support team to find out the reason for
 the failure. 

 Select Configuration Prisma SD-WAN ION Devices Claimed and select the ION 3200 device you wish to configure. 

 On the Basic Info , click the ellipsis menu and select
 L2 to L3 to switch the Device
 Mode from L2 to L3 or vice versa. 

 On the screen you get the below pop-up to proceed with the change, click
 Proceed to continue the change port. 

 See the Device Mode change from what you intended to
 switch (L2 to L3 or L3 to L2).

 Cortex XDR 5.x 

 License expiration 

 Learn more about the Cortex XDR license expiration and validation period. 

 Cortex XDR licenses are valid for the period of time associated with the license purchase. After your license expires, you have access to your tenant for an additional grace period of 48 hours. After the 48-hour grace period, you no longer have access and it is disabled until you renew the license. 

 For the first 31 days of your expired license, Cortex XDR continues to protect your endpoints and/or network and retains data in the Cortex Data Layer according to your data retention policy and licensing. After 31 days, the tenant is decommissioned and agent prevention capabilities cease.

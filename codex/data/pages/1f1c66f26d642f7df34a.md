 AL_RN_PRIMARY_WAN_TUNNEL_FLAP 

 Learn about the AL_RN_PRIMARY_WAN_TUNNEL_FLAP alert. 

 Synopsis 

 Primary WAN tunnel <tunnel-name> for the remote network is flapping. 

 An alert is raised if the primary WAN tunnel <tunnel-name> for the remote network
 flaps (up to down or down to up) 5 times in the past 10 minutes. 

 Alert Code—AL_RN_PRIMARY_WAN_TUNNEL_FLAP

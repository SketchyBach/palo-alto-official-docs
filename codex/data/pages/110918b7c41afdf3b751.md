 Install the PA-400 Series Firewall on a Flat Surface 

 Install a PA-400 Series firewall on a flat surface such
as a desk or shelf. 

 The PA-440, PA-450, and PA-460 firewalls
ship with rubber “feet” attached to each corner of the device. As
pictured below, the PA-440, PA-450, and PA-460 can be situated on
a flat surface both horizontally and vertically. 

 The PA-410, PA-415, PA-415-5G, PA-455, PA-455-5G, and PA-445 have
 rubber feet installed on their bottom side so they can only be installed in a horizontal
 position.

 Cortex XSOAR 8 (SaaS) 

 Supported host regions 

 View Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS host regions and data residency locations for stored data. 

 Select a Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS host region during activation. Your selected region, such as the United States or United Kingdom, determines where Cortex XSOAR logs and ingested data are stored. Select a region that meets your data residency and regulatory compliance requirements. 

 Cortex XSOAR provides high availability through data redundancy. Data is stored across two zones within your selected host region. During an outage, Cortex XSOAR fails over to the secondary zone to help prevent service interruption. 

 The following tables list available Cortex XSOAR SaaS host regions and associated Cortex services:

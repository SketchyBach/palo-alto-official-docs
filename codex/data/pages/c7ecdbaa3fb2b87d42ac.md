 View the Notification History 

 You can retrieve notifications sent to users within the past 30 days.

 Enable Self Serve
 Notification 

 The notifications sent to users are stored for 30 days as long as the users
 do not either clear their notifications or click on the notification to open the
 Access Experience UI. After 30 days stored notifications gets
 automatically deleted. 

 To view past notifications: 
 Open the Access Experience 
 interface. 

 Click the History tab. 

 Use the search bar and the time period filter to look for any specific
 notification. Review End User Coaching to know more about
 DLP notifications.

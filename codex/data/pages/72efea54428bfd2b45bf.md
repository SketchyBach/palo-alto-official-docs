 What is Cortex XSOAR 8? 

 Cortex XSOAR 8 is the rearchitected version of Cortex XSOAR. It is available as a SaaS service and as an on-prem deployment. 

 What are the major benefits and enhancements of Cortex XSOAR 8 compared to version 6? 

 Cortex XSOAR 8 is tightly integrated with the Cortex platform. As such, it offers: 

 A unified user experience and user management across all Cortex products 

 Enhanced performance, stability, and scalability 

 Simplified deployment and onboarding across the Cortex portfolio 

 How can Cortex XSOAR 8 be deployed? 

 Cortex XSOAR 8 is available as SaaS and on-prem. 

 The SaaS deployment runs on Google Cloud Platform and offers: 

 Availability across multiple regions, including the US, Canada, the UK, the Netherlands, Japan, Singapore, Australia, and India 

 Auto-scaling and built-in high availability 

 Automatic software updates 

 When is Cortex XSOAR 8 available? 

 Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS and on-prem are available for new customers. 

 Existing Cortex XSOAR 6 On-prem customers may migrate to Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS. 

 For more information about migration, see the Cortex XSOAR 8 Migration . 

 Can I get a demo of Cortex XSOAR capabilities? 

 Please contact your sales representative to schedule a demo of Cortex XSOAR 8. 

 Is Cortex XSOAR 8 FedRAMP certified? 

 FedRAMP Moderate — FedRAMP Authorized 

 FedRAMP High — FedRAMP Authorized 

 What is the release cadence of FedRAMP?* 

 FedRAMP follows a quarterly release cycle. Each new version is typically released a few weeks after the General Availability (GA) date. 

 Applies to FedRAMP Moderate and FedRAMP High. 

 What, if any, features or functions are not available in the FedRAMP version?* 

 The main differences are: 

 Unit 42 Intel is disabled. 

 The built-in content repository is available only for FedRAMP Moderate. To enable it, open a support case. 

 Applies to FedRAMP Moderate and FedRAMP High. 

 What about performance limitations? 

 Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS performance limits are listed in Cortex XSOAR Service Limits. 

 Which HTTP protocol should be used? 

 Cortex XSOAR is optimized for HTTP/2. If your browser version is unsupported, or your network middleware does not use HTTP/2, you may see slower page loads and response times. 

 Check with your browser or middleware vendor to confirm HTTP/2 support. 

 Is there downtime during Cortex XSOAR SaaS upgrades or maintenance releases? 

 Planned downtime is typically about 30 minutes. Check the Palo Alto Networks status page for current maintenance and release status.

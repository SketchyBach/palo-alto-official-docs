 ION 3200 Front Panel 

 Check out the ION 3200 device front panel parts. 

 The following image shows the front panel
of the ION 3200 and the table describes the front panel components. 

 Item Component Description 

 1 USB Port USB 3.0 (reserved for future use). 

 2 Console Port RJ-45 Serial console port. 

 3 Micro USB Micro USB Type B Console connector. 

 4 SFP/RJ-45 Combo Ports Ports 1 and 2 are SFP/RJ-45 ports. 

 5 Bypass Pair Ports 3 and 4 are RJ-45 ports with Bypass. Bypass is indicated with orange bars
 on the right side of the ports. 

 6 Ethernet Ports Ports 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 are RJ-45 ports. Ports 7-10 are PoE ports indicated with
 yellow bars above and below the ports. 

 7 Link Speed LED On ethernet ports 1-10, the left LED indicates
the link speed. 

 8 Activity LED On ethernet ports 1-10, the right LED indicates the
activity on the port. 

 9 Power Power Input. 

 10 Restart button Restart button. 

 11 Controller LED Controller LED; the LED turns green on successful connection
with the Prisma SD-WAN controller. 

 12 OS LED Operating System status LED. 

 13 Power LED Power LED; the LED turns green when powered on.

 Monitor and Identify AWS Activities 

 This article describes how to do the following: 

 Monitor and track access to your AWS data assets in real-time. 

 Identify actors who are reading, writing, or modifying your AWS data assets, along with timestamps for their last actions. 

 Visualize actions performed by different actors up to 90 days in the past. 

 Monitor and identify AWS activities 

 Navigate to the storage bucket whose active identities you wish to view. 

 In Prisma Cloud DSPM, click Inventory. 

 In Inventory, click the name of the specific storage bucket you want to view. 

 Click the Active Identities tab. 

 View how and when each actor interacted with the data assets. For example, identify when a specific actor last wrote an event. 

 Use the filters to narrow the search. For example, use the filters to search for all actors who wrote an event before a specific date.

 ION 5200 Front Panel 

 Learn the ION 5200 device front panel parts. 

 The following table describes the front
panel components of the ION 5200. 

 Item Component Description 

 1 Bypass Pair Ports 1 - 4 are Bypass pairs, 4 x 1G RJ45. 

 2 PoE Ports Ports 9 - 12 are PoE MGig ports capable of
 1G/2.5G. 

 3 SFP+ ports Ports 13 - 16 are SFP+ (10G) ports and also supports 1G
 SFP. 

 4 RJ-45 Ports Ports 5 - 8, 17-19 are RJ-45 10M/100M/1000M ethernet
 ports. 

 5 Console Port RJ-45 Serial console port. Use this port to connect a
 management computer to the device using a 9-pin serial-to-RJ-45
 cable. 

 6 Standard USB USB Port. A USB port that accepts a USB flash
 drive. 

 7 Micro USB Port Micro USB Console Port. Use this port to connect a
 management computer to the device using a standard Type-A USB-to-micro
 USB cable. 

 8 LED Indicators LEDs indicate the status of the ION device
 components. 

 9 Power/Reset Power LED; the LED turns green when powered on.

 Changed Features 

 These features have been modified or removed in this release. 

 Feature 

 Description 

 Threat Intelligence 

 The following pages and tabs have been removed: 

 The Sample Analysis page under Threat Management > Threat Intelligence. 

 The Sessions & Submissions page under Threat Management > Threat Intelligence. 

 The Unit 42 Intel tab within the indicator details page. Indicator search in the legacy Unit 42 library has been deprecated. 

 Threat Intelligence 

 Due to the deprecation of AutoFocus, the AutoFocus integration option has been removed from the Threat Intelligence screen under Settings. 

 Threat Intelligence 

 Due to the deprecation of Autofocus, AutoFocus data will no longer appear in cases or issues. 

 Query Center 

 The Simulated Compute Units field has been deprecated.

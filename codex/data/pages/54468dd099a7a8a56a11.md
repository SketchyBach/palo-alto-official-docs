 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Prisma SD-WAN 

 Prisma SD-WAN 

 From the Operator portal, navigate to Certificate Management Generate . 

 In the Generate Certificate dialog, enter the
 certificate information. 

 Select Generate the certificate. 

 This will generate the CSR, use this CSR to generate the
 certificate. 

 Upload the Certificate to the Operator portal. 

 Verify the validity of the Certificate by clicking Check
 Validity . 

 You can view the Certificate on the portal.

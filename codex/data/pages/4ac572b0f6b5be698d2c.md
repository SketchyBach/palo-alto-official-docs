 How Does Email DLP Work? 

 Email DLP 

 Email DLP for Enterprise Data Loss Prevention (E-DLP) prevents exfiltration of outbound emails
 containing sensitive information with AI/ML powered data detections. 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need?

 Email DLP license 

 Enterprise DLP inspects outbound emails forwarded through a smart host and applies
 policy decisions—such as blocking, quarantining, or allowing—based on detected sensitive
 content. For example, Enterprise DLP can block an outbound email containing
 financial data sent from a salesperson within your organization to a personal email
 address.

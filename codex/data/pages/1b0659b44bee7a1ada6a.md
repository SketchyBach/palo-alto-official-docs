 How to migrate from Cortex XSOAR 6 On-prem to Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS 

 Learn how to migrate from Cortex XSOAR 6 to Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS. 

 To migrate from Cortex XSOAR 6 On-prem to Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS, work with your Customer Success or Sales Representative to manually migrate to Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS. Migration doesn't include migrating incident or indicator data. 

 You can create a new Cortex XSOAR 8 tenant for up to 60 days by requesting an evaluation license. You can then decommission your Cortex XSOAR 6 server and move your licenses to Cortex XSOAR 8. Your Customer Success or Sales Representative can help you migrate your content, such as scripts, playbooks, and settings. 

 Note 

 You can keep your Cortex XSOAR 6 On-prem installation and data for audit or compliance requirements. You can still access your Cortex XSOAR 6 instance until EoL or the end of your contract term (whichever comes first). Cortex XSOAR 6 will not support processing production workloads and will only provide 30 days of incident history. 

 Follow the steps in Migrate from Cortex XSOAR 6 to Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS (Self-Service) .

 Scan Event Syslog Default Field Order 

 The following identifies the default field order for filters 

 migrated from an earlier version of the log forwarding application. 

 For log filters created after that migration, you specify the field order when you

 create a log filter 

 by specifying the columns you want to receive.

 The fields are identified in the default order that they appear in each log
 line.

 HEADER, vendor_name , log_source , customer_id , tsg_id , log_time , log_type.​value , sub_type.​value , log_source_group_id , log_source_id , time_generated , time_generated_high_res , log_source_name , log_source_tz_offset , platform_type , created_by , enabled_rule_count , error_code.​value , error_message , eval_outcome.​value , labels , model_author , model_formats , model_name , model_uri , model_version , rule_failed_count , rule_passed_count , scan_origin.​value , scan_uuid , scanner_version , sdk_version , security_group_name , security_group_uuid , source_type.​value , time_started , total_files_scanned , total_files_skipped , violation_count , violations

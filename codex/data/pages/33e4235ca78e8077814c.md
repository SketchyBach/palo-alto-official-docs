 GlobalProtect App for Linux 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Linux endpoints only 

 GlobalProtect app version 6.2 or later 

 GlobalProtect™ is a program that runs on your endpoint
(desktop computer, laptop, or server) to protect you by using the
same security policies that protect the sensitive resources in your
corporate network. GlobalProtect™ secures your intranet, private
cloud, public cloud, and internet traffic and allows you to access
your company’s resources from anywhere in the world.

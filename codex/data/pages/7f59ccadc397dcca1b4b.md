 Panorama > SD-WAN 

 Add firewalls, VPN clusters, and monitor firewall health
using the Panorama™ SD-WAN plugin. 

 Download and install the Panorama™ SD-WAN
plugin to centrally manage, monitor, and generate reports. Configure
the SD-WAN topology from Panorama by adding and associating branches
to the appropriate hubs and then associate those branch and hub
firewalls to the appropriate zones. After configuring your SD-WAN
topology, monitor the path health metrics across all configured
firewalls and paths to isolate application and link issues, as well
as to understand your link performance over time. Additionally,
you can generate reports for auditing purposes.

 Autonomous Playbooks 

 Close the loop from detection to response with fully managed, out-of-the-box workflows that proactively remediate threats. 

 This feature is enabled by default for all new tenants created on or after May 31st, 2026. If you would like to add this feature to an existing tenant, contact Customer Support.

 Configure AI agents to automatically investigate and resolve threats in real time, delivering autonomous, end-to-end analysis and remediation. Now available in private preview; contact Cortex Product Management to participate.

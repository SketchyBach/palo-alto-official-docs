 Escape
Sequences 

 Any field that contains a comma or a double-quote is
enclosed in double quotes. Furthermore, if a double-quote appears
inside a field it is escaped by preceding it with another double-quote.
To maintain backward compatibility, the Misc field in threat log
is always enclosed in double-quotes.

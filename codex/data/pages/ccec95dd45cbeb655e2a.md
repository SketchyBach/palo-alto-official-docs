 Reports 

 Create, edit, and customize reports in Cortex XDR. Schedule reports with Cron expressions. 

 Reports contain statistical data in the form of widgets, which enable you to analyze data from inside or outside Cortex XDR, in different formats such as graphs, pie charts, or text from information. After generating a report, it also appears in the Reports tab, so you can use the report again.

 Upgrade to Cortex XDR 5.x 

 Cortex XDR 5 delivers major advances for AI-driven security operations. It adds significant enhancements, a more intuitive experience, and updated terminology. Review the release notes to understand the changes before upgrading. 

 The upgrade follows the standard release process. Eligible tenant and account administrators can upgrade immediately or schedule a time. The tenant is temporarily unavailable during the upgrade. Manage or cancel a scheduled upgrade in Notification Center . 

 To learn more and view upgrade information, see What’s New in Cortex XDR 5 .

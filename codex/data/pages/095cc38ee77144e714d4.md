 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Prisma Access (Managed by Strata Cloud Manager) 

 Prisma Access (Managed by Panorama) 

 NGFW (Managed by Panorama) 

 iOS 16 and later 

 For the per-app connect method, you must attach your per-app VPN profile to the apps
 you want to access through Prisma Access Agent . 

 On the Microsoft Intune admin center, navigate to Apps iOS/iPadOS and then click the app you want to access through Prisma Access Agent . 

 Click Manage Properties . 

 Click Assignments Edit for the user group you want to associate with the per-app VPN
 profile. 

 Click VPN and select the profile. 

 Click OK and then click
 Save . 

 Click Review + Save .

 The Summary and Insights page organizes data into three persona-driven views so you can
 quickly find the information most relevant to your current role or task. Each view
 displays aggregated metrics across your tenant hierarchy, with the ability to filter by
 region and product scope. 

 Select Prisma Browser as the scope selection, then select the appropriate time
 range and region. 

 You can see more information on the dashboard on the Strata Multitenant Cloud Manager Summary and
 Insights page.

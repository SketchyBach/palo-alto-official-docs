 Reference I: Audit logging 

 All policy actions are recorded in the Management Audit Logs : 

 Action 

 Audit Log Entry 

 Create Policy 

 Records the policy name, type, and creating user 

 Edit Policy 

 Records the modified fields and the editing user 

 Delete Policy 

 Records the policy name and the deleting user 

 Enable/Disable Policy 

 Records the status change and the user who performed the action 

 Navigate to Settings → Configurations → Management → Audit Logs to review policy audit entries.

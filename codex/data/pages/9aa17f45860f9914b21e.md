 Unusual AWS S3 objects deletion

 Detection Modules 

 Cloud 

 ATT&CK Tactic 

 Impact (TA0040) 

 ATT&CK Technique 

 Inhibit System Recovery (T1490), Data Destruction (T1485) 

 Severity 

 Informational 

 Description 

 An identity deleted multiple S3 bucket objects from the project, considerably more than usual. 

 Attacker's Goals 

 Adversaries may delete data to prevent the recovery of a corrupted system. 

 They may also aim to interrupt availability to resources. 

 Investigative actions 

 Identify the deleted objects and their containing bucket. 

 Investigate the identity that performed the deletion and review recent related activity. 

 Variations

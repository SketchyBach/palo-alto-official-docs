End-of-life policy

This topic describes the end-of-life policy for Idira products.

Overview

As part of any product lifecycle, products will eventually reach their end of life, mainly due to changes in the marketplace, outdated code and technology, and adoption of new and improved technologies. The purpose of this policy is to assist customers in making informed purchase, support and upgrade decisions, by outlining the end-of-life periods for Idira's products.

Idira may update this policy from time to time.

Scope

This policy applies to Idira's self-hosted products, as well as Idira's locally installed software components (including agents and connectors) that are provided with Idira's SaaS products.

Support services

Idira’s support services are provided according to Idira's support terms and are subject to payment of applicable fees.

Idira regularly releases new product versions with new features and security fixes. While Idira supports its product versions as set forth in this policy, we recommend upgrading to the latest version/patch release when it's available, to benefit from improved security and stability.

Idira doesn't support platforms, browsers, and systems that are no longer supported by their respective vendor.

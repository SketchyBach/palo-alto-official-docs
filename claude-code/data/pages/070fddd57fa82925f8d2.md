 Prioritize incidents with starring and scoring 

 Prioritize and filter your incidents by using incident starring and incident scoring. 

 Cortex XDR provides incident starring and incident scoring to help you to prioritize your incidents. 

 Incident starring enables you to highlight and filter the incidents that you deem most important. Incident scoring assigns a numerical value to each incident to reflect its severity. Using these functions, you can easily compare incident scores, filter starred incidents, and prioritize your most urgent incidents. 

 Notice 

 Incident scoring is available in Cortex XDR Pro only.

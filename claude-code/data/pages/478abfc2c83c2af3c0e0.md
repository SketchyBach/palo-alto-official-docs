 INC_ZTNA_CONNECTOR_CPU_HIGH 

 Learn about the INC_ZTNA_CONNECTOR_CPU_HIGH incident. 

 Synopsis 

 The incident is raised when the average CPU utilization of the Zero Trust
 Network Access (ZTNA) connector over 30 minutes exceeds 90%. The incident is cleared
 when the ZTNA connector average CPU utilization over 30 minutes is less than
 85%. 

 Incident Code—INC_ZTNA_CONNECTOR_CPU_HIGH 

 Severity—Critical 

 For details about incident severity, see Incidents Distribution Over
 Time in Incidents and Alerts Overview .

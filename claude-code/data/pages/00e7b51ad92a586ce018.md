 PA-3500 Series Firewall Overview 

 Get an overview of the PA-3500 Series Firewall. 

 The PA-3500 Series Next-Generation firewalls are high performance appliances designed for
 large branch, enterprise, and data center environments. The following models make up the
 PA-3500 Series: 

 PA-3510 

 PA-3520 

 PA-3530 

 PA-3540 

 The PA-3500 Series firewalls provide flexibility in performance and redundancy to adapt
 to your deployment requirements. These models can use either AC or DC power and come
 with a 960GB SSD for local storage. Dedicated computing and hardware resources ensure
 predictable performance in networking, zero trust security, threat performance, and
 management functions. These firewalls also feature a Trusted Platform Module (TPM) 2.0
 for PAN-OS key storage and security. 

 First Supported PAN-OS® Software Release: PAN-OS 12.2.2 

 The PA-3500 Series Firewall Components topic describes the hardware components of the PA-3500 Series
 firewalls.

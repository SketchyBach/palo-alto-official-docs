 Toggling a Built-in Account On or Off 

 You might need to disable a built-in account temporarily. When you disable an account, you prevent access to the machine you created the built-in account for; of course, this does not prevent user account access. To re-enable access, use the same toggle button. 

 When you disable (or delete) a built-in account, all of its access tokens are invalidated. When you re-enable it again, they are restored. 

 To Toggle a Built-in Account On or Off 

 Sign in to Next-Gen Trust Security. 

 Click System Settings > Certificate Settings > Built-in Accounts . 

 Find the built-in account you want to disable or enable. 

 Click 

 or 

 to toggle the built-in account off or on.

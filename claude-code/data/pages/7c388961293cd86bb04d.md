 Tenant View 

 Use Common Services: Activation and Subscription
 Management to activate applications, claim subscriptions, create a tenant
 hierarchy, and assign product licenses to individual tenants in this hierarchy. Since
 the products in the tenant view uses subscription model, the products are deactivated
 upon subscription expiry. You can track and manage the subscriptions using Common Services: Subscription Management

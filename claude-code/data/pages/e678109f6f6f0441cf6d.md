 Export an incident as a PDF 

 Export incident details as a PDF report from the Incidents page. 

 You can export the information for an incident in the form of a PDF report. The most important details about the incident will be included in the report. 

 Navigate to Incident Response → Incidents and click on the incident in the incident list. The incident details will display in the pane on the right. 

 At the top right of the details pane, click the Export button to generate the PDF report.

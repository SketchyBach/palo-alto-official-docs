 Wall Mount the ION 2000 

 Wall mounting the Prisma SD-WAN ION 2000 is optional,
learn how to wall mount the device using the given kit. 

 Mount the Prisma SD-WAN ION 2000 on the wall
using the optional wall mounting kit. 

 Gather the wall mounting brackets. 

 Turn the ION device upside down and locate the four (4)
screw holes on each corner of the device. 

 Place the brackets on each side of the device, making
sure to align the screw holes on the bracket and the device. 

 Insert the screws to secure the brackets. 

 Place the device on the wall where it will be mounted.
Mark the wall with circles for each screw hole so it can be used
later to insert the wall anchors. 

 With a power drill, apply the four (4) white wall anchors
into the screw holes. 

 Insert the threaded nails partially into the anchors,
leaving a tiny gap to hang the wall mounting bracket. 

 Hang the device onto the wall, making sure to match the
four (4) screw holes on the bracket with the four (4) threaded nails
on the wall. 

 Tighten the screws to secure the device on the wall. 

 Finally, verify that the device is securely mounted on
the wall.

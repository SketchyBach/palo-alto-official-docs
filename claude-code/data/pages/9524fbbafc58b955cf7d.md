 What's New in May 2023 

 FedRAMP Moderate Phase 2 

 FedRAMP Moderate requirements are security controls and
 well-established standards for cloud solutions intended for Cloud Service
 Providers managing and processing government data. Many government agencies
 mandate the FedRAMP Moderate authorization. Palo Alto Networks products and
 services are FedRAMP Moderate Authorized to increase security, reliability,
 consistency, monitoring, and thereby gaining the trust and confidence of Federal
 agencies. 

 To ensure FedRAMP Moderate compliance, Prisma SASE FedRAMP Moderate adds support for additional Prisma SASE apps, add-ons, and
 certain features. 

 FedRAMP High Enhancement 

 FedRAMP High adds additional support for Prisma SD-WAN standalone and add-on. 

 What's New in December 2023 

 FedRAMP High 

 FedRAMP High requirements are security controls and well-established
 standards for cloud solutions intended for Cloud Service Providers managing and
 processing high-impact government data. Many government agencies mandate the
 FedRAMP High authorization. Palo Alto Networks products and services are FedRAMP
 High Authorized to increase security, reliability, consistency, monitoring, and
 thereby gaining the trust and confidence of Federal agencies. 

 To ensure FedRAMP High compliance, Prisma SASE FedRAMP High introduces support for Prisma SASE apps, add-ons, and
 features.

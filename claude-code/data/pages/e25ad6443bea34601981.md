 Strata Logging Service is a cloud-delivered, scalable, and secure log storage service that enables you to ingest, store, and forward logs from your Palo Alto Networks products and services, including Prisma Access, your hardware and software NGFWs, Cloud NGFW for Azure, and Cloud NGFW for AWS. With Strata Logging Service, you can collect ever-expanding volumes of data without needing to plan for local compute and storage, and it's ready to scale from the start.

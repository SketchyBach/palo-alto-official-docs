 INC_RN_SPN_LONG_DURATION_EXCEEDED_ CAPACITY 

 Learn about the INC_RN_SPN_LONG_DURATION_EXCEEDED_CAPACITY incident. 

 Synopsis 

 SPN capacity exceeded 100% usage for 3 days in the last 5 days for SPN <spn-name>
 <ingress | egress> traffic 

 Incident Code—INC_RN_SPN_LONG_DURATION_EXCEEDED_CAPACITY 

 Severity—Critical 

 For details about incident severity, see Incidents Distribution Over
 Time in Incidents and Alerts Overview . 

 Required License 

 Prisma Access 

 Details 

 Raise condition 

 SPN capacity exceeded 100% usage for at least 3 of the last 5
 days for at least 1 hour a day in the given direction. 

 Clear condition 

 SPN capacity did not exceed 90% usage in the last 8 days for at
 least 1 hour a day in the given direction.

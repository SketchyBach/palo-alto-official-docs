 Panorama > SD-WAN 

 Add devices, VPN clusters, and monitor device health
using the Panorama SD-WAN plugin. 

 Download and install the Panorama SD-WAN
plugin to centrally manage, monitor, and generate reports. Configure
the SD-WAN topology from Panorama by adding and associating branches
to their appropriate hubs, and associate those branch and hub devices
to the appropriate zones. After configuring your SD-WAN topology,
you can monitor the path health metrics across all configured devices
and paths to isolate application and link issues, as well as understand
your link performance over time. Additionally, you can generate
reports for auditing purposes.

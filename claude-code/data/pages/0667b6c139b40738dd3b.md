 Rack Mount the Slide Rails 

 Learn how to rack mount the slide rails of the Prisma
SD-WAN ION 7000. 

 Fasten the 19 inch 1U rails securely to the
front and back of the rack mount enclosure. 

 Orient the front bracket of both rails in the
same direction. 

 Mount the rails securely and then insert the ION 7000
into the front of the sliding rails.

 Widgets panel 

 The widget panel provides a visual summary of cloud workload policies. 

 Use the panel to view policy information by policy type or evaluation stage. You can show or hide the panel as needed. 

 Show or hide the widget panel 

 To hide the widget panel, do the following: 

 Navigate to Posture Management → Rules & Policies → Policies → Cloud Workload . 

 On the Cloud Workload Policies page, click the Widget Panel icon at the top of the page. 

 The panel toggles between visible and hidden states.

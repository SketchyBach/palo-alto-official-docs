 Cortex XSOAR 8 (SaaS) 

 Export incidents 

 Export Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS incidents. 

 You can export incidents by doing the following: 

 Manually export one or more incidents from the Incidents table to an Excel or CSV file. For more information, see Export incidents from the Incidents table . 

 Export incidents on demand to external cloud storage. For more information, see Export incidents on demand . 

 Export incidents on a scheduled basis from Cortex XSOAR to external storage. For more information about how to schedule incident export, see Schedule incident export . 

 Incidents are exported based on your retention policy, before retention is enforced. Your retention policy for Cortex XSOAR incidents is 6 months.

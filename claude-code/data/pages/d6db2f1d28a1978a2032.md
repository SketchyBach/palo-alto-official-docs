 Get Started with the Activation Console 

 Use the Activation Console to activate, access, and also manage Palo Alto Networks
 apps and services. 

 Important : We are rolling out this new, unified activation experience in
 stages. For supported SASE products, you may see the new activation console now, or
 you maybe directed to use the Hub for activation until the update reaches your
 tenant. 

 The Activation Console is where you activate and access your Palo Alto
Networks services and apps. Many Palo Alto Networks apps and services
access, analyze, and report on data stored in Cortex Data Lake. 

 Access the Activation Console here: apps.paloaltonetworks.com 

 Before you can access an app, you must activate it from the Activation Console .
You can also perform some application configuration using the Activation Console 
and capture debug information about an app instance if you experience
issues with it.

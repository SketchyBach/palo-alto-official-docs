 PA-800 Back-Panel 

 The following images show the back panel
of the PA-820 and PA-850 Series firewall and the table describes
each back panel component. The only difference between the back
panels of the two firewalls is that the PA-820 has one fixed power
supply and the PA-850 firewall has two hot-swappable power supplies
(the second power supply is for redundancy). 
 PA-820 Back Panel 

 PA-850 Back Panel 

 Item 

 Component 

 Description 

 1 

 Power inputs 

 Use the power supply input(s) to connect
power to the firewall. 

 PA-820 firewall—Single fixed
AC power supply and power input. 

 PA-850 firewall—Two AC power supplies and power inputs. 

 2 

 Ground stud 

 Use the single post ground stud to connect
the firewall to earth ground (ground cable not included). 

 3 

 Cooling fans 

 Fans that provide ventilation and cooling
for the firewall.

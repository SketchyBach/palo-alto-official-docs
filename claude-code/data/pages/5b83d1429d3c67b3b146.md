 Convert Your Production Panorama to an ELA Panorama 

 Convert your production Panorama™ virtual appliance to
an ELA Panorama virtual appliance. 

 You can convert your production Panorama™
virtual appliance to continue leveraging your Panorama with the
benefits of ELA licensing. To convert your production deployment,
Panorama must have out-bound Internet access. 

 Converting
your production Panorama to ELA licensing is supported in Management Only
and Panorama mode with or without a local Log Collector configured.
If your Panorama has a local Log Collector configured, you must
submit a support ticket with Palo Alto Networks to convert your
Panorama to ELA licensing. 

 During conversion
from a production Panorama to ELA licensing, do not change the Panorama
serial number if a local Log Collector is configured. 

 The
log on the local Log collector become inaccessible and other Log
Collectors in the Collector Group may become inaccessible and no
longer ingest logs if the serial number of a Log Collector is changed. 

 Covert your Panorama to ELA licensing. 

 Panorama virtual appliance in Panorama mode
with a local Log Collector. 

 Submit support ticket with Palo Alto
Networks to convert your Panorama to ELA licensing. This
is required in order to preserve all existing logs on the local
Log Collector when converting a Panorama with a local Log Collector
to ELA licensing. An example is provided below to assist in filing
the support ticket. Create the ticket exactly as displayed below,
and select the OS Release your Panorama is
running. 

 Continue to the next step only after Palo Alto Networks
support successfully resolves your support ticket. 

 Panorama virtual appliance in Management Only mode or
Panorama mode with no local Log Collector. 

 Generate a serial number from your ELA licensing
pool. 

 Log in to the Palo Alto Networks CSP . 

 Select Assets VM-Series
Auth-Codes and locate your ELA licensing
pool. 

 In the Actions column, select Panorama and Provision a
new serial number. 

 Confirm the new serial number provision
when prompted. 

 Copy the newly provisioned serial number. 

 Log in to the Panorama
web interface . 

 Select Panorama Setup Management and
edit the General Settings. 

 Enter the Serial Number you
provisioned. 

 Click OK . 

 Select Commit and Commit
to Panorama . 

 Log in to the Panorama
web interface if not already logged in. 

 Select Panorama Licenses and Retrieve
new licenses from the license server . 

 Verify that Panorama retrieved the new licenses as per
your ELA agreement. 

 Verify that the support and device management licenses
are successfully activated. 

 Select Panorama Licenses and verify that the
correct licenses are activated. 

 Select Panorama Support and verify that the
correct support Level and Expiry
Date are displayed.

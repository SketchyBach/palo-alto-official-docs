 Reports: Activity Insights - Summary 

 Share reports within your organization, and schedule reports so that they’re delivered to
 your email inbox—and your colleagues inboxes—at regular intervals (daily, weekly, or
 monthly). 

 Activity Insights Summary report shows the summary of your network data such as
 network traffic, application usage, threats, and user activities to help
 administrator understand network's health and security posture. Activity Insights -
 Summary report has advanced filters to help you focus on the security aspects that
 matter to your deployment. 

 Use the report to: 
 Identify if specific high-risk apps or vulnerabilities are repeatedly
 targeting your network. 

 Review the most matched rule to ensure your policies are working as
 intended. 

 Investigate bandwidth issues. Review the applications that are consuming
 the highest amount of bandwidth on a network over a specific period. 

 Review the threats section to identify the most impacted users and take
 preventive actions. 
 For detailed information on the report, refer to Activity Insights: Overview .

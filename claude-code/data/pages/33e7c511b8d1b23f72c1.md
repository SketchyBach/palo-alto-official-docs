 The Access Experience user interface displays detailed
 information about performance issues that may be impacting your applications. It also
 offers recommendations for resolving the underlying performance issues. 

 When you open Access Experience , it displays
 information that is specific to your notification. When you open it directly, it will
 display the current state of your Device, WiFi, and Internet connectivity. 

 The notifications get categorized by their source. 

 Device 

 CPU usage notifications 

 Memory usage notifications 

 WiFi 

 Poor WiFi quality 

 Disconnected WiFi 

 WiFi Network Switching 

 Internet 

 Disconnected internet 

 Click Device , WiFi , or
 Internet to display the current performance of that
 segment. 

 Click an endpoint to view the metrics for the past hours. For
 example, click Device to view the CPU and memory usage metrics
 from the last few hours. You can also view the list of notification alerts received for
 your managed device across endpoints. 

 The usage metrics chart shows the following: 

 The x-axis shows the timestamp for the last few hours. Depending on where you
 mouse over and click in the chart, you can view the status that was indicated at
 that specific time. Click anywhere within the 

 The trend lines in the chart may display one or more red dots, which indicate
 that a notification was sent to 
 you at that moment in time where the dot appears.

 The usage metrics chart may show gaps if the device was offline or disconnected
 during that time. Metrics are only collected when the device is active and
 connected.

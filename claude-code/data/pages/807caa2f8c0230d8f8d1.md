 Replace an M-300 or M-700 Drive 

 Learn how to replace a system drive and log drive on
an M-300 or M-700 appliance. 

 M-300 and M-700 appliances store the Panorama™ system
files and system logs on a single solid-state drive (SSD) and the
logs collected from Palo Alto Networks® firewalls are stored on
hard-disk drives (HDDs). The HDD log drives are in RAID 1 arrays
so that if a drive fails, you can replace the failed drive without service
interruption.

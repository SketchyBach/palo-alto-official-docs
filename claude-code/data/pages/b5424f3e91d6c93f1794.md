 Notification 

 When your device running out of memory
or CPU processing power, you receive a notification on your screen.
By default, a notification is raised when your device is using 95%
or more of its available CPU or memory. This threshold is set by
your IT administrator. The notification tells you what the issue
is and also suggests ways to remediate it.

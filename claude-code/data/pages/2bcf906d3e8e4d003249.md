 Delete an Identity Federation Through Common Services 

 Learn how to delete an identity federation through the Common Services . 

 After you add an identity federation ,
you can delete the identity federation. 

 Use one of the various ways to access 
 Common Services Identity & Access . 

 Select Identity & Access . Only one way is shown
 here. 

 Select Identity & Access/Access
Management Identity Federations Add Identity Federation to
add an identity federation. 

 Go to your domain provider’s console and delete the owner.
Otherwise the txt record is still being verified from when you originally added an identity
federation , so the deactivation request will be canceled. 

 In the , scroll to your identity
federation of choice and Deactivate it. 

 Delete the identity federation.

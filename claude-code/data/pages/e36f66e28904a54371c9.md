 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Prisma Access Agent 

 Android 10 and later versions 

 Internet access 

 To uninstall the Prisma Access Agent from the unmanaged devices: 

 Find the Prisma Access Agent app icon in your app drawer
 or on your home screen. 

 Tap and hold the Prisma Access Agent app icon. 

 Drag it to an Uninstall that appears on the screen, or
 select Uninstall from the context menu that pops up. 

 Confirm the uninstallation. 

 When you uninstall the app, the VPN profile is also removed. 

 If your device is managed by an MDM, the ability to uninstall the app might
 be restricted by your administrator. 

 Alternatively: 

 Launch the Settings app. 

 Navigate to Apps . 

 Find and select the Prisma Access Agent app from
 the list. 

 Tap Uninstall . 

 Confirm the uninstallation.

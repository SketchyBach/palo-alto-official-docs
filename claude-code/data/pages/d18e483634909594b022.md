Find Idira Identity documentation in spaces

Idira documentation is evolving alongside the platform experience.

As part of the move to a unified platform, documentation is now organized by spaces, based on workflows and outcomes rather than services.

If you’re used to working with Idira Identity documentation, the content has moved to new locations in the spaces structure.

Use this topic to quickly find where Idira Identity content now resides.

You can still access the documentation in the previous structure for Identity and Identity Administration. The previous docs reflect the structure and content as of April 2026 and are no longer updated.

About spaces documentation

In the new structure, documentation is organized by spaces, not services.

Each space brings together related concepts, tasks, and reference material based on what you’re trying to accomplish.

Content that was previously part of Idira Identity may now:

Appear across multiple spaces

Be combined with related content from other services

This structure helps you find information based on your workflow rather than where it originated.

 Walk a MIB 

 If you want to see which SNMP objects (system statistics
and traps) are available for monitoring, displaying all the objects
of a particular MIB can be useful. To do this, load the Supported MIBs into your SNMP manager and perform a walk on the
desired MIB. To list the traps that Palo Alto Networks firewalls,
Panorama, and WF-500 appliance support, walk the panCommonEventEventsV2

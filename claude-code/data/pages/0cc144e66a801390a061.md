 WiFi Notifications 

 Receive Autonomous DEM (ADEM) WiFi notifications when your connection disconnects, drops
 below quality thresholds, or switches networks.

 Enable Self Serve
 Notification 

 You device sends you a notification when
your WiFi gets disconnected or the WiFi connection quality falls
below the threshold set by your IT administrator. By default, the
threshold is set to 48%. If your WiFi connection quality is at or
below 48%, it indicates that you have poor WiFi connection quality. 

 You also get notified if your device switches over to a poorer
quality WiFi in your vicinity.

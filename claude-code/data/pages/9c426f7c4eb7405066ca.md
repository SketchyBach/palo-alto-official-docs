 PAN-1RU-4POST-RACK-16 

 Install a PA-520-5G firewall in a four-post equipment rack using the
 PAN-1RU-4POST-RACK-16 mounting kit. 

 The PA-520-5G firewall can be mounted in a 4-post equipment rack using the
 PAN-1RU-4POST-RACK-16. 

 Parts List – PAN-1RU-4POST-RACK-16 

 Quantity Item 

 1 4-post rack mount tray 

 1 Fixed rail 

 1 Adjustable rail 

 2 Phillips pan head screw — M4 × 8mm 

 30 Torx flat head screw (T10) — M3 × 5mm 

 16 Adhesive cable tie mount 

 28 Locking cable tie 

 4 Power cord retainer 

 8 Velcro strap 

 4 RF cable assembly — R/A SMA to N-type, 450mm L 

 4 RF cable assembly — ST SMA to N-type, IP67, 93mm L 

 2 Dual LTE/5G cable mount bracket 

 4 Single LTE/5G cable mount bracket 

 1 Power cord routing cover, right 

 1 Power cord routing cover, left 

 Verify that the equipment rack has enough space to accommodate the firewall and
 has 1RU of space both above and below it. 

 Align the four holes on the bottom of the firewall with the designated location
 in the mounting tray. Seat the firewall and use four M3 Torx flat head screws to
 secure it in place. 

 Place up to two power adapters in the designated locations on the mounting tray.
 Loop the provided Velcro straps through the cutouts in the mounting tray, using
 two straps per power adapter. 

 Connect your cables and antenna connectors to the device and route them through
 the mounting tray in order to minimize slack. 

 A total of six antenna cable brackets are included — four single cable
 brackets and two dual cable brackets. Install each bracket using two M3
 screws through the holes in the mounting tray. After guiding the antenna
 cables through the brackets, secure each cable in place using the
 included lock washer and nut. 

 Wrap cable ties through the small cutouts in the tray to secure the cables in
 place. 

 Install the two provided power cord routing covers on the right and left sides
 of the tray. Use two M3 screws for each side (four total). 

 Apply cable retention clamps over the power cords by slotting them into the
 tray. 

 Slide the adjustable rail into the fixed rail to create a mounting rail. Repeat
 for the second mounting rail. The adjustable and fixed rails are the same for the
 left and right side. 

 Align the bottom edge of the mounting rails to the bottom of the space reserved
 for your firewall. Align the slotted holes in the adjustable rail to the holes on
 the rear of the equipment frame. 

 Secure the rails to the equipment frame with mounting screws (not provided)
 compatible with your equipment frame. Tighten the screws to their recommended
 torque value. 

 Slide the firewall (seated securely in the mounting tray) into the equipment
 rack. 

 Make sure that you do
 not block any of the airflow vents on the firewall. 

 Secure the mounting tray to the equipment rack using compatible screws. 

 After installation, you
 should inspect the hardware on a regular basis in order to account for dust,
 debris, and any other obstructions that may affect the firewall's
 airflow. 

 Do not place any items or
 obstructions directly above or below the firewall within the 1RU of free
 space.

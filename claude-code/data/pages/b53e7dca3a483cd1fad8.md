 Discover new features introduced in PAN-OS 11.2.

 PAN-OS® 11.2 leverages our artificial intelligence (AI) and deep learning techniques. 

 Review information about the new features, including Advanced DNS Security,
 Local deep learning for Advanced Threat Protection (ATP), Quantum Security, Virtual System
 Support on VM-Series firewall, CN-Series support for ATP and User-ID, and more.

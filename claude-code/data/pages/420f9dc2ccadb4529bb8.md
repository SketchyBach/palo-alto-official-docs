Idira's CORA AI

By integrating AI into the Idira Identity Security Platform, Idira offers a friendlier and easier onboarding and work experience. AI-based tools help your administrators navigate the setup and ongoing maintenance of your system. AI is also integrated into the main functionality of the system, to give you valuable insights into your operational settings and to offer helpful recommendations for your setup or your next steps. AI-based assistance helps you deploy Idira solutions and improve system efficiency.

Idira CORA AI assistant

	

Enables you to engage with Idira's services through natural language to post queries, gain important information about the system, run commands, and easily set up and maintain your system.

This helps you to quickly gain confidence in performing your setup and administrative tasks and subsequently update and maintain your system.

For more information, see Idira's CORA AI assistant.




CORA AI - Functional insights and recommendations

	

Idira CORA AI capabilities are based on operational experience and know-how that Idira has amassed over many years in managing identity security systems.

CORA AI is integrated into the key functions for securing user identities, securing access, and discovering accounts. These features help you quickly find important accounts or remove those that are no longer needed.

As Idira's CORA AI engines evolve, you can rely on their insights and support to make your tasks easier to accomplish.

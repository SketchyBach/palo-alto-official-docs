 Allocate ION Devices for an Existing TSG-Enabled Tenant 

 Allocate ION devices and subscriptions to an existing Prisma SD-WAN tenant that has been migrated to a Tenant Service Group (TSG). 

 Where Can I Use
 This? What Do I Need? 

 Prisma SD-WAN (Managed by Strata Cloud Manager ) 

 Prisma SD-WAN 

 Superuser or IAM Administrator role 

 TSG-enabled tenant 

 If you are an existing Prisma® SD-WAN customer with a TSG-enabled
 tenant, use the following steps to allocate ION devices and subscriptions from a
 recent purchase to your tenant. ION devices and subscriptions purchased separately
 do not appear in your tenant automatically — this allocation process links them to
 your tenant so you can begin managing and configuring them. 

 After Palo Alto Networks fulfills your order, the admin associated with the purchase
 receives an activation email that includes a magic link — a one-time URL that
 launches the guided allocation flow. 

 Depending on how your purchase order was processed, you may
 receive more than one magic link. Repeat these steps for each link you receive. Do
 not use the same magic link twice, as each link activates a specific set of devices
 or subscriptions. 

 If you do not know your TSG ID, see Find Your Tenant Service Group (TSG) ID 
 before you begin Step 4. 

 Locate the activation email from Palo Alto Networks and click
 Activate . 

 If you did not receive the activation email, check your spam folder. If
 the email is not there, contact Palo Alto Networks Support to resend
 it. 

 Enter the email address associated with your Customer Support Portal account
 and click Sign In . 

 On the Activate Subscription page, choose a
 Customer Support Account for Prisma SD-WAN . 

 For organizations with multi-tenant hierarchies,
 Palo Alto Networks recommends using the same Customer Support Portal account
 for all Prisma SD-WAN assets to avoid visibility and
 management inconsistencies across tenants. 

 Choose the TSG ID to which you want to allocate your devices and
 subscriptions. 

 A list of TSG IDs associated with your Customer Support Portal account
 appears on this page. 

 For organizations with a multi-tenant hierarchy,
 Palo Alto Networks recommends allocating devices and subscriptions to the
 root tenant, then using the Prisma SD-WAN device manager to
 assign devices to sub-tenants. Allocating directly to a sub-tenant can
 cause visibility and management issues at the root level, including
 incomplete device counts and incomplete policy inheritance. 

 Agree to the Terms and Conditions . 

 Click Activate Now . 

 Region selection applies only to new tenant activations. 

 For existing tenants, the region defaults to the region where you
 originally activated the tenant. 

 After activation, all allocated device serial numbers appear in your tenant.
 It may take 15–60 minutes for all devices to appear in the web interface.
 If devices do not appear after 60 minutes, contact Palo Alto Networks
 Support. 

 Select Configuration Prisma SD-WAN ION Devices and click the Unclaimed Devices 
 tab. 

 The Unclaimed Devices tab lists all devices allocated to your tenant. 

 It may take 15–60 minutes for all devices to appear in
 the web interface. If devices do not appear after 60 minutes, contact Palo
 Alto Networks Support. 

 Find Your Tenant Service Group (TSG) ID 

 A Tenant Service Group (TSG) is a
 logical container that groups tenants and other TSGs within the Prisma SASE
 platform. At the leaf level of a tenant hierarchy, a tenant and a TSG share the
 same technical structure, so the terms are sometimes used interchangeably in the
 platform interface. 

 After your Prisma SD-WAN instance is migrated to a Tenant Service
 Group, you have access to Common Services for subscription
 management, tenant management, and identity and access management. Use the
 following steps to locate your TSG ID. You will need it in Step 4 of the device
 allocation process. 

 Log in to the hub . 

 Select Common Services Tenant Management . 

 If you are using the View by
 Support Account view and cannot locate your Prisma SD-WAN application, your tenant is
 TSG-migrated. Select Common Services Tenant Management to locate your TSG ID. 

 Select a tenant and note the TSG ID displayed in
 the upper-right corner of the page. 

 Copy or note this TSG ID. You need it in Step 4 of the device
 allocation process to select the correct allocation target.

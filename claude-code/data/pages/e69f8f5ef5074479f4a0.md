 Description 

 A cloud identity listed available AI models. This behavior often suggests reconnaissance on AI models and potential misuse. MITRE ATLAS Technique: AML.T0007 - Discover ML Artifacts. 

 Attacker's Goals 

 Gather information about AI models in the environment. 

 Investigative actions 

 Determine which AI models were enumerated. 

 Monitor the usage of affected AI models to detect potential misuse, such as unusual or excessive access attempts. 

 Investigate any unusual activity originating from the suspected identity.

Unmaintained Item

Severity

🟢 Low (3)

Short Description

Flags items that are not maintained on the marketplace, suggesting concerns about the item's reputation and the publisher's reliability.

Suggestion

Replace unmaintained items with actively supported alternatives to reduce risks.

Information

Unmaintained items no longer receive updates or security patches, increasing the risk of vulnerabilities and compatibility issues.

Risks of Unmaintained Item

Security Vulnerabilities: Unmaintained items do not receive critical updates.

Compatibility Issues: These items may not function well with new platform versions.

Loss of Trust: Unmaintained items indicate a lack of ongoing investment by the publisher.

Recommended Actions

Investigate the Item:

Review Usage: Assess whether the item is essential to your workflow.

Search for Alternatives: Look for actively maintained items offering similar functionality.

Immediate Action:

Replace: Replace the item with a better-supported alternative.

Remove If Risky: If no alternatives exist and the item poses risks, remove it.

 Renew Signing Keys 

 Renew a Signing Key if it is nearing expiration, has expired, or requires updated properties. 

 To Renew a Signing Key 

 Sign in to Next-Gen Trust Security. 

 Click Insights > Certificate Inventory > Signing Keys . 

 Select the Signing Key to open its details. 

 To renew the Signing Key without changing properties, select Renew . 

 To change properties before renewal: 

 Select the Renewal Properties tab. 

 Update any necessary fields, and then select Save . 

 Select Renew . 

 After the Signing Key is renewed, its updated properties and renewal date appear in the details drawer. On the Cryptographic Objects tab, you can view the previous certificate and key pair settings by clicking the Current drop-down menu and selecting a past object.

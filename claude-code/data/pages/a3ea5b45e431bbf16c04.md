 Assess Your Compliance Posture in SSPM 

 Release Date: May 2024 
 | 
 Last Updated: May 2026 

 SaaS Security Posture Management now includes a Compliance dashboard , which summarizes policy violations for common compliance standards and frameworks. From the dashboard, you can navigate to compliance reports for each of the compliance standards and frameworks. The compliance reports show the non-compliant settings by SaaS application. From there, you can drill down further to view the settings that passed and failed, and remediate settings as needed.

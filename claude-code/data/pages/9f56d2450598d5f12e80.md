 Upgrade your SD-WAN Firewalls 

 Upgrade your Panorama , SD-WAN hub, and SD-WAN 
 branch firewalls. 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 NGFW 

 Advanced SD-WAN for NGFW 

 Upgrading your SD-WAN firewalls
 is essential to ensure optimal performance, security, and functionality of your
 network infrastructure. By keeping your
 Panorama 
 and managed SD-WAN firewalls
 up to date, you gain access to the latest features, bug fixes, and security
 enhancements. 

 Review the Panorama plugin for SD-WAN release notes 
 and then use the following procedure to upgrade your
 Panorama and managed SD-WAN firewalls. 

 PAN-OS 10.0.0 and 10.0.1 support SD-WAN plugin 1.0.3
 and later releases. If your SD-WAN firewalls are running PAN-OS 10.0.2 or later releases, upgrade the
 SD-WAN plugin
 to SD-WAN plugin 2.0. 

 Install Content and Software Updates for
 Panorama . 

 Upgrade your managed Log Collectors. 

 You can upgrade the log collectors when Panorama is either connected
 or not connected to the internet. Using Panorama , you can securely
 manage and deploy updates to your log collectors without compromising your
 network's security posture. By keeping the log collectors up to date, you
 can ensure that your log collectors receive the latest security
 enhancements, bug fixes, and content updates while maintaining control over
 the update process. 

 Upgrade Log Collectors when
 Panorama is Internet-Connected . 

 Upgrade Log Collectors when
 Panorama is not Internet-Connected . 

 Upgrade your SD-WAN hub firewalls. 

 You must upgrade your hub firewalls from PAN-OS 10.0.0 to
 PAN-OS 10.0.1 or later release before you upgrade your
 branch firewalls. Upgrading branch firewalls before hub firewalls may
 result in incorrect monitoring data ( Panorama SD-WAN Monitoring ) and for SD-WAN links to erroneously
 display as down . 

 Upgrade firewalls when Panorama
 is connected to the internet . 

 Upgrade firewalls when Panorama
 is not connected to the internet . 

 Upgrade your SD-WAN branch firewalls. 

 Upgrade firewalls when Panorama
 is connected to the internet . 

 Upgrade firewalls when Panorama
 is not connected to the internet .

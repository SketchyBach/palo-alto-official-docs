 Install the PA-50R Series Firewall on a Wall 

 Learn how to mount the PA-50R Series firewall on a wall. 

 The PA-52R-5G, PA-54R-POE, and PA-54R-POE-D-5G, and PA-56R-POE-5G can be installed on
 a wall. The following procedure describes how to attach the wall mount bracket to
 the device and install it on a wall. 

 The wall mount bracket is required for this installation and
 must be purchased separately. 
 PA-52R-5G —PAN-PA-52R-WALL-MOUNT 

 PA-54R-POE and PA-54R-POE-D-5G — PAN-PA-54R-WALL-MNT 

 PA-56R-POE-5G —PAN-PA-56R-WALL-MNT 

 After installing, make sure to keep the device
 clean and clear of dust to ensure optimal heat dissipation and maintain proper
 hardware operation.

 PA-54R-POE and
 PA-54R-POE-D-5G 

 PA-56R-POE-5G 

 PA-52R-5G 

 Learn how to mount the PA-50R Series firewall on a wall. 

 The following table lists the items included in the PAN-PA-52R-WALL-MOUNT kit. 

 Parts List – PAN-PA-52R-WALL-MOUNT 

 Quantity Item 

 1 Wall mount bracket 

 4 Phillips pan head screw — M4 × 8mm, stainless steel 

 4 Torx head screw (T10) — M3 × 5mm, zinc-plated steel, nylon
 patch 

 2 Desiccant 

 Mark the locations on the wall that line up with the four wall mount holes on
 the bottom of the wall mount bracket. 

 Ensure there are no building services (water, gas, or wiring) behind the
 wall where you intend to install the firewall. 

 Drill holes into the four marked locations on the wall. Make sure to remove any
 loose debris around the holes. 

 Attach the firewall to the wall mount. 

 Identify the four screw holes on the bottom panel of the
 PA-52R-5G. 

 Align the wall mount bracket with the four screw holes. 

 Secure the wall mount bracket to the firewall using the four provided
 T10 Torx M3 x 0.5 screws. 

 Using four M4 Pan head screws screws (two for each side), install the wall
 mount bracket to the previously drilled holes in the wall. 

 Ensure that the firewall is oriented in such a way
 that its front and side panel components are accessible. 

 PA-54R-POE and PA-54R-POE-D-5G 

 Learn how to mount the PA-54R-POE and PA-54R-POE-D-5G firewalls on a
 wall. 

 The following table lists the items included in the PAN-PA-54R-WALL-MNT kit. 

 Parts List – PAN-PA-54R-WALL-MNT 

 Quantity Item 

 1 Wall mount bracket 

 4 Phillips pan head screw with plastic anchor — #6-20 Type AB,
 1.25" L, zinc-plated steel 

 2 Desiccant 

 Mark the locations on the wall that line up with the four wall mount holes on
 the bottom of the wall mount bracket. 

 Ensure there are no building services (water, gas, or wiring) behind the
 wall where you intend to install the firewall. 

 Drill holes into the four marked locations on the wall. Make sure to remove any
 loose debris around the holes. 

 Install the four provided wall anchors into the four holes. 

 Remove the DIN clip from the back panel of the firewall by unscrewing the four
 Flathead screws. 

 Attach the wall mount bracket onto the back panel by using four M4
 screws. 

 Secure the wall mount bracket using four additional M4 screws in the corner
 holes. 

 Using four #6-20 screws, install the wall mount bracket to the anchors in the
 wall. 

 PA-56R-POE-5G

 The following table lists the items included in the PAN-PA-56R-WALL-MNT kit. 

 Parts List – PAN-PA-56R-WALL-MNT 

 Quantity Item 

 1 Wall mount bracket 

 4 Phillips pan head screw — M4 × 8mm, stainless steel 

 4 Phillips pan head screw with plastic anchor — #6-20, 1.25"L,
 zinc-plated steel 

 2 Desiccant 

 Mark the locations on the wall that line up with the four wall mount holes on
 the bottom of the wall mount bracket. 

 Ensure there are no building services (water, gas, or wiring) behind the

 Drill holes into the four marked locations on the wall. Make sure to remove any

 Install the two provided wall anchors into the upper two holes. 

 Attach the firewall to the wall mount. 

 Identify the four screw holes on the bottom panel of the
 PA-56R-POE-5G. 

 Align the wall mount bracket with the four screw holes. 

 Secure the wall mount bracket to the firewall using the four provided
 M4 Pan head screws. 

 Using four #6-20 screws, install the wall mount bracket to the anchors in the
 wall.

 Discovery (TA0007) 

 ATT&CK Technique 

 Cloud Infrastructure Discovery (T1580) 

 Severity 

 Informational 

 Description 

 Discovery API calls were executed across multiple AWS regions. 

 Attacker's Goals 

 Discover resources across regions to understand the cloud deployment footprint. 

 Target regions or services that may have weaker controls, lower visibility, or misconfiguration for potential exploitation. 

 Build a complete view of infrastructure for lateral movement or privilege escalation. 

 Investigative actions 

 Identify which services and regions were targeted. 

 Analyze the identity performing the discovery. 

 Correlate with other discovery or suspicious activities.

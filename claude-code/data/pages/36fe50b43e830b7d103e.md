 Navigate the Prisma Browser 

 Use Secure Browser Vision to work with the Prisma Browser in a dedicated
 Strata Cloud Manager view that brings its pages together in one place. 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Strata Cloud Manager 

 Prisma Access with Prisma Browser bundle license or
 Prisma Browser standalone license 

 Superuser or Prisma Browser 
 role 

 Secure Browser Vision available for your tenant 

 Secure Browser Vision is a dedicated Strata Cloud Manager view for the
 Prisma Browser . It brings the pages you use to monitor, configure, and
 investigate the Prisma Browser together into one task-based layout, so you can manage
 the Prisma Browser end to end from a single, dedicated workspace. 

 Your Prisma Browser pages are available in both Manage
 Everything (the full Strata Cloud Manager view) and Secure
 Browser Vision . Secure Browser Vision brings all of your
 Prisma Browser pages together into one focused, self-contained experience,
 organized around the way you manage the Prisma Browser . 

 Role-based access control works exactly as before in Secure Browser Vision: you see a
 page only if your role grants access to it. 

 The Vision Switcher and Access 

 The Vision switcher is a control in the left navigation that shows your active
 view and lets you move between views. 

 If you have access to more than one app or service, you see the Vision switcher
 and can switch between Manage Everything (the full
 Strata Cloud Manager view) and Secure Browser Vision 
 at any time. Your active view is saved to your preferences and restored at your
 next sign-in. 

 If you have only a Prisma Browser role, you do not see the switcher. You land
 directly in Secure Browser Vision and do not see other product areas. 

 Navigation Structure 

 Secure Browser Vision organizes your Prisma Browser pages into the following
 left-navigation groups: 

 Command Center - The Prisma Browser summary landing page, with an
 at-a-glance view of activity, health, and key metrics. 

 Insights - The visibility and analysis pages, grouped into Analytics
 (Highlights, Events, Investigation), Discovery (Application Usage, Account Usage,
 Extensions), Deployment (Users, Devices), Security (AI Access, Threat Search),
 and Operational (Application Experience, Operations). 

 Strata Canvas - AI Canvas, an AI-assisted workspace where you use
 natural-language prompts to explore data and build custom views. 

 Configuration - Onboarding; Policy (Rules, Controls Sets, Sign-in,
 Permission Requests); Policy Objects (Device Groups, User Groups, Applications,
 Data Profiles); Private Access (ZTNA Connector); and Change Management
 (Configuration Logs). 

 System Settings - The Prisma Browser administration pages (Browser
 Audit Logs, Browser Integrations, Application Experience Management), together
 with the pages to manage your Strata Cloud Manager tenant and platform settings,
 such as identity and access, tenants, trusted IPs, Strata Logging Service, and
 subscriptions. 

 Availability 

 Secure Browser Vision is being rolled out gradually to all customers. When it becomes
 available for your tenant, administrators with access to more than one app or service
 see the Vision switcher and can move between Manage Everything 
 and Secure Browser Vision , while administrators with only a
 Prisma Browser role land directly in Secure Browser Vision.

 Deactivate all Products 

 This topic contains procedure to deactivate all the products in the
 tenant. 

 Use one of the various ways to access Tenants. If you
 have a single tenant environment, you will see Products instead. The steps are the
 same. 

 Select the tenant that is linked to the product. 

 To deactivate all the products in the tenant, click the Deactivate
 Products icon. 

 Specify the reason for deactivation and Schedule
 Deactivation . 

 All the superusers will receive an email notification when you initiate
 product deactivation. 

 Cancel Deactivation Request to cancel deactivation
 during the 24 hour grace period.

 Monitor the Health of the App Servers 

 Monitor app server health in a Cortex XSOAR 6.13 high availability environment. 

 The App Servers page displays the health of the app servers in your environment. You can monitor your app servers and if one goes down, you can remove that app server from the cluster. 

 To view the health of the app servers, go to Settings → Advanced → App Servers . 

 To remove an app server, first make sure it is uninstalled (or permanently down), then select it and click Remove .

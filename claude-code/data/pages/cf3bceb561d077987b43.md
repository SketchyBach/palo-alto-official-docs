 Verify Failover or Suspend HA (SCM) 

 Suspend high availability (HA) for a managed firewall in an active/passive HA
 configuration from Strata Cloud Manager . Suspend the HA for maintenance or to verify a
 failover. 

 Log in to Strata Cloud Manager . 

 Configure Active/Passive HA . 

 Suspend HA for the HA peer. 

 Select Workflows NGFW Setup Device Management System Settings Device Management Cloud Managed Devices and locate the HA peer for which you want to suspend
 HA. 

 In the Actions column, expand the menu and Suspend
 HA . 
 Click OK to confirm suspending HA on the HA
 peer. 

 Verify the HA status for the now suspended HA peer displays
 suspended . 

 Restore HA for the suspended HA peer. 

 Select Workflows NGFW Setup Device Management System Settings Device Management Cloud Managed Devices and locate the suspended HA. 

 In the Actions column, expand the menu and Restore
 HA . 
 Click OK to confirm restoring HA on the
 suspended HA peer. 

 Verify the HA status for the restored HA peer displays
 active .

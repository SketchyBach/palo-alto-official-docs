 Manually De-Duplicate Incidents 

 Manually de-duplicate Cortex XSOAR 6.14 incidents. 

 You can manually de-duplicate incidents from the Incidents page or the Related Incidents tab. 

 To de-duplicate incidents from the Incidents page: 

 Select the incident checkboxes that are duplicates. 

 Click Mark as Duplicate . 

 Type the ID of the incident that you want to retain. 

 Click Validate . 

 Click Close others as duplicate . 

 The duplicated incident is removed from the table. 

 To de-duplicate incidents from the Related Incidents tab: 

 From the Incidents page, select the relevant incident checkbox. 

 In the Related Incidents tab, click the related incident you want to de-duplicate. 

 Click Close as duplicate . 

 The duplicated incident is closed and marked as duplicate.

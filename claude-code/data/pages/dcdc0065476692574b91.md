 Cortex XSOAR 8 (SaaS) 

 Cortex service-level agreement 

 Describes the SLA agreement for all Cortex products including Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS. 

 Cortex XSOAR hosted services are available 99.9% of the time, measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance. Any downtime resulting from outages of third-party connections or utilities or other reasons beyond the control of Palo Alto Networks will be excluded. For more information, see Cortex Products Service-Level Agreement .

 Replace a PA-1400 Series Power Supply 

 Learn how to replace a power supply on a PA-1400 Series firewall. 

 PA-1400 Series firewalls have two AC or DC power supplies (the second power supply is for
 redundancy). If one power supply fails, you can replace it without service interruption
 as described in the following procedure.

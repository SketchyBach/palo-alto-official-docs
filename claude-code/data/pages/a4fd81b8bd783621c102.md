 Install the PA-400R Series Firewall on a Flat Surface 

 Install a PA-400R Series firewall on a flat surface such as a desk or shelf. 

 The PA-450R and PA-450R-5G firewalls have four circular
 feet on their bottom side that can be used to install the device in a horizontal
 position. 

 If you have a firewall that supports
 multi-band antennas, proceed to Install Antennas on the PA-400R Series 5G Firewall . 

 Keep the device clean and clear of dust to ensure
 optimal heat dissipation and maintain proper hardware operation.

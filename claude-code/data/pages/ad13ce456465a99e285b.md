Data Processing Addendum (DPA)
1. Applicability

This Data Processing Addendum (“DPA”) shall apply to the services agreement (“Services Agreement”) by and between Extension Total Ltd. and its affiliates (“Extension Total”) and you (“Client”) to the extent that Extension Total processes Personal Data (as defined below).

2. Definitions

Terms used in this DPA but not defined herein (whether or not capitalized) shall have the meanings assigned to such terms in the Applicable Data Protection Laws.

Applicable Data Protection Laws shall mean, to the extent applicable to Extension Total's processing of Personal Data hereunder (with respect to each data subject):

(i) General Data Protection Regulations (European Parliament and Council of European Union (2016) Regulation (EU) 2016/679) (EU GDPR);

(ii) EU GDPR as it forms part of the law of England and Wales by virtue of section 3 of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 and UK Data Protection Act 2018 (UK GDPR);

(iii) California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018 (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020 (CPRA);

(iv) Israel’s Protection of Privacy Law (Israel);

(v) Any similar laws in any jurisdiction that may be enacted from time to time;

(vi) Any rules or regulations that amend and/or replace any of the aforementioned Data Protection Laws.

Personal Data refers to the definition of that term or any other similar term defined under the Applicable Data Protection Laws.

Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) shall mean:

(i) Where the EU GDPR applies, the standard contractual clauses pursuant to the EU Commission's Implementing Decision 2021/914 of 4 June 2021.

(ii) Where the UK GDPR applies, the EU SCCs together with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office addendum.

3. Processing of Personal Data on Behalf of Client

Extension Total acts as a processor/service provider for Client and performs processing operations on behalf of Client and upon the instructions of Client as a controller/business.

Processing is carried out as set forth in the Terms and for the Contracted Business Purpose.

4. Sensitive Data

The provision of services under the Agreement is not intended for the processing of Sensitive Data.

Extension Total shall have no liability for Sensitive Data, whether in connection with a Personal Data Breach or otherwise.

5. Client Representations

Client represents and warrants that:

It complies with personal data security and other obligations under Applicable Data Protection Laws.

It processes Personal Data collected in accordance with Applicable Data Protection Laws.

It has procedures in place for individuals to exercise their rights under Applicable Data Protection Laws.

It provides Personal Data to Extension Total only for the Contracted Business Purpose and does not sell Personal Data to Extension Total.

It remains responsible for the accuracy, quality, and legality of Client’s Personal Data.

6. Extension Total Obligations

Processing Personal Data solely on Client's behalf, in compliance with Client’s instructions.

Implementing technical and organizational security measures to protect Personal Data.

Limiting access to Personal Data on a need-to-know basis.

Assisting Client with data protection impact assessments or prior consultations with supervising authorities, at Client's sole expense.

CCPA Compliance:

Extension Total acts solely as a service provider.

Extension Total does not retain, use, or disclose Personal Data beyond the Contracted Business Purpose.

Extension Total may de-identify or aggregate Personal Data.

7. Sub-Processing

Client authorizes Extension Total to appoint sub-processors as per the Terms.

Sub-processors must qualify as service providers under Applicable Data Protection Laws.

Extension Total will notify Client of any new sub-processor appointments.

8. Data Subjects' Rights

Client is responsible for handling requests from data subjects regarding their rights under Applicable Data Protection Laws.

Extension Total will notify Client if it receives such a request and will not respond unless instructed by Client.

9. Personal Data Breach

Extension Total will notify Client without undue delay upon becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach.

At Client's request and expense, Extension Total will assist in investigating and notifying supervisory authorities and data subjects.

10. Deletion or Return of Processed Personal Data

Within sixty (60) days of termination or expiration of this DPA or the Services Agreement, Extension Total will return and destroy Personal Data, except where retention is authorized by law.

Extension Total will provide written certification of compliance upon Client’s request.

11. Audit Rights

Once a year, at Client’s expense, and upon reasonable prior notice, Extension Total will allow audits by a reputable auditor mandated by the Client.

Any third-party auditor must adhere to confidentiality obligations.

12. International Data Transfers

If Personal Data is transferred outside the European Economic Area (EEA) and/or the UK, such transfers shall be subject to:

Adequacy Decisions, or

Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

Client and Extension Total adopt Module II - “Controller to Processor” of the SCCs.

SCC modifications:

Clause 7: Not applicable.

Clause 9(a): Option 2 – General Written Authorization with a 30-day period.

Clause 17: Governed by Irish law.

Clause 18(b): Jurisdiction in Ireland.

13. General Terms

Governing Law and Jurisdiction: As per the governing law provisions in the Services Agreement.

Conflict: In the event of a conflict, this DPA prevails.

Changes in Law: Client may request changes to this DPA due to changes in Applicable Data Protection Laws with 45 days’ notice.

Severability: If any provision is found to be invalid, the remainder of the DPA remains in effect.

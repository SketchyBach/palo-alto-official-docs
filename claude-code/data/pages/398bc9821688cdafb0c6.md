 C2C in ASPM Command Center 

 The ASPM Command Center consumes the lineage signal at the executive level, receiving a propagation of code-side lineage statistics for organization-wide posture review. Each measurement is reported at the organization scope and respects the same RBAC and SBAC restrictions as the Coverage dashboard. 

 Use the Command Center when the audience is leadership tracking lineage maturity as a program metric, rather than a practitioner closing individual gaps.

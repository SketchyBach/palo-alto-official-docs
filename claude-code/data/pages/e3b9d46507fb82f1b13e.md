 ServiceNow in the Incidents and Alerts Overview 

 View ServiceNow in the Prisma Access Incidents and Alerts Overview in Strata Cloud
 Manager. 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Prisma Access (Managed by Strata Cloud Manager) 

 Prisma Access license 

 The Incidents Prisma Access Incidents & Alerts Overview page in the Strata Cloud Manager shows you a birds-eye view of the
 incidents and alerts related to Prisma Access. Three of the widgets on this page reflect
 information about your ServiceNow bidirectional integration: Incidents by
 Operational Status , Incidents by Assignee , and
 Incidents by Priority . 

 Incidents by Operational Status 

 Incidents by Operational Status shows the number of ServiceNow
 incidents by State. 

 Incidents by Assignee 

 Incidents by Assignee shows the amount of ServiceNow incidents
 by the person or entity who has the task of resolving them. 

 Incidents by Priority 

 Incidents by Priority shows the number of ServiceNow incidents
 by priority, with P1 being the most severe. 

 ServiceNow in the Incident List 

 The Incidents Prisma Access Incident List page in the Strata Cloud Manager shows an aggregated view of all of
 your incidents. The ServiceNow Ticket ID ,
 Priority , Operational Status , and
 Assignee columns show information about each ServiceNow
 ticket in your bidirectional integration.

 Ingest logs and data from Salesforce 

 Link to content pack/integration details (onboarded prior to July 26, 2026) 

 Includes the: 

 SalesforceV2 content pack, which provides CRM services and automations and contains the Salesforce v2 (Community Contribution) integration. 

 Salesforce Fusion content pack, which executes CRUD operations for employee lifecycle processes and contains the Salesforce Fusion IAM integration. 

 Link to connector 

 Salesforce connector : Uses the Salesforce content pack to provide full CRM services for data security, automation and remediation, security posture, and identity posture. Includes the following integrations: 

 Salesforce IAM: Performs Identity Lifecycle Management operations. 

 Salesforce: Provides CRM services.

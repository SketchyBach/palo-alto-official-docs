 XSOAR 6.14 Threat Intel Management 

 Generate a Threat Intel Report 

 Generate, export, and share threat intelligence reports in Cortex XSOAR 6.14. 

 Generate a Threat Intel report in PDF format. 

 Navigate to the Threat Intel Reports table and click the Name of the report you want to export. 

 Click the vertical ellipsis icon at the top right of the report and click Report . 

 (Optional) If your report has multiple tabs, and you want to generate a report from a specific tab, you can choose the tab from the Select a tab to generate report from dropdown list. 

 From the Properties section, choose a Format , Orientation , and Paper Size for the report. 

 Generate the report. 

 If you save the report as a template you can use the format again.

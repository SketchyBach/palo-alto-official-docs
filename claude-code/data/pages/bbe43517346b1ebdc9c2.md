 Branch HA Topologies 

 Learn more about the different topologies for Branch HA in Prisma SD-WAN . 

 Where Can I Use
 This? What Do I Need? 

 Prisma SD-WAN (Managed by Strata Cloud Manager ) 

 Prisma SD-WAN 

 Prisma SD-WAN supports different High Availability (HA) topologies
 for ION devices at branch sites. Use the following table to view the topology as per
 your device platform and configuration. 

 Topology Models Supporting the Topology 

 Configure Branch HA with Gen-1 Platforms (2000, 3000, 7000, and 9000) ION 2000, ION 3000, ION 7000, ION 9000 

 Configure Branch HA with Gen-2 Platforms (3200, 5200, and 9200) ION 3200, 5200, and 9200 

 Configure Branch HA with Gen-2 Embedded Switch Platforms (1200-S or 3200-L2) ION 1200-S and 3200 (L2) 

 Configure Branch HA for Devices with Software Cellular Bypass (1200-S-C-5G) ION 1200-S-C-5G 

 Configure Branch HA for Platforms without Bypass Pairs ION 1000, 1200, or for any model (with or without bypass
 pairs). 

 Configure Branch HA in a Hybrid Topology with Gen-1 (3000) and Gen-2 (3200) Platforms ION 3000 and ION 3200 

 After you have selected your topology model, configure physical connections and
 interfaces as per your topology model. The next step is to configure HA groups and then
 add your ION devices to the newly created HA Group. You can then edit the HA Group and
 the HA Group Membership. Read on to perform the following tasks that will help you
 configure branch HA.

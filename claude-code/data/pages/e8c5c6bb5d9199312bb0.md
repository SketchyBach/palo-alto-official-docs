 Cortex XSOAR 8 (SaaS) 

 Supported ciphers 

 Supported TLS 1.2 and TLS 1.3 cipher suites for Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS. 

 Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS supports the following Cipher Suites for TLS1.2 and TLS1.3: 

 TLS ECDHE ECDSA with AES256 GCM SHA384 

 TLS ECDHE RSA with AES256 GCM SHA384 

 TLS ECDHE ECDSA with AES128 GCM SHA256 

 TLS RSA with AES128 GCM SHA256

# Introduction to the Idira marketplace

Introduction to the Idira marketplace

The Idira Marketplace delivers an entitlement-based catalog tailored to your organization’s historical purchase record.

The Marketplace provides integrations, setup guides, and plugins and tools for easy discovery, filtering and secure download of required items.

Before you begin

To access the Idira marketplace you must have the following:

An ISPSS environment

You must be assigned to the System Administrator role or to the Ecosystem Admins role

Download Idira components

In the Marketplace workspace browse and download integrations, setup guides, and plugins.

Go to Ecosystem hub > Marketplace.

The available components for download appear with initial details, indicating purpose and level of support.

You can perform the following actions:

Action	Steps
Locate Assets	

Use the global search bar or filter options to sort by Certification level, Category, Use case, Idira service, or Support provider.

The Marketplace only shows categories that apply to your current purchases and setup.


Review Lifecycle Metadata	

Click on the asset's tile for critical operational data:

Item details: Use case, Category, Version, Release date, Last updated dates, Product support, Related services, and optionally End of Support (EOS/EOL) date.

Certification level: 

Certification level

Level

Who supports the item: Idira, Partner, or community

If you are required to download the item or not


Download	

A download button appears In the item's details pane only if the item requires download.

Click the download button.

Audit download actions

To maintain high security standards and operational compliance, all download actions in these workspaces are automatically logged in the platform's Audit service for future reference.

FedRAMP availability

If your FedRAMP environment requires marketplace resources, you can request an additional commercial tenant that is dedicated specifically for this purpose by opening a Technical Support ticket.

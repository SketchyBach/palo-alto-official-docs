 API documentation 

 Use the Cortex XSIAM APIs to integrate Cortex XSIAM with third-party apps or services to ingest alerts and to leverage alert stitching and investigation capabilities. 

 Using the Cortex XSIAM APIs, you can integrate Cortex XSIAM with third-party apps or services to ingest alerts and to leverage alert stitching and investigation capabilities. The APIs allow you to manage incidents in a ticketing or automation system of your choice by reviewing and editing the incident's details, status, and assignee. Using the APIs, you can also retrieve information on the endpoints, create an installation package, perform response actions directly on the endpoint, and more.

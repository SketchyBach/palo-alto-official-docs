 Activate Strata Logging Service 

 Learn how to activate the Strata Logging Service . 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Prisma AIRS (API Intercept) 

 Strata Cloud Manager 

 Strata Logging Service
 license 

 Access to the purchase confirmation email 

 Palo Alto Networks Customer Support Portal credentials 

 When your Strata Logging Service subscription expires, you will have a 30-day
 grace period to renew your license before log data is deleted. 

 Click the link in your purchase confirmation email. 

 Select your Strata Logging Service subscription and click
 Activate Subscription . 

 Log in to the Hub with your Palo Alto Networks customer support
 credentials. 

 Select the customer support account that you want to associate with your
 subscription. 

 Create a new tenant service group (TSG) or select
 an existing TSG. 

 If you are activating the Strata Logging Service instance in a new
 TSG, select Create New from the Tenant drop-down
 list and then enter a tenant name. 

 If you want to add the Strata Logging Service instance to an
 existing TSG, select the TSG from the drop-down list. 

 A tenant can have only one Strata Logging Service instance running on it. 

 Select the geographical Region for your Strata Logging Service instance. 

 Add a Strata Logging Service instance to the TSG. 

 Verify the storage space and region for your Strata Logging Service 
 instance. 

 If you choose to store log data in Strata Logging Service , Strata Logging Service keeps a record
 of your logs for future reference. 

 Review your selections, Agree to the Terms and Conditions, and click
 Activate Now .

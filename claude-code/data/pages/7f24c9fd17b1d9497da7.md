 AI Access Logs 

 AI Access logs record traffic between users and generative AI (GenAI) applications
 that traverse your Palo Alto Networks security infrastructure. Use these logs to monitor
 GenAI usage, review prompted content and AI-generated responses, and audit policy
 enforcement actions applied to that traffic.

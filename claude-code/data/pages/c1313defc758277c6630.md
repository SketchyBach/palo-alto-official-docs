 Prisma SASE FedRAMP Moderate and High Locations 

 Learn about supported locations for use in Prisma SASE FedRAMP Moderate and High "In
 Process" environments. 

 The following locations are authorized for use with a Prisma SASE FedRAMP environment,
 which includes support for locations in the continental United States (CONUS) and
 outside the continental United States (OCONUS). 

 Moderate Locations 

 The following are FedRAMP Moderate Locations. 

 Australia Southeast 

 Belgium

 Japan South

 US Central West 

 US East 

 US Northwest 

 US Southeast 

 US Southwest 

 US South 

 Limitation: Region-based multicloud redundancy isn't supported for FedRAMP
 Moderate.

 Reports 

 Create and manage reports in Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS. 

 Reports offer a streamlined way to analyze and present security data, helping to identify trends, evaluate response effectiveness, and facilitate communication across teams for better incident management. 

 Reports display data in the form of widgets, which enable analyzing data in different formats such as graphs, pie charts, or text.

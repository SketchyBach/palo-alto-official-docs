 Custom Document Types for Enterprise DLP 

 Upload custom documents to Enterprise Data Loss Prevention (E-DLP) to create match criteria specific
 to documents your organization uses.

 Upload your custom documents containing intellectual property or sensitive information
 to Enterprise Data Loss Prevention (E-DLP) to create custom document types. Custom document types
 serve as match criteria in data profiles to detect and prevent exfiltration of
 sensitive data specific to your organization. 

 Enterprise DLP supports both predefined and custom document types. Predefined
 document types cover standardized, widely-recognized content categories such as source
 code or financial records. Custom document types extend detection to content specific
 to your organization that predefined types don't cover. 

 Use custom document types when you need to detect: 

 Documents your organization produces that follow a consistent internal format,
 such as contracts, patent filings, or proprietary specifications 

 Categories of sensitive content unique to your organization, such as unreleased
 product designs or confidential customer agreements

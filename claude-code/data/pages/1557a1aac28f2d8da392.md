 Deactivate an Individual Product 

 This topic contains to deactivate specific products from the tenant. 

 Use one of the various ways to access Tenants. If you have a single
 tenant environment, you will see Products instead. The steps are the
 same. 

 Select the tenant that is linked to the product. 

 To deactivate individual products, go to Actions Deactivate Product . 

 Specify the reason for deactivation and Schedule
 Deactivation . 

 All the superusers will receive an email notification when you initiate
 product deactivation. 

 Cancel Deactivation Request to cancel deactivation
 during the 24 hour grace period.

 Rack Mount the ION 1000 

 Rack mount the Prisma SD-WAN ION 1000 on a standard 19
inch rack using the rack mount kit provided. 

 Rack mount the ION 1000 on a standard 19 inch
rack. The optional rack-mount kit includes two identical L-shaped
brackets that you can attach to either side of the ION 1000 using screws. 

 Gather the L-shaped rack-mounting brackets. 

 Locate the three (3) screw holes on each side of the
device 

 Attach the L-shaped brackets to the screw holes on the
front of the device. 

 Fasten the screws securely into the brackets. 

 Attach the L-shaped rack-mounting brackets to a standard
19 inch rack with the cage screws. 

 Verify that the device is securely mounted on the rack.

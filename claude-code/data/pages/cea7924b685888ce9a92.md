 Cortex XSOAR 8 Updates 

 Learn how Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS updates work, including the upgrade process and release guidance. 

 What is the Cortex XSOAR 8 upgrade process? 

 Major release : Released once a quarter. Includes new features, enhancements, and fixes. Customers are divided into 4 upgrade groups, with each group being upgraded 1 - 2 weeks after the previous group. FedRamp tenants will be upgraded after all 4 group upgrades are completed. 

 Maintenance release : Each quarter, we have one or two maintenance releases. Includes non-customer-facing changes/fixes. Email notifications are sent to customers. 

 Hotfixes : Once a week, for relevant customers. Includes urgent fixes. The fixes are merged into the maintenance release and will be available to all customers eventually.

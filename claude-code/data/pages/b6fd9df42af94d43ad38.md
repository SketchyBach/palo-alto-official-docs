 Manage Application Roles and Permissions 

 The roles assigned to your account determine what you
are able to do with any given app. 

 For a user in your organization to use an app, you must assign the proper roles to the user
 account. The roles assigned to your account determine what you are able to do with any
 given application. 

 These roles apply to apps only in the support account view of
 the Activation Console . These are app instances that haven't yet transitioned to a
 tenant service group (TSG). Where Can I Find My Apps describes further
 information about TSGs, the available views of the Activation Console , and how to
 manage roles for your TSG.

 How to Access my Applications? 

 Use the Activation Console to access your Palo Alto Networks apps and
services. 

 Use the Activation Console to access your apps. To access your
 apps, log in to the Activation Console , which requires that you have a Palo Alto
 Networks Customer Support Portal (CSP) account with
 one of the following roles: Super User, Standard User, or Limited User. Be aware that,
 to access an app, the app must be activated, and you must have the appropriate role
 assigned to you. 

 When you log in to the Activation Console , use the same username and password
combination that you use to log in to CSP. If you enter an incorrect
username or password ten times in a row, you will be locked out
for the next 60 minutes. After 60 minutes, you can try to log in
again. 

 To change the password that you use to log in to the Activation Console , you
will need to reset your password on the Customer
Support Portal . 

 See Activate Applications for a high level description on app
 activation. 

 See Manage App Roles for
details on the available roles and how to assign roles to users. 

 Accessing Apps Without a Role 

 You cannot
log in to the Activation Console unless you have been assigned at least one role in the Activation Console . If you do not have
at least one role, you will see a message stating that the Activation Console is
not available. 

 Contact your Account Administrator, App Administrator,
or Instance Administrator to have a role assigned to you. If you
do not know who to contact, identify them by signing in to your
Customer Support Portal account. Then, refer to How to Manage Users in Your Account and
follow the instructions to Edit an Account Member .

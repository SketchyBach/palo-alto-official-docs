 PA-500 Series Firewall Overview 

 Get an overview of the PA-500 Series Firewall. 

 The Palo Alto Networks® PA-500 Series Next-Generation firewalls include the
 PA-501, PA-505,
 PA-510, 
 PA-520, PA-540, PA-545-POE, PA-550 , PA-555-POE, and
 PA-560. These firewalls are designed for small organizations or branch offices and
 include the following main features: a TPM module for PAN-OS key storage and security,
 ZTP functionality, Power Over Ethernet (PoE) support for select models, and
 active/passive and active/active high availability (HA). All PA-500 Series firewalls (except the PA-505) can make use of dual
 power adapters for load sharing and power redundancy (the second power adapter is sold
 separately). The PA-500 Series firewall enables you to secure your organization through
 advanced visibility and control of applications, users, and content. 

 First Supported PAN-OS® Software Releases: 
 PAN-OS 12.1.2 — PA-520, PA-540, PA-545-POE, PA-550, PA-555-POE, and
 PA-560 

 PAN-OS 12.1.3 — PA-505 and
 PA-510

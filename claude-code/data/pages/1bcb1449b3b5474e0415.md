 AIOps for NGFW Premium license (use the Strata Cloud Manager app) or Strata Cloud Manager Pro 

 → The features and capabilities available to you in Strata Cloud Manager depend on which license(s) you are
 using. 

 For the selected time duration and VPN cluster, Strata Cloud Manager 
 displays your top 5 impacted applications based on their computed percentage of impacted
 traffic out of total bytes. A higher computed percentage indicates a greater impact on
 the application. 

 Click View More to check all the impacted applications. 

 Furthermore, click an application to view its details including traffic and the used
 links. You can also click a used link to view its details.

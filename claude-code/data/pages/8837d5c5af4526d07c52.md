 Manage dashboards and reports 

 Manage dashboard and report ownership, sharing, imports, and deleted content. 

 You can access all pages for managing your dashboards and reports under Dashboards & Reports. Dashboards are managed through the Dashboard Manager , and report templates and generated reports are managed through the Reports page. You can also manage and review your deleted content in the Trash folder.

 Export an alert as a PDF 

 You can export the information for an alert in the form of a PDF report. 

 Navigate to Incident Response → Incidents and click on the incident in the incident list. The incident details will display in the pane on the right. 

 In the details pane, select the Alerts tab, and click on the alert. 

 At the top right of the alert details pane, click the Export button to generate the PDF report. 

 You can also generate a PDF of an alert from the Alerts table by clicking on an alert in the table, and clicking the Export button on the alert details page.

 Get started with Autonomous DEM in Prisma
Access to continually monitor the digital experience across all
users, applications, and remote sites in your SASE environment.

DEM for your GlobalProtect Mobile Users connecting to Prisma Access
or Prisma SD-WAN remote sites that are connected to Prisma Access,
or both.

 Prisma SASE FedRAMP Moderate and High Activation 

 Learn how to activate a license for FedRAMP Moderate and High. 

 The Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program (FedRAMP) is a United States
 government-wide program that provides a standardized approach to security assessment,
 authorization, and continuous monitoring for cloud products and services for government
 users. Palo Alto Networks has demonstrated FedRAMP compliance. 

 For more information about Palo Alto Networks FedRAMP authorization, visit
 Prisma Access for U.S. Public Sector . 

 To ensure FedRAMP compliance, you should install and configure your environment
 using recommended best practices and guidelines in the following topics.

 First Time Activation and Return Access 

 There are a few ways to access your license activation and subscription or tenant
 management based on first time or return visit. 

 First Time Activation Prisma SASE Multitenant Portal and FedRAMP 

 If you are activating a license for the first time, you are
 automatically directed to Common Services Subscription or Tenant Management during the activation process. 

 If you are a FedRAMP Moderate customer as of April 2024 or Prisma
 SASE FedRAMP High customer as of December 2023, the following
 also applies to you. 

 You can access through the original support account view of the
 hub Prisma SASE Platform button Tenants and Services Common Services Subscription or Tenant Management . 

 FedRAMP Moderate License Activation 

 Get started with FedRAMP Moderate license activation . 

 The FedRAMP Moderate license activations are similar to the consumer license
 activation in Common Services. Just remember to use the Customer
 Service FedRAMP account , the Gov Region ,
 and the Gov Strata Logging Service .

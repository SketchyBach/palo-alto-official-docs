 Migration FAQs - XSOAR 6 On-Prem to XSOAR 8 SaaS 

 Frequently asked questions about the migration from Cortex XSOAR 6 to Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS. 

 The following sections describe the frequently asked questions when migrating from Cortex XSOAR 6 to Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS.

 General 
 Who is the Cortex XSOAR 6 On-prem to SaaS migration available for? 

 The Cortex XSOAR 6 On-prem to SaaS migration (for a single tenant) is available for all customers, including FedRAMP Moderate.

 Self-service migration to Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS 

 Now 

 A self-service migration option is available. This does not include the migration of indicator or incident data. 

 To use the self-service migration option, activate a new Cortex XSOAR 8 tenant for up to 60 days by requesting an evaluation license. After self-service migration, you can decommission your Cortex XSOAR 6 server and then move your licenses to Cortex XSOAR 8. We recommend going through this process with your assigned Customer Success Representative and Sales Representative to assist in migrating your content such as automations, playbooks, etc. 

 See Migrate from Cortex XSOAR 6 to Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS (Self-Service) .

 I currently have a child tenant (part of MSSP), and I want to move to an Enterprise (standalone) tenant. What are my options? 

 This is not supported. We support migration from Cortex XSOAR 6 Enterprise to Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS Enterprise and Cortex XSOAR 6 Multi-tenant to Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS Multi-tenant. For other options, you need to create a new Cortex XSOAR 8 tenant. 

 In which region will my Cortex XSOAR 8 tenant be created? 

 Cortex XSOAR 8 is deployed on GCP Cloud. When you create your Cortex XSOAR 8 tenant, you select a geographical region where the tenant will reside. You can view a complete list of available regions . 

 Who can initiate the migration? 

 You can activate your tenants (DEV/PROD) using the CSP Account Admin (Super User). If you can't see your tenant for activation in Cortex Gateway, check your entitled account using your serial number in the Admin Site . 

 Do I need to redeploy engines as a part of the migration to Cortex XSOAR 8? 

 Yes. Engines and IP addresses must be upgraded. 

 Are API keys migrated? 

 No. The Cortex XSOAR 8 URL is different from the Cortex XSOAR 6 URL. Users have to use a new URL to access the new system. Other components, such as SSO integrations or API keys, that rely on the legacy URL, will not continue to function and must be reconfigured. 

 Do I need to do anything with my SSO? 

 Yes, SSO settings depend on the URL, which will change when you move to Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS. 

 How long are Management Audit logs kept? 

 For migrated customers, Management Audit logs are kept for 10 years. 

 IP address changes 
 Are IPs and URLs changed during the migration? What do we need to do? 

 Yes, both IPs and URLs have been changed. Add the Cortex XSOAR 8 IP addresses to your firewall list before transitioning to Cortex XSOAR 8. You must also create new API keys and SSO connections for Cortex XSOAR 8. 

 What are the new IPs and URLs we need to add to our allow list? When should we add them to the allow list? 

 We recommend adding the new IPs and URLs to the allow list ASAP, even before the migration. For a list of the new IP addresses, see Enable access to Palo Alto Networks resources .

 Install the PA-500 Series Firewall in an Equipment Rack 

 Learn how to install the PA-500 Series Firewall in an equipment rack. 

 There are different rack mount SKUs that enable you
 to install a PA-500 Series firewall in a four-post 19" rack. The rack mount SKU
 used and the number of firewalls you can install depends on which PA-500 Series firewall
 you are installing. 

 PA-501 and PA-505 —One firewall and one power
 adapter can be installed in the PAN-1RU-4POST-RACK-10. 

 PA-510 —Up to two firewalls (with up to two power adapters each) can be
 installed in the PAN-1RU-4POST-RACK-10. 

 PA-520 and PA-540 — Up to two firewalls (with up to two power adapters each)
 can be installed in the PAN-1RU-4POST-RACK-11. 

 PA-550 and PA-560 — One firewall and up to two power adapters can be
 installed in the PAN-1RU-4POST-RACK-11. 

 PA-545-POE and PA-555-POE — One firewall and up to two power adapters can be
 installed in the PAN-1RU-4POST-RACK-12. 

 PA-520-5G — One firewall can be installed in the
 PAN-1RU-4POST-RACK-16 (four-post rack) or the PAN-2RU-2POST-RACK-17 (two-post
 rack).

 PAN-1RU-4POST-RACK-10 

 Install a PA-501, PA-505, or PA-510 firewall in an equipment rack using the
 PAN-1RU-4POST-RACK-10 mounting kit. 

 Secure the power adapter(s) to the mounting tray. 

 For each power adapter being installed (for a maximum of two for the
 PA-510), feed one Velcro strap through the opening at the dedicated
 power adapter locations on the mounting tray. 

 Place each power adapter into its designated location, then wrap each
 Velcro strap over the top of the adapter so that the adapter is fixed
 in place. 

 Install the firewall in the mounting tray. 

 Align the four rubber feet of each device to the mounting tray, then
 secure it using three #6-32 x 3/16" long flathead screws. 

 Slide one of the adjustable mounting brackets into one of the fixed mounting
 brackets to create a mounting rail. Repeat for the second mounting rail. The
 adjustable and fixed brackets are the same for the left and right side. 

 Align the bottom edge of the mounting rails to the bottom of the space reserved
 for your firewall. Align the slotted holes in the adjustable mounting bracket to
 the holes on the rear of the equipment frame. 

 Secure the rails to the equipment frame with mounting screws (not provided)
 compatible with your equipment frame. Tighten the screws to their recommended
 torque value. 

 Slide the mounting tray into the rails previously fixed to the equipment rack.
 Stop when the front flange on the mounting tray is flush with the front of the
 rail. 

 Align the slotted hole in the mounting tray to the hole in the equipment frame.
 Secure the mounting tray to the equipment frame on both sides using one screw
 each (not provided). The screws must be compatible with your equipment
 frame. 

 Proceed to Connect Power to the PA-500 Series Firewall . 

 PAN-1RU-4POST-RACK-11 

 Install a PA-520, PA-540, PA-550, or PA-560 firewall in an equipment rack using the
 PAN-1RU-4POST-RACK-11 mounting kit. 

 Secure the power adapter(s) to the mounting tray. 

 For each power adapter being installed (for a maximum of four for the
 PA-520 and PA-540 and a maximum of two for the PA-550 and PA-560), feed
 two Velcro straps through the openings at the dedicated power adapter
 locations on the mounting tray. 

 Place each power adapter into its designated location, then wrap both
 Velcro straps over the top of the adapter so that the adapter is fixed
 in place. 

 Route the power cables through paths on the mounting tray. 

 Route the initial segment of the power cables (that plugs into the
 firewall) along the wall of the mounting tray. Use two #6-32 screws to
 install a routing cover over each wall of the mounting tray so that the
 power cable is contained within. 

 Using the following images as references, guide the remaining length of
 the power cables along the mounting tray. Thread the provided cable ties
 through the lances and cutouts in the mounting tray. Wrap the cable ties
 around the power cables, ensuring that the power cables are fixed in
 place.

 place.

 PAN-1RU-4POST-RACK-16 

 Install a PA-520-5G firewall in a four-post equipment rack using the
 PAN-1RU-4POST-RACK-16 mounting kit. 

 The PA-520-5G firewall can be mounted in a 4-post equipment rack using the
 PAN-1RU-4POST-RACK-16. 

 Parts List – PAN-1RU-4POST-RACK-16 

 Quantity Item 

 1 4-post rack mount tray 

 1 Fixed rail 

 1 Adjustable rail 

 2 Phillips pan head screw — M4 × 8mm 

 30 Torx flat head screw (T10) — M3 × 5mm 

 16 Adhesive cable tie mount 

 28 Locking cable tie 

 4 Power cord retainer 

 8 Velcro strap 

 4 RF cable assembly — R/A SMA to N-type, 450mm L 

 4 RF cable assembly — ST SMA to N-type, IP67, 93mm L 

 2 Dual LTE/5G cable mount bracket 

 4 Single LTE/5G cable mount bracket 

 1 Power cord routing cover, right 

 1 Power cord routing cover, left 

 Verify that the equipment rack has enough space to accommodate the firewall and
 has 1RU of space both above and below it. 

 Align the four holes on the bottom of the firewall with the designated location
 in the mounting tray. Seat the firewall and use four M3 Torx flat head screws to
 secure it in place. 

 Place up to two power adapters in the designated locations on the mounting tray.
 Loop the provided Velcro straps through the cutouts in the mounting tray, using
 two straps per power adapter. 

 Connect your cables and antenna connectors to the device and route them through
 the mounting tray in order to minimize slack. 

 A total of six antenna cable brackets are included — four single cable
 brackets and two dual cable brackets. Install each bracket using two M3
 screws through the holes in the mounting tray. After guiding the antenna
 cables through the brackets, secure each cable in place using the
 included lock washer and nut. 

 Wrap cable ties through the small cutouts in the tray to secure the cables in
 place. 

 Install the two provided power cord routing covers on the right and left sides
 of the tray. Use two M3 screws for each side (four total). 

 Apply cable retention clamps over the power cords by slotting them into the
 tray. 

 Slide the adjustable rail into the fixed rail to create a mounting rail. Repeat
 for the second mounting rail. The adjustable and fixed rails are the same for the
 left and right side. 

 Align the bottom edge of the mounting rails to the bottom of the space reserved
 for your firewall. Align the slotted holes in the adjustable rail to the holes on
 the rear of the equipment frame. 

 Secure the rails to the equipment frame with mounting screws (not provided)
 compatible with your equipment frame. Tighten the screws to their recommended
 torque value. 

 Slide the firewall (seated securely in the mounting tray) into the equipment
 rack. 

 Make sure that you do
 not block any of the airflow vents on the firewall. 

 Secure the mounting tray to the equipment rack using compatible screws. 

 After installation, you
 should inspect the hardware on a regular basis in order to account for dust,
 debris, and any other obstructions that may affect the firewall's
 airflow. 

 Do not place any items or
 obstructions directly above or below the firewall within the 1RU of free
 space. 

 PAN-2RU-2POST-RACK-17 

 Install a PA-520-5G firewall in a two-post equipment rack using the
 PAN-2RU-2POST-RACK-17 mounting kit. 

 The PA-520-5G firewall can be mounted in a 2-post equipment rack using the
 PAN-2RU-2POST-RACK-17. 

 Parts List – PAN-2RU-2POST-RACK-17 

 Quantity Item 

 1 2-post rack mount tray 

 8 Phillips pan head screw — M4 × 8mm 

 8 Torx flat head screw (T10) — M3 × 5mm 

 16 Locking cable tie 

 2 Power cord retainer 

 4 Velcro strap 

 4 RF cable assembly — R/A SMA to N-type, 450mm L 

 Verify that the equipment rack has enough space to accommodate the firewall and
 has 1RU of space both above and below it. 

 Using a Flathead screwdriver, remove the six screws (three on each side)
 securing the metal stiffener to the top of the rack tray. Remove the
 stiffener. 

 Align the four holes on the bottom of the firewall with the designated location
 in the mounting tray. Seat the firewall and use four M3 Torx flat head screws to
 secure it in place. 

 Place up to two power adapters in the designated location beside the firewall.
 Loop the provided Velcro straps through the cutouts in the mounting tray, using
 two straps per power adapter. Use cord retainers to secure the power cables in
 place. 

 Install the stiffener back over the device and secure it using three M3 screws
 on each side (6 total). 

 Connect your cables and antenna connectors to the device and route them through
 the mounting tray in order to minimize slack. 

 Wrap cable ties through the small cutouts in the tray to secure the cables in
 place. 

 Slide the firewall (seated securely in the mounting tray) into the equipment
 rack. 

 Make sure that you do
 not block any of the airflow vents on the firewall. 

 Secure the mounting tray to the equipment rack using compatible screws. 

 After installation, you
 should inspect the hardware on a regular basis in order to account for dust,
 debris, and any other obstructions that may affect the firewall's
 airflow. 

 Do not place any items or
 obstructions directly above or below the firewall within the 1RU of free
 space.

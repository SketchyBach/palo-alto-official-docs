 ADEM Tier 2 Support Role-Based Access Control 

 Release Date: March 2026 
 | 
 Last Updated: May 2026 

 To provide targeted read and write access for incident remediation workflows without exposing broader system configurations, Autonomous Digital Experience Management (ADEM) offers the ADEM Tier 2 Support role . You can assign this predefined role to third-party helpdesk personnel, tier 2 and 3 support teams, or administrators who only require access to Autonomous DEM capabilities. 

 This role operates within the Prisma® Access application and specifically restricts access to other services, overarching dashboards, or Strata Logging Service logs. By limiting access, you ensure support personnel interact only with the tools necessary for their specific scope of work. 

 When you assign the ADEM Tier 2 Support role in Strata Cloud Manager, you empower your support teams to actively manage user experience monitoring. This role enables administrators to perform the following tasks: 

 Create, edit, and delete application tests and application suites. 

 Manage Real User Monitoring (RUM) results and browser plug-in configurations. 

 Administer Canary Groups and system upgrades. 

 Deploy or remove Universal Agents and manage self-serve configurations.

 Application Experience by Office Location or Wireless Network 

 Release Date: March 2026 
 | 
 Last Updated: May 2026 

 To segment application experience data by physical office or building location, Autonomous DEM supports office location filtering . This feature helps you determine whether performance issues are isolated to specific sites and streamlines troubleshooting for location-dependent problems. 

 You can create custom office locations and associate them with IP subnets. When users connect from networks that match these subnets, ADEM automatically tags their sessions with the corresponding office location. The office location filter is available across the Application Experience dashboard. 

 In addition to office location filtering, you can further refine your analysis by filtering user experience data for a specific SSID and BSSID. This allows you to isolate performance issues down to specific wireless networks or even individual access points. 

 When you apply an office location filter, the dashboard metrics display data only for the time users spent at that specific location. This time-based filtering ensures you're analyzing performance that's relevant to the selected office, even if users move between locations.

 Autonomous DEM Universal Agent 

 Release Date: March 2026 
 | 
 Last Updated: May 2026 

 Palo Alto Networks® introduces Autonomous Digital Experience Management (ADEM) Universal agents to expand deployment capability and network visibility. Previously, ADEM agents were restricted to environments utilizing Prisma® SD-WAN or Next-Generation Firewall (NGFW) SD-WAN. To provide a truly unified monitoring experience, ADEM agents now offer expanded support to all branch locations that use non Palo Alto Networks®' SD-WAN solutions. 

 The Universal Agents solve these challenges and enable seamless deployment across a wider array of hardware. These agents install directly behind SD-WAN devices that support containers. This capability allows organizations to maintain consistent digital experience insight across their entire network without dependence on specific Palo Alto Networks® SD WAN platforms. The agents perform synthetic tests from branch offices to provide critical data on network performance and user experience, regardless of the underlying infrastructure.

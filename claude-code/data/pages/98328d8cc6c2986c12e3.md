 Activate Prisma SD-WAN License for CSP Users 

 Learn how to activate Prisma SD-WAN license for a CSP users. 

 Activate your single TSG tenant using CSP activation. 
 Activate your Prisma SD-WAN tenant only if: 

 You or your company admin have not received a magic link by email for
 activation. 

 You have a Superuser role. 

 You have access to the Customer Support Portal. 

 Sign in to https://support.paloaltonetworks.com/ to access the
 Customer Support Portal. 

 Click Activate Products on the left-hand navigation
 bar. 

 Locate your Sales Order number and click Activate to
 launch the activation user interface. 

 Create and activate your Prisma SASE account 

 When your Prisma SD-WAN tenant is migrated to a Tenant Service Group (TSG) , you
 will see this notification when you log in to the Prisma SD-WAN web interface. 

 Use the activation link in your email to activate your
 account. 

 Click the Activate Account button in your
 email. 

 Set your password and then click Create My
 Account . 

 Set up multi-factor authentication and then click
 Finish . 

 Log in to Prisma SASE. 

 Your view of tabs and widgets will be based on your
 license.

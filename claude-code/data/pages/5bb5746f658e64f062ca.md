 Install the Slide Rail into the Rack 

 Install the slide rail into a standard 19 inch rack using
the given components of the Prisma SD-WAN ION 7000 device. 

 Install the slide rail into a standard 19
inch rack as described below: 

 Select a 1-U slot to insert the slide rail into
a standard 19-inch rack. 

 Snap the three (3) square pegs of the rail into the front
and back mounting plate of the rack. 

 Ensure that you fully engage the spring retention clip
so that the rail is securely attached to the mounting plate. 

 If the rack has square holes: 

 Insert the slide rails into a standard 19-inch
rack making sure that the rails snap into place. 

 Attach the chassis-mounting brackets to either side
of the ION 7000. 

 Slide the ION 7000 into the rack and confirm that
the device is installed securely in the rack. 

 If the rack has threaded round holes: 

 Unscrew and detach the square rail snap
ends from both ends of each rail. 

 Using screws that fit the threads of the rack (not
included), attach each end of the rail to the rack. 

 Attach the chassis-mounting brackets to either side
of the ION 7000. 

 Slide the ION 7000 into the rack and confirm that
the device is installed securely in the rack. 

 If the rack has unthreaded round holes: 

 Insert the rail as sent behind the hole
plate. 

 Using screws that fit the threads of the rail (not
included), screw through the hole in the plate to the screw holes
on the end of the rack (from the front). 

 Attach the chassis-mounting brackets to either side
of the ION 7000. 

 Slide the ION 7000 into the rack and confirm that
the device is installed securely in the rack.

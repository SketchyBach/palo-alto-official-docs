 Subscribe to Prisma Cloud DSPM Within Prisma Cloud 

 This article describes how existing customers can subscribe to DSPM (Data Security Posture Management) within Prisma Cloud. 

 DSPM is an agentless, multi-cloud, data security platform that discovers, classifies, protects, and governs sensitive data. 

 To subscribe to DSPM: 

 Click your profile image in the top right corner, and then click View Subscriptions .

 In the Subscriptions window, navigate to Data Security Posture Management , and click Subscribe .

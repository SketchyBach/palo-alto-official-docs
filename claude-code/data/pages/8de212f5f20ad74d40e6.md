 Disable Predefined Reports 

 Conserve firewall resources by disabling unneeded predefined reports. Ensure no
 report groups or PDF summaries include disabled reports to prevent data voids. 

 To manage system resources efficiently on your firewall, you can disable predefined
 reports that are not being utilized. The firewall typically generates about 40 such
 reports daily, and deactivating the ones that are not needed helps conserve valuable
 system resources. 

 Important Considerations Before Disabling Reports 

 Before you disable predefined reports, it is crucial to ensure that no report group or PDF summary report report includes the
 specific predefined reports you intend to disable. If they are included, those
 report groups or PDF summaries will be rendered without any data, leading to
 incomplete or blank reports. Therefore, ensure that you perform a quick check of
 your existing report configurations. 

 Select Device Setup Management and
edit the Logging and Reporting Settings. 

 Select the Pre-Defined Reports tab
and clear the check box for each report you want to disable. To
disable all predefined reports, click Deselect All . 

 Click OK and Commit .

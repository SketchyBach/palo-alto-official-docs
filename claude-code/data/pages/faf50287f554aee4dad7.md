 ADEM Zoom Integration 

 Release Date: March 2023 
 | 
 Last Updated: June 2026 

 Debugging Zoom call quality issues and proactively identifying performance problems impacting users required manually correlating network data with Zoom call logs. ADEM now integrates with Zoom to provide the first per-minute root-cause analysis on the market, giving you minute-by-minute visibility into every Zoom call across your network. 

 The integration helps you debug active call quality problems, monitor Zoom performance trends over time, and identify degradation before it affects your users' experience. When a user reports a problem with a Zoom call, you can quickly review the per-minute performance data to pinpoint exactly when the issue occurred and which network or endpoint condition caused it. Rather than relying on user-reported descriptions, you get objective, time-stamped data for every call in your environment. 

 To enable the ADEM Zoom integration, you need a Zoom Quality of Service Subscription (QSS) license from Zoom. Once enabled, ADEM automatically correlates Zoom call quality metrics with network performance and endpoint data from Prisma® Access, giving you a comprehensive picture of each call's quality drivers without requiring separate monitoring tools.

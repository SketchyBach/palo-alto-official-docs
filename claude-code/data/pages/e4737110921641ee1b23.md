 Log Collection and Troubleshooting for iOS Endpoints 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Prisma Agent 

 iOS 16 and later versions 

 Internet access 

 When you experience unusual behavior, you can collect and share logs with your
 administrator for further troubleshooting 

 Open the Prisma Agent app. 

 Go to the Settings page by tapping the Settings 
 icon. 

 View connection statistics to see more information to help with
 troubleshooting. 

 Under Logs , select Share Logs and
 click Download to download the log files locally to your
 device. 

 You can share the logs with your administrators using mail or other preferred
 apps.

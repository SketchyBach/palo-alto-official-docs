 Scanning 

 Cortex Xpanse provides targeted scanning of customer networks from an attributed scanning infrastructure. 

 Cortex Xpanse uses data collected from global internet scans as well open-source intelligence about the internet to maintain a complete inventory of all the internet-facing assets that belong to an organization.

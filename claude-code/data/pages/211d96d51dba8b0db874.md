 Upgrade from Cortex XSOAR 6 

 Learn how to upgrade from Cortex XSOAR 6 to Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS, including migration FAQs. 

 If I currently have Cortex XSOAR 6 On-prem, what are my upgrade options? 

 Existing Cortex XSOAR 6 On-prem customers may migrate to Cortex XSOAR 8 SaaS. 

 Will on-prem and hosted deployments continue to have access to both Dev and Prod after migration to Cortex XSOAR 8? 

 Yes, the dev and prod environments are supported in Cortex XSOAR 8. 

 Is migration from a single server to a multi-tenant and MSSP supported? 

 This is considered a complex migration path and is not supported. 

 How often will Cortex XSOAR 8 have new releases? 

 We will also release a new version of Cortex XSOAR 8 approximately every 3 months, which will be upgraded automatically. 

 While Cortex XSOAR 6 is still available, how often will it have new releases, and what is the end-of-life policy? 

 We will continue to release new versions of the 6 product line approximately every 3 months. 

 The End of Life (EOL) policy of Cortex XSOAR 6 is detailed on the Product Support Lifecycle page. 

 How long can Cortex XSOAR 6 On-prem and Cortex XSOAR 8 tenants run in parallel? 

 For more information, see. 

 Engine Upgrades - Will customers have to manually upgrade the engine whenever Cortex XSOAR is upgraded? 

 Engine upgrades are required when a Cortex XSOAR upgrade includes an engine update. Some Cortex XSOAR cloud updates do not require an engine update. 

 Will migrated customers with both Dev and Prod get the same setup as in Cortex XSOAR 8? 

 Yes, they will get one tenant for Dev and one for Prod. 

 Cortex XSOAR Version 6 will be released throughout 2024 and 2025. The EOL timeline for all relevant versions continues as before. For example, the XSOAR 6.9 version was EOL on Nov 1, 2023.

 Application and Link Characteristics 

 Monitor the application and link characteristics of SD-WAN
firewalls. 

 Select an app in the App Performance section to view
the detailed traffic characteristics for the application during
the specified time frame. Here you can view a pie chart to help
you understand the breakdown of app traffic across your internet
services, as well as a line graph to understand how many bytes of
data were transferred over each internet service over time. Additionally,
displayed are the links used by the application. 

 In the Links Used section, click an ethernet Link to view the
link characteristics. Reviewing the link characteristics helps you
to understand which links the app traffic used and to understand
how much of the bytes were impacted out of the total bytes in the
selected time frame.

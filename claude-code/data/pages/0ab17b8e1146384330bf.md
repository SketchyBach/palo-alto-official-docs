 Connect Power to a PA-3200 Series Firewall 

 PA-3200 Series firewall power connection overview. 

 PA-3200 Series firewalls have either two AC or two DC power
supplies (the second power supply is for redundancy). Before you connect
power, read the PA-3200
Series Electrical Specifications .

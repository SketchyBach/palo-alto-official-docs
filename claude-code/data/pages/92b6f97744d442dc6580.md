 Strata Cloud Manager uses the Unified Incident Framework to
 centralize incident management and provide a more contextual experience. The
 product-specific alerts and incidents are now located under Incidents . The previous Alerts & Incidents
 dashboard remains available in read-only mode for reference. 

 Strata Cloud Manager gives you a common framework for interacting and
 investigating the incidents and alerts that Palo Alto Networks products and subscriptions 
 detect in your enterprise:

 To help you maintain the ongoing health of your devices and deployments, and to
 avoid disruption to your business, explore each of the incidents and alerts pages
 to: 

 View incidents and alerts across your network, and drill down to
 investigate. 

 Create and review rules that trigger incident and alert
 notifications. 

 You can move between your incidents and alerts and the Log Viewer: Strata Cloud Manager to investigate activity on your network
 that's triggering or is associated with incidents and alerts.

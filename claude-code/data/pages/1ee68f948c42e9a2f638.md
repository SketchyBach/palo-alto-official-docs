 INC_ZTNA_CONNECTOR_MEMORY_HIGH 

 Learn about the INC_ZTNA_CONNECTOR_MEMORY_HIGH incident. 

 Synopsis 

 The incident is raised when the ZTNA connector average memory utilization
 over 30 minutes exceeds 80%. The incident is cleared when the ZTNA connector average
 memory utilization over 30 minutes is less than 75%. 

 Incident Code—INC_ZTNA_CONNECTOR_MEMORY_HIGH 

 Severity—Critical 

 For details about incident severity, see Incidents Distribution Over
 Time in Incidents and Alerts Overview .

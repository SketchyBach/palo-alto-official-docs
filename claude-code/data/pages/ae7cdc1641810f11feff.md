 Onboard a BlueJeans App to SSPM 

 Connect a BlueJeans instance to SSPM to detect posture risks. 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Strata Cloud Manager 

 SaaS Security Posture Management license 

 Or any of the following licenses that include the Data Security license: 

 CASB-X 

 CASB-PA 

 For SSPM to detect posture risks in your BlueJeans instance, you must onboard your
 BlueJeans instance to SSPM. Through the onboarding process, SSPM connects to a
 BlueJeans API and, through the API, scans your BlueJeans instance for misconfigured
 settings. If there are misconfigured settings, SSPM suggests a remediation action
 based on best practices. 

 SSPM gets access to your BlueJeans instance through a BlueJeans Enterprise app that
 you create. During onboarding, SSPM prompts you for information that identifies your
 BlueJeans Enterprise app. 

 To onboard your BlueJeans instance, you complete the following actions: 
 Collect Information for Connecting to Your BlueJeans Instance 

 Connect SSPM to Your BlueJeans Instance 

 Collect Information for Connecting to Your BlueJeans Instance 

 To access your BlueJeans instance, SSPM requires the
 following credentials for a BlueJeans Enterprise app. You must create this BlueJeans
 Enterprise app before you begin the onboarding process. 

 Item Description 

 App Key 

 A character string that identifies your BlueJeans Enterprise
 app. You specify this App Key when you create your BlueJeans
 Enterprise app. 

 App Secret 

 A character string that BlueJeans generates when you create
 your BlueJeans Enterprise app. 

 As you complete the following steps, make note of the values of the items
 described in the preceding table. You will need to enter these values during
 onboarding to enable SSPM to access your BlueJeans instance. 

 Identify the BlueJeans administrator account that you will use to create
 your BlueJeans Enterprise app. 

 ( Required Permissions ) The BlueJeans Enterprise app must be
 created by a BlueJeans administrator. 

 Create your BlueJeans Enterprise app. 

 Open a web browser and go to the BlueJeans login page. 

 Log in to the administrator account that you identified
 earlier. 

 From the navigation bar at the top of the page, navigate to your
 ADMIN page. 

 From your Admin page, select GROUP SETTINGS ENTERPRISE APPS . 

 If the
 ENTERPRISE APPS tab is not enabled,
 contact the BlueJeans support team and ask them to enable
 enterprise-level APIs for your account. 

 On your Enterprise Apps page, ADD NEW
 APP . 

 On the Create App page, specify a Name, Description, and App Key and
 SAVE your BlueJeans Enterprise app. 

 Copy your BlueJeans Enterprise app credentials. 

 Your BlueJeans app credentials are the App Key that you specified for
 your app, and the App Secret that was generated by BlueJeans. These
 credentials are shown on your Enterprise Apps page. Copy the App Key and
 App Secret and paste the them into a text file. 

 Do not continue to the next step unless you
 have copied the App Key and App Secret. You must provide this
 information to SSPM during the onboarding process. 

 Connect SSPM to Your BlueJeans Instance 

 By adding a BlueJeans app in SSPM, you enable SSPM to
 connect to your BlueJeans instance. 

 Log in to 
 Strata Cloud Manager . 

 Select Configuration SaaS Security Posture Security Applications Add Application and click the BlueJeans tile. 

 Under posture security instances, Add Instance or,
 if there is already an instance configured, Add New 
 instance. 

 Choose the option to Log in with Credentials . 

 Enter the application credentials (App Key and App Secret) and
 Connect .

 GlobalProtect App for iOS 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 iOS endpoints only 

 GlobalProtect app version 6.1 or later 

 GlobalProtect™ is an application that runs on your endpoint
(desktop computer, laptop, tablet, or smart phone) to protect you
by using the same security policies that protect the sensitive resources
in your corporate network. GlobalProtect™ secures your intranet,
private cloud, public cloud, and internet traffic and allows you
to access your company’s resources from anywhere in the world.

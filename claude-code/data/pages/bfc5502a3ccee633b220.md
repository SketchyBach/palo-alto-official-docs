 Review your unmanaged cloud services in your Attack Surface inventory. 

 Review your unmanaged cloud services in your Attack Surface inventory. Unmanaged cloud services are cloud services that were discovered in an ASM scan and cannot be correlated with cloud assets that were previously onboarded into your inventory. 

 Navigate to Modules → Attack Surface → Asset Inventory → Services . 

 On the Services page, filter the list of services using the filter Partially Onboarded = Yes .

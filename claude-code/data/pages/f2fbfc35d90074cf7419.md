 Onboard a Zoho WorkDrive App to SSPM 

 Connect a Zoho WorkDrive instance to SSPM to detect posture and account
 risks. 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Strata Cloud Manager 

 SaaS Security Posture Management license 

 Or any of the following licenses that include the Data Security license: 

 CASB-X 

 CASB-PA 

 For SSPM to detect posture risks in your Zoho WorkDrive instance, you must onboard
 your Zoho WorkDrive instance to SSPM. Through the onboarding process, SSPM connects
 to a Zoho WorkDrive API and, through the API, scans your Zoho WorkDrive instance for
 misconfigured settings and account risks. SSPM gets access to your Zoho WorkDrive
 instance through OAuth 2.0 authorization. During the onboarding process, SSPM will
 redirect you to log in to Zoho WorkDrive. After you log in, Zoho WorkDrive will
 prompt you to grant SSPM the access it needs to your Zoho WorkDrive instance. 

 The supported Zoho WorkDrive account plans for SSPM scans are the following plans:

 Business Plan 

 Enterprise Plan 

 To onboard your Zoho WorkDrive instance, you complete the following actions: 

 Identify the administrator account that you will use to log in to Zoho
 WorkDrive during onboarding. 

 Required Permissions: To grant SSPM access to the API scopes it
 needs, you must log in as a Team Admin or as the Super Admin. 

 Identify the region that is associated with the administrator account. 

 Zoho has data centers located in specific regions. During onboarding, SSPM
 will prompt you for the region that is associated with the administrator
 account. You can determine the region from the base URL of the account. If
 the base URL is zoho.com with no region information
 included, your region is the United States (US). Otherwise, the base URL
 will include the region code. For example, the base URL
 zoho.eu identifies the European Union (EU) and
 zoho.in identifies India (IN). 

 Sign out of all Zoho WorkDrive accounts. 

 Signing out of all Zoho WorkDrive accounts helps ensure that you sign in
 under the correct account during the onboarding process. Some browsers will
 automatically sign you in by using saved credentials. To ensure that the
 browser does not automatically sign you in to the wrong account, you can
 turn off any automatic sign-in option or clear your saved credentials.
 Alternatively, you can prevent the browser from using saved credentials by
 opening SSPM in an incognito window. 

 Connect SSPM to your Zoho WorkDrive instance. 
 In SSPM, complete the following steps to enable SSPM to connect to your Zoho
 WorkDrive instance. 

 Log in to 
 Strata Cloud Manager . 

 Select Configuration SaaS Security Posture Security Applications Add Application and click the Zoho WorkDrive tile. 

 On the Posture Security tab, Add
 New instance. 

 Log in with Credentials . 

 Select your region and Connect . 

 SSPM redirects you to the Zoho WorkDrive login page. 

 Log in to the Zoho WorkDrive administrator account. 
 Zoho WorkDrive displays a consent form that details the access
 permissions that SSPM requires. 

 Review the consent form and allow the requested permissions.

 Attack surface assets 

 The assets discovered in an attack surface management scan are called attack surface assets or external surface assets. 

 The internet-facing assets that were discovered in an attack surface management (ASM) scan and attributed to your organization are available in the inventory on the Modules → Attack Surface → Asset Inventory pages. These assets are referred to as attack surface assets or external surface assets. For information about Attack Surface assets, including domains, certificates, services, and websites, see External Surface assets .

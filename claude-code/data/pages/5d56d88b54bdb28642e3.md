 Role of Panorama in HSF Cluster Orchestration 

 Panorama serves as the central management and orchestration platform for your
 Hyperscale Security Fabric (HSF) deployment. 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Prisma AIRS 

 Software NGFW Credits 

 HSF subscription license 

 Panorama serves as the central management and orchestration platform for your
 Hyperscale Security Fabric (HSF) deployment, providing comprehensive control over the
 entire firewall cluster through a dedicated plugin. When you deploy HSF in ESXi
 environments, you use the Panorama plugin to connect to multiple vCenter instances and
 orchestrate the deployment of both P-Nodes and S-Nodes across your infrastructure. The
 plugin enables you to select compute resources, map interfaces to port groups, and
 configure auto-scaling parameters for your cluster components. 

 Through Panorama, you can add, delete, or manage your HSF cluster as a single
 logical entity while maintaining visibility into individual cluster members. The
 platform automatically handles the distribution of configuration updates to all cluster
 members and provides centralized monitoring capabilities that display real-time metrics
 for CPU usage, memory consumption, session capacity, and throughput across your
 deployment. You can configure auto-scaling policies that allow Panorama to dynamically
 provision additional Prisma AIRS instances when utilization thresholds are exceeded,
 ensuring your cluster automatically responds to traffic demands without manual
 intervention. 

 Panorama also coordinates rolling upgrades across your HSF cluster, maintaining
 service continuity while sequentially updating individual firewall instances.

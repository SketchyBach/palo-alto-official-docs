 Troubleshoot Active Response 

 To fix a playbook error for an alert, restart the playbook. 

 Caution 

 When you restart the playbook, any data collected by the previous playbook execution may be lost. 

 On the Incident Response → Incidents page, select the incident that has an alert with a playbook error. 

 In the details pane, select the Overview tab . 

 Scroll to the alert with the alert with the playbook error, and click Restart Playbook . 

 If restarting the playbook does not fix the error, reach out to a member of the Customer Success Team.

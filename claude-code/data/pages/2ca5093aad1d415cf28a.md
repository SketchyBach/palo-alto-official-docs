 Cortex Xpanse Browser Support Policy 

 Cortex Xpanse products support the latest versions of the following browsers: 

 Google Chrome 

 Mozilla Firefox 

 Microsoft Edge 

 We understand that many of our customers have stringent browser support policies. In the future, we plan to support more browsers. Until then if you experience any issues that are browser related, contact Customer Support and we will do our best to resolve them.

 PA-7500 Series Firewall Power Cord Types 

 Learn about the different power cords supported by the PA-7500 Series
 firewall. 

 The PA-7500 Series firewalls ship with four AC or four DC power supplies by default. You
 can order up to six additional power supplies (ten total) and power cords are included
 with each power supply. 

 The table below lists all the power cords supported on the PA-7500 Series firewall.

 PA-3200 Series Electrical Specifications 

 Learn about the PA-3200 Series firewall electrical specifications. 

 The following table describes PA-3200 Series firewall
electrical specifications. The electrical specifications are the
same for all models in the series (PA-3220, PA-3250, and PA-3260
firewalls). 

 Specification 

 Value 

 Power Supplies 

 Two 650W AC or DC power supplies; the second power supply
is for redundancy. 

 Input voltage 

 AC power supplies —100-240VAC (50-60Hz) 

 DC power supplies — -48 to -60VDC 

 Power consumption 

 Maximum 

 AC power supplies—240W 

 DC power supplies—228W 

 Average 

 AC and DC power supplies—195W 

 Maximum current consumption 

 AC power supplies —2.3A@100VAC, 1.0A@240VAC 

 DC power supplies —4.7A@-48VDC, 3.8A@-60VDC 

 Maximum inrush current 

 The following value applies to both AC and DC power
supplies. 

 Maximum inrush current per power supply —25A

 Connect Cables to the PA-5500 Series Firewall 

 Learn how to connect cables to the PA-5500 Series Firewall. 

 After you Connect Power to the PA-5500 Series Firewall , connect your management computer to the management
 port on the firewall so you can begin the initial configuration. You can optionally
 connect your management computer to the console port, which provides a serial connection
 to the firewall and enables you to view the bootup messages and manage the firewall
 using the command line interface (CLI).

 Monitor Incidents 

 Lets see on how to Monitor, acknowledge and synchronize the incidents in Prisma SD-WAN . You can acknowledge the incidents and synchronize the
 incidents between the controller and the device. 

 Where Can I Use
 This? What Do I Need? 

 Prisma SD-WAN (Managed by Strata Cloud Manager ) 

 Prisma SD-WAN 

 When incidents are generated on the Prisma SD-WAN due to some failure,
 you can acknowledge the incidents and synchronize the incidents between the
 controller and the device. You can create an event policy to manage the event code
 suppression based on the specified classifications and action attributes
 configured. 

 Acknowledge generated incidents in the system on the Strata Cloud Manager.
 Acknowledge indicates that you are aware of the incident but may not take any action
 on the incident at this point in time. You acknowledge only unresolved incidents.
 Acknowledging an incident enables you to display and focus on incidents that require
 your attention. 

 Select Incidents Prisma SD-WAN Incidents . 

 The Incidents are displayed. 

 Select one or more incident to
 Acknowledge it. 

 By default the acknowledged incidents disappear from the page. 

 Synchronize Incidents 

 You can synchronize incidents consistently between the controller and the device.
 During the incidents synchronization, the device sends standing incident
 snapshots to the controller every 24 hours and the controller clears the
 incidents that no longer exist in the device and raises the device's missing
 incidents. 

 Go to Configuration Prisma SD-WAN ION Devices . 

 The list of claimed devices is displayed. 

 From the ellipsis menu next to a device, select
 Sync Incident to synchronize the incidents
 between the controller and the device.

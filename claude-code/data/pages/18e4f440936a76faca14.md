 Software NGFW Credit Distribution and Management 

 Interchange and allocate credits among your Managed AIRS for AWS resources
 regardless of the cloud deployment method. 

 Where Can I Use This? What Do I Need? 

 Managed AIRS for AWS 

 Managed AIRS for AWS subscription 

 Palo Alto Networks Customer Support Portal account 

 AWS Marketplace account 

 User role (either tenant or administrator) 

 Credit Admin or Super User role in Customer Support
 Portal 

 Software NGFW Credits fund your consumption of Managed AIRS for AWS resources and
 their related usage of Cloud-Delivered Security Services (CDSS) and centralized
 management (Strata Cloud Manager, and Strata Logging Service). Software NGFW credits
 allow you to consume Managed AIRS for AWS resources in your tenant at a lower cost
 than PAYG rates, up to a specific capacity until your contract expires. 

 You may currently be using legacy Cloud NGFW credits for
 your AWS tenants. Work with your Palo Alto Networks sales team to migrate to the
 more flexible Software NGFW Credits for better flexibility. 

 Credit usage for your Managed AIRS for AWS deployment is based on the Managed AIRS
 for AWS subscription and billing model.

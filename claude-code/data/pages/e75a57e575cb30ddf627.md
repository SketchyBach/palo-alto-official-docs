 Rule Details 

 Cortex AppSec Rule ID 

 APPSEC_LIC_4 

 Category 

 Licenses 

 Severity 

 HIGH 

 Framework 

 SCA 

 Impact 

 This policy identifies software packages distributed under strong copyleft licenses, such as the GNU General Public License (GPL). Strong copyleft licenses require derivative works to be distributed under the same copyleft license terms as the original work. Please contact the legal team for further investigation.
